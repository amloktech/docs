Your account manager coordinates commercial changes and the final cancellation of your AMLOK agreement. These are managed account processes, not customer API operations.

Contact your account manager for changes to the monthly minimum commitment, products, complexity tiers, volume discounts, renewal, cancellation notice, and the effective end date. If you do not have their contact details, email [support@amlok.tech](mailto:support@amlok.tech?subject=AMLOK%20account%20management) and include your company and workspace reference.

## Final cancellation and refund

1. Send the request to your account manager. AMLOK confirms the notice requirements and effective cancellation date under your agreement.
2. AMLOK stops new usage and automatic top-ups at the effective end date and reconciles final usage and pending funding.
3. AMLOK confirms the remaining paid balance and refund payment instructions before closing the account.
4. **Immediately after account closure, AMLOK issues the full remaining paid-balance refund.** Funds may take **7-10 business days** to reach your bank account, depending on your bank.

You do not submit a refund, payout, approval, transfer, or closure command through the API. Your account manager coordinates the refund and provides its reference and delivery updates. A failed or returned payment does not cancel the refund obligation.

Your credit view is read-only. Payment-method changes, additional credits, and agreed top-up changes go through your account manager. The wallet cannot be withdrawn while the agreement remains active. See [agreement terms](/pricing/contract-terms) and [refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts).

## Workspace retention and deletion

Authorized users manage retention through workspace settings in the AMLOK platform, in accordance with applicable compliance requirements. **When workspace deletion is completed, all data associated with that workspace is deleted.** The API does not provide a separate deletion-request endpoint.

Account cancellation and workspace deletion are distinct actions. Coordinate the necessary final statements and exports with your account manager before completing workspace deletion. See [privacy and data handling](/security/privacy).
