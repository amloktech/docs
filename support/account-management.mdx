Use Console to manage authorized workspace access, API keys, integrations, webhook destinations and the payment settings available to your environment. Enterprise clients can also work with their dedicated account manager on agreements, rates, capacity and renewal.

For account assistance, [create a support request](/support/create-ticket) or email [support@amlok.io](mailto:support@amlok.io?subject=AMLOK%20account%20management) with your company, workspace reference and environment. Do not include passwords, API keys, bank credentials or full payment details.

## Access and settings

Workspace Owners manage membership and billing. Developers can create API keys within their permissions; each key belongs to one workspace and environment. Use the Console controls to rotate or revoke keys and configure authorized integration return URLs and webhook destinations. See [API keys](/console/api-keys), [authentication](/api-reference/authentication) and [integration configuration](/api-reference/configuration).

## Payments and top-ups

Sandbox **Billing** supports simulated card and ACH payment methods and automatic-payment settings. The minimum trigger is **CAD 200.00** and the minimum recharge is **CAD 500.00**. It does not collect or refund real funds. Live funding requires an activated payment facility; support confirms the funding options and secure payment instructions for your account.

CAD 200.00 is a top-up trigger, not a balance floor. Processing can continue below it when available credits cover the operation. Pending funding is not spendable. Saving an agreement, a payment method or an autopay amount is not proof of settlement. See [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing).

## Cancellation, corrections and refunds

An authorized Owner can request cancellation at any time without notice through support or the account manager. There is no account-cancellation or refund action in Console and no public API mutation for these actions.

Support confirms the processing cutoff, in-flight funding, final reconciliation, month-end closure and refund instructions under the [commercial terms](/pricing/contract-terms). Keep the closure confirmation and actual payment reference. Use [cancellation and refunds](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts) to track each step. A provisional statement or Sandbox refund is not proof of a real refund.

For a charge correction, include the usage, transaction or payment ID and the disputed amount. Support reconciles the original entry and any linked adjustment; historical charges are not silently rewritten. Disabling autopay does not reverse submitted payments, and stopping one monitor does not close the account.

## Retention and deletion

Request retention requirements or workspace deletion through support; privacy requests can also be sent to [privacy@amlok.io](mailto:privacy@amlok.io). These are coordinated processes rather than customer-configurable Console retention settings or public API commands.

Export needed data before access ends. Support confirms the affected data, any financial-record retention or legal hold, backup handling and deletion completion. Disconnecting an integration does not itself delete previously collected data or the original records held by that provider. See [privacy and data handling](/security/privacy).
