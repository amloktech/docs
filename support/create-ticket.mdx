---
title: "Create a support ticket"
description: "Contact AMLOK support with the information needed to investigate your request."
---

Email **[support@amlok.tech](mailto:support@amlok.tech?subject=AMLOK%20support%20request)** from your business email address. This link opens your email application; your request is sent only when you send the message.

Use a subject such as **[P2] Insights - underwriting job not completing**. Choose a [severity](/support/severity-and-escalation) based on business impact. For a production outage with no workaround, start the subject with **[P1] Production incident** and include an available incident contact.

## Include these details

| Detail | What to send |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Company name and workspace identifier. |
| Product and environment | Insights, Signal, an integration, reporting, or billing; production or sandbox. |
| Business impact | Affected workflow, approximate scope, urgency, and any workaround. |
| Time | When the issue started, the last successful attempt, and timezone or UTC timestamps. |
| Expected and actual result | A short description and steps to reproduce. |
| Technical references | Request ID, job or connection ID, HTTP status, and error code where applicable. |
| Evidence | Redacted screenshots or logs containing only the information needed to investigate. |
| Contact | A person who can answer follow-up questions, with availability and timezone. |

```text
Company / workspace:
Product / environment:
Severity and business impact:
Started at (including timezone):
Last successful attempt:
Expected result:
Actual result:
Steps to reproduce:
Request / job / connection reference:
HTTP status / error code:
Workaround attempted:
Contact and availability:
```

## After you submit

Keep replies in the same email thread and include any support reference provided. AMLOK reviews the impact, confirms priority, requests missing diagnostics where necessary, and provides the next step. An automated acknowledgment is not the initial human response measured by the SLA.

If impact increases, reply with **Escalation requested**, the new impact, and your support reference. Follow [severity and escalation](/support/severity-and-escalation). Avoid opening duplicate tickets for the same incident.

## Billing and account requests

For a usage discrepancy, include the statement period and usage or ledger references. For a payment missing from your credit report, include the existing payment or statement reference. Support and your account manager will reconcile it; the credit view has no payment controls.

Send agreement, renewal, or cancellation requests to your account manager. If you need help reaching them, email support and request routing to account management. A support message does not itself close the account or change your agreement.
