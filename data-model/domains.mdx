Source-normalized records are grouped by evidence domain. The counts below describe the [source dictionary](/data-model/field-dictionary), not the number of endpoints or populated fields on one borrower. Shared `common` metadata is counted once. Detailed verification fields and risk metrics are separate definitions linked from the [model index](/data-model/start-here).

| Domain | Normalized source resources | Dictionary fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | 4 | 65 |
| Identity | 7 | 123 |
| Banking | 5 | 97 |
| Payments | 6 | 86 |
| Commerce | 4 | 68 |
| Accounting | 10 | 133 |
| Inventory | 8 | 109 |
| Lending | 9 | 155 |
| Liens | 3 | 47 |
| Risk | 4 | 51 |
| Operations | 3 | 40 |

`verification_assessment` adds one typed aggregate with 25 observation sections under `normalized.assessment`. Financial results use the 24 [risk dimensions](/api-reference/underwriting-dimensions). Source domains, assessment dimensions and research feature families serve different purposes and do not share a one-to-one count.
