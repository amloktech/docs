AMLOK classifies transaction purpose separately from account context, cash effect, payment rail and counterparty role. Use the leaf category to understand the activity and the group to summarize related purposes. The current taxonomy contains **327 leaf categories within 45 groups**; groups are not additional leaf categories.

These meanings do not establish tax deductibility, accounting recognition, verified identity, fraud or creditworthiness. A card purchase can share a purpose category with a bank payment while having a different cash and liability effect. [Read the classification rules](/data-model/transaction-categorization) and [all classification-axis values](/data-model/assessment-definitions#transaction-classification-axes).

Use the exact `category_id`, not a translated label. The [taxonomy download](/downloads/transaction-taxonomy.json) supplies allowed account contexts, directions, parent IDs and evidence requirements. [Plain-language definitions](/downloads/financial-definitions.json) are also available as JSON.

## Operating receipts

`revenue` - Cash receipts from the business's operating activity, distinguished from funding and asset sales.

This parent group summarizes its child groups below.

## Sales and service receipts

`revenue.sales` - Receipts from goods, services and related customer activity. A receipt does not by itself establish when revenue is recognized.

| Category key | Name and meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `revenue.sales.unspecified` | **Unspecified operating sales receipts**. Supported operating sales receipts whose specific goods or service type is unresolved. |
| `revenue.sales.goods` | **Goods sale receipts**. Money received for goods sold by the business. |
| `revenue.sales.services` | **Service delivery receipts**. Money received for services delivered by the business. |
| `revenue.sales.subscription` | **Subscription receipts**. Customer receipts under a subscription arrangement. |
| `revenue.sales.usage` | **Usage-based service receipts**. Customer receipts for services billed according to usage. |
| `revenue.sales.licensing` | **Licensing receipts**. Receipts for granting permission to use supported licensed rights or products. |
| `revenue.sales.royalty` | **Royalty receipts**. Receipts earned through a supported royalty arrangement. |
| `revenue.sales.commission` | **Commission receipts**. Receipts earned for arranging or facilitating a sale or service. |
| `revenue.sales.franchise` | **Franchise fee receipts**. Fees received from franchisees under a franchise arrangement. |
| `revenue.sales.rental` | **Operating rental receipts**. Receipts from renting property or assets as an operating activity. |
| `revenue.sales.maintenance` | **Maintenance contract receipts**. Customer receipts under a maintenance or service-support contract. |
| `revenue.sales.project` | **Project and milestone receipts**. Customer receipts tied to a project or completion milestone. |
| `revenue.sales.invoice_collection` | **Customer invoice collections**. Cash received against a customer invoice; collection is distinct from issuing or recognizing the invoice. |
| `revenue.sales.customer_deposit` | **Customer deposits and retainers**. Customer funds received in advance or as a retainer. They are not automatically earned revenue. |
| `revenue.sales.gift_card_redemption` | **Supported redeemed stored-value receipts**. Receipts supported as relating to redeemed stored value, rather than an unsupported assumption from a gift-card balance. |
| `revenue.sales.reimbursement` | **Customer expense reimbursements**. Customer money received to reimburse supported expenses incurred on the customer's behalf. |
