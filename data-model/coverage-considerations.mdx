AMLOK normalizes information from connected sources and institution-supplied evidence. The fields returned for a request depend on your enabled integrations, product modules, permissions, market, and available history.

## Check integration and resource coverage

Use the [integration status catalog](/integrations/status) for availability and markets. Review [provider coverage](/data-model/provider-coverage), [resource coverage](/data-model/resource-coverage), and [field mappings](/data-model/field-mappings) for the corresponding data references.

A required field defines a valid resource when that resource exists. It does not guarantee that every connected account supplies every resource or optional field. Inspect `data_status`, `missing_fields`, and the returned coverage period before using a result.

## Match evidence to the workflow

| Evidence | Interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| Bank transactions | Observed deposits and withdrawals; deposits alone do not establish verified gross income or employment. |
| Accounting records | Coverage follows enabled ledger, reporting, tax, and other modules. Invoice access alone does not imply complete financial statements. |
| Balances | A current balance is one observation. Historical metrics require the appropriate observations or an explicitly defined reconstruction. |
| Inventory and held assets | Establish beneficial ownership before treating trust, consigned, or third-party assets as collateral or liquidity. |
| Registry and lien records | Apply the jurisdiction and search scope. A UCC or PPSA search is not a complete real-property title search or valuation. |
| Derived attributes | Use the required evidence, calculation version, and coverage window. Missing prerequisites produce an unavailable result. |

## Permissions and additional sources

Collection, processing, retention, and onward use must follow source permissions and your agreement. Consumer-reporting, credit-bureau, payroll, tax-authority, and property-title workflows require the appropriate sources and permitted purposes.

Read and assessment access does not authorize money movement, filings, communication dispatch, or changes to credit terms. Use an explicitly authorized workflow for consequential actions.

## Preserve data quality

Keep missing, partial, stale, and conflicting evidence visible in your application. A failed lookup is not a verified no-hit result. Use [normalization rules](/data-model/normalization-rules) and [response conventions](/api-reference/response-format) to interpret the returned data consistently.
