## How to interpret the definitions

### Source / derived / managed

**Three distinct origins**

Normalized source records preserve provider evidence. Derived attributes require calculations. AMLOK-managed records describe workflows and governance.

### Availability

**Conditional per source and entitlement**

An active integration does not make every field universally available. Optional data depends on source, country, module, permissions and history.

### Required fields

**Contract requirement when a resource exists**

Required does not mean the upstream provider guarantees this field. Reject or quarantine records that cannot meet identity requirements.

### Missing values

**Null is not zero**

Use missing\_fields reasons; \[\] means a known empty collection. Partial results remain partial.

### Resource prefixes

**Documentation context**

bank\_transaction.amount.amount refers to data.amount.amount on a bank\_transaction response. Common fields are inherited at the same object level.

### Excluded assumptions

**No invented credit-bureau, payroll, tax-authority or title feeds**

These require additional sources or explicit lender-supplied evidence. See [coverage considerations](/data-model/coverage-considerations).

### Change control

**Version definitions and mappings together**

Changes to meanings, types, requiredness or calculations require review and versioned client communication.
