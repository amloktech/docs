AMLOK's **150 cross-system attributes** describe the supplied invoices, bills, orders, payments, inventory and other authorized records. This dictionary explains each key without requiring you to read its calculation specification.

Every entry describes **observed records**, not a claim that all accounts or business systems are known. Read the result's scope, dates, currency, status and input manifest. Missing records do not establish zero. Receivables and payables aging follows due dates; timed observations follow the cohort specified in the definition.

Amounts use the returned currency. Ratios are fractions; concentration indices run from zero to one. Higher concentration means greater dependence on fewer parties, not an automatic adverse decision. [Retrieve attributes](/data-model/financial-attributes), [read result fields](/data-model/assessment-definitions), or [download the full attribute dictionary](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/amloktech/docs/main/downloads/financial-attribute-dictionary.csv).

## Commerce

Observed order activity, discounts, fulfillment and concentration.

### cross_system.commerce.cancelled_order_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Portion of observed orders placed in the window that are cancelled.

### cross_system.commerce.discounted_order_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Portion of observed noncancelled orders placed in the window with a positive discount.

### cross_system.commerce.discount_to_subtotal_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Discount value relative to source-reported subtotals on observed noncancelled orders. The source subtotal convention matters; this is not a profit margin.

### cross_system.commerce.paid_unfulfilled_order_count

Unit: `count`.

Number of observed noncancelled orders that are paid but remain wholly or partially unfulfilled.

### cross_system.commerce.paid_unfulfilled_order_value

Unit: `currency`.

Full current value of paid orders that remain wholly or partially unfulfilled. Partial fulfillment does not mean this entire value is undelivered.

### cross_system.commerce.oldest_paid_unfulfilled_order_days

Unit: `days`.

Age of the oldest paid, wholly or partially unfulfilled order among observed orders placed in the reporting window.

### cross_system.commerce.channel_concentration_hhi

Unit: `ratio`.

Concentration of observed noncancelled order value across sales channels. Higher zero-to-one values indicate greater dependence on fewer channels.

### cross_system.commerce.customer_concentration_hhi

Unit: `ratio`.

Concentration of observed noncancelled order value across identified customers. Higher zero-to-one values indicate greater dependence on fewer customers.

### cross_system.commerce.location_concentration_hhi

Unit: `ratio`.

Concentration of observed noncancelled order value across identified locations. Higher zero-to-one values indicate greater dependence on fewer locations.

## Payments and disputes

### cross_system.payments.effective_processing_fee_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Explicitly reported processing fees relative to captured payment value. Missing fees are not estimated from a nominal fee schedule.

### cross_system.payments.refunded_payment_count_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Portion of payments captured in the window with a positive refunded amount. This follows the capture cohort rather than the date of refund events.

### cross_system.payments.capture_to_settlement_days.median

Unit: `days`.

Middle elapsed time from payment capture to settlement among observed settled payments captured in the window.

### cross_system.payments.capture_to_settlement_days.p90

Unit: `days`.

90th-percentile elapsed time from payment capture to settlement among observed settled payments captured in the window.

### cross_system.payments.dispute.needs_response.count

Unit: `count`.

Number of observed disputes opened in the window and currently requiring a response.

### cross_system.payments.dispute.under_review.count

Unit: `count`.

Number of observed disputes opened in the window and currently under review.

### cross_system.payments.dispute.won.count

Unit: `count`.

Number of observed disputes opened in the window and currently explicitly recorded as won.

### cross_system.payments.dispute.lost.count

Unit: `count`.

Number of observed disputes opened in the window and currently explicitly recorded as lost.

### cross_system.payments.dispute.closed.count

Unit: `count`.

Number of observed disputes opened in the window and currently closed without inferring whether they were won or lost.

### cross_system.payments.resolved_dispute_win_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Portion of explicitly won or lost disputes recorded as won. Unresolved and generically closed disputes do not count as wins.

### cross_system.payments.open_dispute_exposure

Unit: `currency`.

Value disputed in cases requiring a response or under review. This is unresolved exposure, not expected loss.

### cross_system.payments.overdue_dispute_response_count

Unit: `count`.

Number of observed disputes still requiring a response after their response deadlines.

## Accounting

Explicit journal origins, reversal links and source-document links.

### cross_system.accounting.reversal_journal_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Portion of observed posted or reversed journals with an explicit link to the journal they reverse. Missing links do not prove no reversals occurred.

### cross_system.accounting.source_document_link_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Portion of observed posted or reversed journals linked to a source document. An absent link alone does not establish fraud or manual origin.

### cross_system.accounting.manual_origin_journal_share

Unit: `ratio`.

Portion of observed posted or reversed journals explicitly identified as manual entries. Narrative text is not used to guess manual origin.
