Sources were reviewed for the November 1, 2026 research specification. A citation identifies related work or a source's own claim. It does not establish access to proprietary data, permission to train on a source, independent replication, or an AMLOK result. Record an exact revision when a source or model becomes an experimental dependency.

The initial research consulted public source pages and selected AMLOK starter documents. It did not acquire competitor datasets, commercial model weights, private borrower histories, or a Canadian loan-outcome corpus. Public-data availability and source quality are evaluated separately from permission to collect, redistribute, or train.


## Original records and acquisition sources

The [public-source inventory](/research/canadian-data) contains the full source-by-source access and field register. The executed source analysis uses original [EDC disclosures](https://www.edc.ca/en/about-us/corporate/disclosure/reporting-transactions/individual-transaction-information.html), [Investissement Québec intervention records](https://www.investquebec.com/fr/tout-sur-nous/diffusion-de-linformation/archives), [CSBFP data](https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/3f718801-099d-4037-bb0a-1d41ba8aca8b), [Kinross financing disclosure](https://www.kinross.com/news-and-investors/news-releases/press-release-details/2022/Kinross-announces-US1.0-billion-term-loan/default.aspx), [Lundin Mining's amendment](https://www.lundinmining.com/news/lundin-mining-increases-existing-credit-facility-to-us45-billion), and [BCE's financial report](https://www.bce.ca/cs-assets/2025-bce-annual-financial-report.pdf-blt5d552f866d8a58a3).

The [SBA data portal](https://data.sba.gov/dataset/7a-504-foia) supplies the inspected US loan schema. The original [FinQA repository](https://github.com/czyssrs/FinQA) supplies the executed retrieval task; [TAT-QA](https://github.com/NExTplusplus/TAT-QA) supplies development examples. [FinanceBench's publisher page](https://www.patronus.ai/research/financebench) identifies the open sample and its use conditions. [CUAD](https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad) is a candidate contract-clause benchmark, not an executed result here.

The [acquisition manifest](/downloads/research/public-records/acquisition-manifest.json) records the exact file URLs and hashes. A citation alone is not evidence that the entire archive was collected, labels were adjudicated or a model was trained.
