Public Canadian data can strengthen identity checks, contextual analysis, and selected event labels. It generally cannot reconstruct a business's private bank ledger, processor sales, repayment history, or every closed lending deal. The public-data program therefore measures what each source actually establishes.

## Source acquisition register

| Source | Candidate use | Required limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Corporations Canada data services](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/corporations-canada/en/data-services) | Federal legal identity, status, and permitted registry attributes | Federal scope is not all Canadian businesses. Preserve jurisdiction and effective dates. The authority documents an API; “no Canadian registries have APIs” is inaccurate. |
| Provincial and territorial registries, including Québec REQ | Jurisdiction-specific identity corroboration | Verify current terms, fields, access rights, and historical availability individually. Do not assume uniform coverage or bulk-training rights. |
| PPSA/PPR and Québec RDPRM | Registered security-interest evidence | Registration is not necessarily a funded loan, current balance, default, fraud, or a unique deal. Amendments and discharges change interpretation. |
| [OSB insolvency statistics](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/office-superintendent-bankruptcy/en/statistics-and-research) | Population context and changes in reported insolvencies | Aggregate rates do not label individual borrowers. Reporting lag must enter historical analysis. |
| Authorized insolvency/court case records | Carefully adjudicated events for matched entities | Publication and selection bias; filings, allegations, judgments, appeals, and final dispositions differ. |
| [Statistics Canada WDS](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/developers/wds/user-guide) | Industry, employment, price, and regional context | Use the original release vintage where available. Revised present-day series can leak future information. Check the applicable [open licence](https://www.statcan.gc.ca/en/terms-conditions/open-licence-faq). |
| [Bank of Canada Valet](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/valet/docs) | Rates and foreign-exchange context | State units, dates, missing days, and transformations; observe [source terms](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/terms/). |
| SEDAR+ issuer filings | Financial-document extraction and public-company case studies | Public issuers do not represent typical private SMEs or consumers. Document-specific rights and accounting periods matter. |
| CanLII | Potential legal research reference | Terms could not be retrieved in this review. No bulk acquisition, redistribution, or model-training entitlement is asserted. |

This register is a source plan, not a claim that these sources were acquired or included in model training. Only the public FX analysis below was downloaded and calculated in this release.

## Executed public-data example

We retrieved the Bank of Canada's `FXUSDCAD` observations for January 1 to December 31, 2025. The returned series contains **249 observations**. Values are Canadian dollars per US dollar. The minimum is **1.3558**, the maximum **1.4603**, and the arithmetic mean of returned observations **1.39776225**. These are descriptive calculations over published observations, not a forecast, trading result, annual accounting conversion rule, or borrower-risk finding.

The mean is not weighted by transaction value. Non-reporting days are not zero rates. Actual financial conversion requires the relevant transaction date, reporting policy, and documented handling of unavailable observations. See the [acquisition URL](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/valet/observations/FXUSDCAD/json?start_date=2025-01-01&end_date=2025-12-31) and [machine-readable summary](/downloads/research/public-data-summary.json).

## Public-record validation design

1. Define the unit: registry row, distinct legal entity, registered interest, or independently documented lending outcome.
2. Deduplicate using source identifiers, amendments, parties, dates, and jurisdiction. Publish uncertainty in entity merges.
3. Establish source rights and access conditions before acquisition.
4. Construct the historical view using only information available at the cutoff.
5. Evaluate entity matching and event extraction against adjudicated source evidence.
6. Evaluate repayment or default prediction only where an independent authorized outcome record exists.

A security registration can corroborate a creditor relationship. It does not reveal the full repayment schedule or the descriptor that will appear in a private bank account. Public records alone cannot substantiate claims that raw bank data was rerun for every closed Canadian deal.

## Canadian adaptation hypotheses

Test French and English descriptors, accented and truncated names, numbered corporations across jurisdictions, Interac ambiguity, processor settlements, provincial tax conventions, CAD/USD activity, seasonal businesses, and incomplete histories. These are evaluation dimensions, not assertions that a model has solved them. Rail recognition must not be represented as recovery of hidden sender identity.
