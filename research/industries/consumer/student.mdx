Separate government student loans, private education loans and student lines of credit. Study period, grace period, repayment entry and repayment assistance are different contractual states.

## Financial-risk measures

| Measure | Definition and comparison rule |
| --- | --- |
| Repayment-entry performance | Arrears or default within a specified period after repayment begins, not after the original disbursement. |
| Payment burden | Contractually payable installments divided by supported current income; preserve approved assistance or payment deferrals. |
| Income transition | Stability of observed income after leaving study, with a defined observation window. |
| Default and recovery | Separate default event, principal exposure, subsequent recovery and cure. |
| Assistance participation | Report the program state separately from delinquency; an authorized reduction is not a missed contractual payment. |

## Public benchmark sources

The [2023-2024 Canada Student Financial Assistance annual report](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/esdc-edsc/documents/programs/canada-student-loans-grants/reports/csfa-annual-2023-2024/5447-2023-24-CSFA-Program-Annual-Report-EN.pdf) reports an **8% three-year default rate for the 2021-2022 repayment-entry cohort**. This measure is loan-value weighted and uses default after more than 270 days of arrears. It is not the percentage of all student borrowers currently delinquent.

Use the [program's statistical review](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/canada-student-loans-grants/reports/student-financial-assistance-statistics-2023-2024.html) to retain the federal program's coverage. Do not transfer its default rate to private education lending or jurisdictions outside its participating scope.

## AMLOK evaluation

Test contract-state recognition, supported income, payment obligations and assistance treatment before evaluating risk. Hold out later repayment-entry cohorts and report the amount of mature follow-up. A borrower with less than three years of observed repayment cannot be silently labeled a three-year non-default.

For Signal, measure sustained income or payment changes and false-alert rates across repayment states. Evaluate missing employment or income data as missing evidence rather than inventing future earnings. Use the [consumer underwriting protocol](/research/consumer-underwriting) for fairness and consent boundaries.
