Use this collection to select financial-risk benchmarks and evaluate AMLOK for a specific industry. Consumer Lending and Business Lending define product-level cohorts. Financial Institutions and Fintech describe institution and distribution contexts. Insurtech covers trade credit, surety and premium finance. A loan can belong to several contexts; count it once in a consolidated portfolio.

Each page separates published market evidence from AMLOK evaluation methods. A market delinquency rate is not a model accuracy score. The [measured results](/research/public-results) page contains completed AMLOK research measurements; these industry pages do not add unexecuted model results.

## Benchmark contract

Record these fields with every numerical comparison:

| Field | Required information |
| --- | --- |
| Population | Geography, product, borrower type, origination vintage, selection criteria and exclusions. |
| Observation unit | Borrower, account, facility, invoice, policy, bond or exposure amount. |
| Outcome | Exact event, past-due threshold, measurement horizon and cure treatment. |
| Denominator | Eligible accounts, borrowers, principal, exposure, insured sales or earned premium. |
| Time | Decision cutoff, observation window, outcome maturity and publication date. |
| Evidence | Source URL, table or field identifier, extraction date, transformation and known gaps. |
| Uncertainty | Sample size, confidence interval, missingness and censoring. |

Do not compare a borrower-level 60-day arrears rate with an account-level 90-day rate. Do not infer a loan's default probability from an approval rate, rental vacancy rate or aggregate banking return.

## AMLOK evaluation design

For Insights, freeze the evidence available at the underwriting cutoff. For Signal, evaluate new information against the previously available baseline and measure the timing and usefulness of a change signal. A later repayment outcome must not enter the input snapshot.

Compare the same eligible cohort, evidence and decision date across a tabular baseline, the AMLOK system under evaluation and any eligible frontier-model comparator. Keep borrowers and related entities out of both training and evaluation sets. Report classification, calibration, evidence quality, abstention, latency and cost separately. See the [evaluation protocol](/research/evaluation), [frontier comparison](/research/frontier-benchmark) and [document benchmarks](/research/document-benchmarks).

Institutions define lending and intervention policies. These research pages introduce no servicing workflow, automatic credit decision, new paid product or public API endpoint.
