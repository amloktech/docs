AMLOK studies how financial language, transaction histories and entity relationships can support business and consumer underwriting and continuous risk monitoring. The research combines institution evidence, borrower-permissioned systems and public context while preserving what each source actually establishes.

## Abstract

Financial activity appears in several systems. An order becomes an invoice, payment, processor settlement, bank deposit and accounting entry. Treating these records as independent revenue overstates activity; looking only at bank deposits misses obligations, reversals and changes before settlement. AMLOK's research design separates document and transaction interpretation, economic-event reconciliation, temporal entity resolution, deterministic financial calculation and outcome prediction.

This release builds the public-record foundation from original Canadian financing disclosures, corporate reports and registry documentation. It identifies extractable fields, verifies concrete financing examples, records source limitations and executes an evidence-retrieval benchmark on real financial reports. The benchmark supplies a reproducible baseline for the specialist and frontier-model comparison program. The model architecture and training curriculum specify the experiments needed to test the larger system.

## Research questions

| ID | Question | Decisive comparison |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | Does multi-source permissioned evidence improve financial understanding over banking alone? | Same cohort and model, adding source families in controlled stages. |
| H2 | Does financial-language adaptation improve bilingual interpretation? | Unseen entities, unseen formats and time-held-out Canadian records. |
| H3 | Does a temporal graph improve identity and reconciliation? | Graph ablation at the same false-match tolerance and evidence cutoff. |
| H4 | Does event pretraining transfer to risk tasks? | Pretrained versus from-scratch models with equal data and compute accounting. |
| H5 | Does change monitoring identify useful intervention signals earlier? | Paired lead time at matched sensitivity, with false-alert and review burden. |
| H6 | Can explanations remain faithful with incomplete evidence? | Evidence support, numerical correctness, abstention and adversarial document tests. |

## How to read the evidence

An **observed source finding** comes from an inspected original record. A **measured result** comes from executed code with saved inputs, predictions and scoring. A **published finding** belongs to its cited author and original population. A **research protocol** specifies an experiment without implying it has run. These distinctions apply to every table and performance statement.

The inventory counts documents, disclosure rows, source events and distinct facilities separately. It does not equate a large aggregate loan count with possession of that many individual training records. See the [deal-corpus rules](/research/deal-corpus).

## Product boundary

[Insights](/pricing/insights) performs full underwriting. [Signal](/pricing/signal) monitors changes and includes reassessment. Institutions retain lending decisions, borrower communication and intervention. Research introduces no new paid product, standalone stage charge or public API schema. Business and consumer outcomes require separate validation populations.
