## Intended use

Use the package to review the research design, reproduce the synthetic experiment, evaluate data requirements, and plan controlled experiments. The methods connect to Insights and Signal without changing their API or commercial boundaries.

## Unsupported uses

Do not use the synthetic probabilities for lending decisions, affordability determinations, capital calculations, IFRS 9 provisions, fraud accusations, regulatory reporting, or customer targeting. Do not present the synthetic test set as Canadian market validation. No causal loss reduction, approval expansion, production latency, fairness certification, or first-in-country claim is supported.

## Known limitations

The experiment's target is generated from an authored equation. Its 12 monthly observations per source omit transaction-level descriptors, payment timing, real identity ambiguity, integration failures, borrower selection, human interventions, and economic-regime change. Synthetic institution labels do not simulate real institution distributions. The graph and text-model hypotheses remain untested.

A learned representation can absorb population and source biases. Public records may overrepresent distress and formal legal entities. More connected systems can increase information and error simultaneously. Provider availability and permitted histories vary; an integration catalog entry is not evidence of complete financial coverage.

## Evidence required for the next model card

Record the actual base model and revision, weight digest, architecture, parameter count, training corpus and purposes, source licences, token/event counts, training dates and hardware, full configuration, split manifests, independent labels, predictions, calibration, costs, failures, subgroup results, and reviewer findings. Publish changes and limitations alongside improvements.

Model cards for business outcome prediction, consumer outcome prediction, entity resolution, narrative generation, and monitoring should be separate. One successful component does not validate the complete system.
