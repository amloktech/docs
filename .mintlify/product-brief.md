# AMLOK documentation product brief

## Audience and jobs

Financial-institution developers, risk teams, model validators, procurement teams, and authorized workspace administrators use this site to understand AMLOK, integrate its API, and assess the evidence behind its methods. Business and consumer underwriting require distinct validation populations.

## Product and terminology

AMLOK Insights performs a full underwriting. AMLOK Signal monitors changes and includes change-driven reassessment. Institutions make decisions and perform outreach in their own systems. Preserve the finalized API, pricing, credit, cancellation, and data-handling rules in AGENTS.md. Research does not change those contracts.

## Research evidence boundary

The November 2026 research section contains a proposed scientific program, attributed prior work, one executed public FX descriptive analysis, and an executed synthetic event-transformer study. It does not establish production foundation-model training, real borrower validation, Canadian market superiority, or a completed frontier-model benchmark. Distinguish proposed methods, synthetic experiments, public-data calculations, and actual customer evidence.

## Voice and presentation

Use concise, scientific language with explicit units, populations, task definitions, limitations, and primary-source citations. Use sentence case and regular dashes. Keep navigation icons on category containers. Use charts for measured quantities and diagrams for proposed architecture. Do not turn a research hypothesis into marketing copy or add invented results to make a comparison look complete.

## Review and publication

Prepare substantial research changes on a review branch. Preserve source records and private source assessments outside public documentation. Publish only within the user's authorization. Existing public product references remain separate from proposed research schemas.
