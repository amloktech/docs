AMLOK Sandbox generates billing records so you can build account-balance displays, payment-status handling, usage reports and reconciliation. **Sandbox activity does not incur real usage charges or initiate real payments.** The financial records you see belong only to that workspace's Sandbox.

## Why the Billing screen contains records

Every Sandbox receives **CAD 5,000.00 in simulated starting credits**. Sample underwriting and monitoring consume simulated credits and create usage records. Payment scenarios create fictional funding and reversal records so your integration can exercise both successful and unsuccessful outcomes.

| Record | What it represents in Sandbox | What it does not mean |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Available credits | The simulated balance available to test processing. | Money deposited into your Live account. |
| Funding history | A fictional card or ACH payment and its current state. | A real card charge or bank debit. |
| Transactions | Simulated credit movements, usage deductions and linked reversals. | An invoice, amount owed or withdrawal of real funds. |
| Usage and workload | The simulated consumption associated with product processing. | A real bill or a guarantee of future Live pricing. |
| Refund or return | A linked reversal used to test reconciliation. | Money sent to a bank account. |

Test credits have no cash value and cannot be moved to Live. Sample prices and balances are fixtures; your [Live pricing and agreement](/pricing/overview) govern real operations.

## Payment outcomes you can exercise

- **Successful card top-up:** creates a successful simulated payment and posts test credits.
- **Declined card payment:** records failure without increasing the balance.
- **Pending ACH:** appears in funding history while the balance remains unchanged.
- **ACH settlement:** moves a pending payment to success and posts test credits.
- **ACH return or refund:** records a linked reversal without rewriting earlier transactions.
- **Automatic top-up:** evaluates a fixed synthetic CAD 199.00 balance against a CAD 200.00 trigger and posts a CAD 1,000.00 simulated top-up. It does not change your saved automatic top-up settings or drain the shared balance.
- **Insufficient credits:** pauses an underwriting, applies simulated funding and resumes the same Origination. It does not drain other developers' test credits.

Run these from **Dev Tools > Sandbox scenarios > Billing**. Financial scenarios and the complete sample pack require an Owner with billing authority. Do not enter real card numbers or bank credentials for these tests; the scenarios supply the simulated payment behavior.

## Reconcile the generated activity

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the balance">
    Read `GET /v1/billing/summary` to see simulated available credits and consumption. Confirm that you are using a Sandbox key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inspect funding states">
    Read `GET /v1/billing/payments`. Pending and failed payments do not create spendable credits; successful settlement does.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow credit movements and usage">
    Read `GET /v1/billing/transactions` and `GET /v1/billing/usage`. Match funding, deductions and reversals using their linked identifiers. Use pagination to retrieve the complete history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare with the console">
    Review the same generated activity in **Billing** and the associated scenario run. Repeating a test adds new records; it does not clear earlier ledger entries or reset credits.
  </Step>
</Steps>

See the [billing API guide](/api-reference/credit-reporting) for response fields and [consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting) for usage details.

## Keep Live financial settings separate

Sandbox scenario commands never invoke a payment processor and cannot run in Live. They do not configure Live payment methods, automatic top-ups, cancellation or refunds. Use the client portal's Live controls for those operations after your workspace is enabled and the appropriate agreement and funding are in place.

For the broader environment differences, see [Sandbox and Live compared](/sandbox/overview#sandbox-and-live-compared).
