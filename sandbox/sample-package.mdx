Load **Explore sample businesses** during first-use setup, or choose **Dev Tools > Sandbox scenarios > Load six sample businesses**. The package creates six separate business journeys. Preparing the 36-month package can take several minutes. The Console shows actual setup progress; you can return to the workspace later to check the persisted run. Use a completed `healthy` journey to explore the full result, then use the other cases to test missing information and recovery.

## What each business contains

| Area | Included data | Where to inspect it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business and owner | Fictional registered business, industry, address, registration number, sole owner and director, and ownership relationship. | Borrower profile, verification source records and the risk package's `verification`. |
| Banking | Operating account and business card, 36 complete months of categorized activity, monthly statements and daily operating-account closing balances. | Connection data, bank statement and cash-flow dimensions. |
| Accounting | Three annual income statements, invoices, credit notes, paid and outstanding bills, balanced monthly cash-cost postings, current supporting schedules, two credit facilities and tax records. | Normalized records, financial dimensions and `analyses.financial_statements`. |
| Payments and commerce | 36 complete months of individual orders and payments, customers, products, discounts, cancellations, declined payments, partial/full refunds, disputes, weekly payouts and explicit settlement links. | Source records, revenue metrics, concentrations and reconciliation. |
| KYB and owner verification | Simulated company registration, owner identity, liveness, ownership, screening and account-holder checks. | `verification`, its provenance and the associated reports. |
| Documents | Six readable PDFs: financial statement with three-year comparisons, 36-month bank history, business registration, identity verification report, tax filing and lending terms. | Origination documents and the document endpoints. |
| Underwriting | Submitted application, versioned lending product and risk policy, assessment, dimensions, source evidence and analysis sections. | Origination drawer and immutable risk package. |
| Monitoring | Funded deal, baseline, evaluations, events and scenario-specific alerts. | Portfolio drawer and monitoring endpoints. |
| System records | API requests, scenario steps and assertions, exports, usage, complexity assessments, test funding, credit movements and statements. | Dev Tools, Billing and the corresponding API endpoints. |

Completed assessments create risk packages and monitoring records. An Origination that requires a document, requires reauthorization or fails retains that state; it does not invent completed results. Recovery scenarios continue the same workflow after the required action. A cancelled connection scenario intentionally creates no connected sources or document pack.


## Exercise every underwriting metric

Use the following scenarios in **Dev Tools > Sandbox scenarios**, or submit their IDs to `POST /v1/sandbox/runs`. Each run adds separate fictional records; it does not replace your existing businesses or historical assessments.

| Scenario | Fixture | What it demonstrates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `full_metric_coverage` | `maple-retail` | Business underwriting with explicit receivables, repayment and fixed-charge inputs, supplier history, reconciliation, simulated credit-report and fraud/consistency observations. |
| `full_metric_coverage` | `cedar-software` | Subscription cohort and retention examples. Select this scenario explicitly; Cedar's starter `manual_review` case intentionally lacks a repayment schedule. |
| `personal_income` | `maple-retail` | Alex Example, an individual borrower, with two fictional payroll streams, matching deposits, rent, essential expenses, obligations and proposed financing. The selected fixture supplies the market/currency; this scenario creates a person rather than the business. |
| `monitoring_metric_coverage` | `maple-retail` | A funded portfolio with an explicitly reconciled debt inventory, a first monitoring baseline and a comparable second evaluation. |

Together these cases demonstrate all **184 scalar underwriting metrics across 24 dimensions**. A single borrower does not have 184 applicable values: consumer affordability belongs to individuals, subscription retention requires a recurring cohort, inventory ratios require inventory, and baseline comparisons require a prior monitoring assessment. A zero denominator remains unavailable instead of becoming a fabricated zero.

This coverage count refers to the scalar metrics in `dimensions[].metrics`, which can be used by risk-policy rules. The separate [financial attribute registry](/data-model/financial-attributes) contains **12,955 definitions** across contexts, categories, periods and statistics. It is not a promise that every attribute is applicable or available for every borrower.

Download the [metric coverage manifest](/downloads/sandbox-metric-coverage.json) to find each metric's scenario, fixture, example value, unit, observation window and supporting-evidence count. It contains fictional examples evaluated at the stated `fixture_observation_at`, not customer records. A later run uses its own frozen observation dates; do not assert exact future values from this snapshot. The manifest includes all six comprehensive business fixtures, the individual case and both monitoring evaluations; the four runs in the table above are sufficient to demonstrate every metric.

The monitoring comparison uses the same frozen observations twice, so zero change is the correct initial example. Inspect the previous-package reference, supporting evidence and calculation basis. The deterioration and recovery scenarios separately demonstrate changes in source activity and alert behavior. Pre-funding and post-funding DSCR are not silently treated as comparable.

The comprehensive business case contains additional fictional source reports, not live credit-bureau results. Provider/model scores and benchmark percentiles are not invented to fill optional sections. Every available metric includes its time window, numeric inputs and evidence references. Missing-source and manual-review scenarios remain available to test null values and explicit missing reasons.

Discover the current `catalog_version` from `GET /v1/sandbox/catalog`; pass it with `scenario_id` and `fixture_id` when creating a run. Poll the returned run ID until completion. Retrieve the resulting package with `GET /v1/risk-packages/{id}?view=summary`, then use `view=section&section=metrics&limit=1` to page individual metrics and `section=financial_attributes` for the detailed attributes. Each metric item contains `dimension_key`, `dimension_status` and the unchanged `metric` object. Follow `page.next_cursor` until `page.has_more` is false. `section=dimensions` remains available for smaller grouped results; an evidence-rich dimension can exceed the response byte limit even with `limit=1`. Use the individual-metric view in that case, or the returned export recovery when one metric is itself too large.

The full example history remains 36 complete months; current underwriting metrics use their stated observation periods, usually the latest twelve months. Individual payroll evidence keeps gross income separate from net bank deposits. Its stressed residual income is conditional on the disclosed 20% income reduction, not a default probability.

## Understand the figures

The source history covers **36 complete calendar months before the run's creation month**. A run created in November 2026 contains business activity from November 1, 2023 through October 31, 2026. The history is fixed for that run, including later recovery or refresh actions. Leap days are included.

Balances and settlement statuses describe the snapshot at the history cutoff. A payout scheduled after that cutoff remains pending in this frozen example, even if you load or inspect it later. `extensions.sandbox.history_window` identifies that boundary; collection timestamps record when Amlok generated and ingested the sample.

<Note>
Completed assessments, frozen evidence and previously issued documents retain their original contents. Retained source records keep their IDs and financial values, and older captured versions can still show the coverage metadata recorded at that time. Read current connection coverage, the current bank-account record and newly issued statements for the available 36-month collection. Newly loaded samples use the 36-month `history_window` throughout; historical audit metadata describes its capture, not additional currently available activity.
</Note>

### Opening balances and settlement references

The first day starts with carried bank cash and unsettled processor funds. Inspect `extensions.sandbox.opening_balance` on the operating bank account and merchant account; its `as_of`, `amount` and `basis` explain that starting position.

A first-period payout can settle a payment captured just before the 36-month window. The package keeps only the related prior-period payment, order and any required supporting reference so you can follow that payout without a broken link. These records are marked `extensions.sandbox.carried_forward: true`, `excluded_from_history_period: true` and `carry_forward_reason: "opening_balance_settlement"`. They are opening-balance context, excluded from the 36-month activity period and the underwriting observation window. A payout's `extensions.sandbox.carried_forward_net` shows its signed prior-period allocation when present.

Use `extensions.sandbox.history_window` for the activity boundaries. Exclude records marked `carried_forward` when counting period activity; their original dates remain visible for reconciliation. Company formation dates, loan funding dates and subscription start dates can also precede the window because they describe the business or agreement, not extra transaction history.

The underwriting observation window remains the **latest twelve complete months**. The 36-month transaction history is not added together and labeled annual revenue. Prior-year comparisons, current balances and pending processor settlements remain distinct. Retrieve the full historical collection through the data endpoints; the risk package freezes the observations used in its assessment.

Canadian businesses report in **CAD**; US businesses report in **USD**. Your simulated Amlok wallet and API consumption remain **CAD**. Business revenue and financing currency do not change the currency of your Amlok invoice.

### Follow the money

1. An order contains its items, discount, sales tax and total. Cancelled or declined orders have no captured funds.
2. A payment records the capture, processing fee and any refund. Refunds and lost disputes have their own dated records and linked accounting credit notes.
3. Processor balance entries group charges and later deductions into weekly payouts after a business-day availability delay. Each paid payout links to its bank transaction; pending payouts remain a processor receivable.
4. Supplier payments, payroll, rent, software, insurance, debt service, capital expenditure and simulated tax remittances affect the operating bank. Business-card repayments link both sides of the transfer.
5. Daily bank balances roll forward from posted activity. Annual statements use business revenue excluding sales tax, with expenses and processor fees accounted for separately.

**Bank receipts are not gross sales.** Taxes, fees, refunds, disputes and settlement timing explain the difference. Inspect `extensions.sandbox.balance_entries` on payouts for the signed allocations. The risk package’s reconciliation analysis compares explicit paid payouts with their bank postings. Its cash-flow statement uses actual operating-bank receipts and payments; a gross invoice total is not treated as net cash. Missing source connections leave links absent; a deterioration scenario intentionally changes bank collections and can produce unmatched reconciliation.

Amounts are decimal strings; ratios such as `"0.20"` mean 20%. Evidence references identify the collected record and field used in a calculation. Not every metric is applicable or available: the ordinary starter cases leave unsupported bureau information, unobserved churn and calibrated predictive outputs unavailable. Use the dedicated metric scenarios above for additional supported fictional examples.

### Business history versus workspace activity

Each business has 36 complete months of operating activity, with separate opening-balance context. The workspace, API requests, invitations, verification checks, scenario actions and Amlok billing records keep their **actual execution dates**. Loading samples does not fabricate three years of customer access or Amlok charges. Scheduled future installments are explicitly marked as scheduled.

New sample runs use this history automatically. The catalog version identifies the sample generation used for a run. Use [Sandbox data setup](/console/sandbox) to replace a workspace's samples when you choose to. A reset clears Sandbox data and requires confirmation; loading the new catalog never resets a workspace silently.

Live history depends on the provider and consented permissions. For example, [Shopify requires additional access for orders older than sixty days](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/Order). Sandbox history is not a promise that every live connection supplies 36 months.

<Note>
The data, people, verification results and files are fictional. Identity files are **verification reports**, not genuine identity documents. A simulated passing check does not establish that any real person, business or document was verified.
</Note>

## Retrieve real document URLs

Document IDs are durable references; download URLs are temporary capabilities. **Do not hard-code a URL from a screenshot, example response or previous run.** Request a fresh link when the user opens a file and use its `expires_at` value.

1. Wait for `GET /v1/sandbox/runs/{id}` to return `completed`.
2. Find resources with `kind: "document"`, or read `document_ids` from the Origination's submitted request.
3. Read `GET /v1/documents/{id}` and check that `status` is `available`.
4. Call `POST /v1/documents/{id}/download-links` with a `purpose` describing the access and a new idempotency key.
5. Fetch the returned `data.url` without forwarding your API key. Check the file's SHA-256 against the returned `sha256`.

This Node.js 22+ example downloads one file from your own completed run. Set `AMLOK_DOCUMENT_ID` to a document resource ID; keep `AMLOK_API_KEY` server-side.

```javascript
import { randomUUID, createHash } from 'node:crypto';
import { writeFile } from 'node:fs/promises';

const base = process.env.AMLOK_API_BASE_URL || 'https://api.amlok.io';
const id = process.env.AMLOK_DOCUMENT_ID;
if (!id || !process.env.AMLOK_API_KEY?.startsWith('amlok_test_')) {
  throw new Error('Set a document ID and Sandbox API key.');
}
const response = await fetch(`${base}/v1/documents/${encodeURIComponent(id)}/download-links`, {
  method: 'POST', redirect: 'error', signal: AbortSignal.timeout(30000),
  headers: {
    Authorization: `Bearer ${process.env.AMLOK_API_KEY}`,
    'Content-Type': 'application/json',
    'Idempotency-Key': randomUUID(),
  },
  body: JSON.stringify({ purpose: 'Review fictional Sandbox evidence' }),
});
const body = await response.json();
if (!response.ok) throw new Error(`${response.status}: ${body.error?.message}`);
if (body.meta.environment !== 'sandbox') throw new Error('Expected Sandbox.');
const link = body.data;
if (Date.parse(link.expires_at) <= Date.now()) throw new Error('Request a fresh link.');
const download = await fetch(link.url, {
  redirect: 'error', signal: AbortSignal.timeout(30000),
});
if (!download.ok) throw new Error(`Download failed: ${download.status}`);
const bytes = Buffer.from(await download.arrayBuffer());
if (createHash('sha256').update(bytes).digest('hex') !== link.sha256) {
  throw new Error('File checksum mismatch.');
}
await writeFile('sandbox-document.pdf', bytes, { mode: 0o600 });
```

Treat signed URLs as secrets while valid. A URL can stop working after expiry, key revocation or an authorization change. Keep the document ID and request a new link when access is still authorized. See [files and downloads](/api-reference/downloads) for range requests and download errors.

## Follow the result back to its sources

From a completed Origination, retrieve its `risk_package_id`. Inspect `assessment`, then a dimension's `metrics`. An available metric includes its value, unit, calculation version, assumptions and inputs. Follow the input's `evidence_id` and `field_path` to the source observation; use `document_id` when a file supports that observation.

Use `GET /v1/data/records` to inspect normalized integration data and its source envelope. Traverse the returned pagination cursors to retrieve the whole collection. The package also includes financial statements, obligations and repayment schedules, collateral, tax, concentration, reconciliation and deterministic cash projections under `analyses`. Verification sections include the source report references and missing-field explanations.

The `manual_review` case deliberately omits the proposed repayment schedule, so DSCR and dependent debt-service metrics remain unavailable. The lending-terms PDF explains that omission. The `monitoring_alert` case reduces bank receipts while keeping the accounting observations, producing both deterioration and a reconciliation difference. `monitoring_recovery` restores receipts and reevaluates the monitor.

## Explore detailed financial attributes

Choose **Underwriting > Detailed financial attributes** (`detailed_attribute_coverage`) with any of the six existing fictional business profiles. This scenario creates a new borrower and linked records for that run. It does not replace existing sample businesses, change historical assessments or require a separate business for each attribute.

The history covers **36 calendar periods: 35 complete months plus the current partial month**. A run created on November 19, 2026 starts on December 1, 2023 and includes current-month observations through its frozen cutoff. Daily bank and issuer-card attributes use completed UTC days; current-day activity cannot establish a complete day. On the first day of a month, a month-to-date attribute can correctly remain unavailable because no complete day has elapsed. The ordinary sample pack and existing comprehensive scalar-metric scenarios retain their 36 complete-month histories.

The detailed scenario retains 35 complete months of individual orders and captured processor payments. Its current partial-month additions are bank/accounting activity and status observations on existing provider records, such as dispute inquiries and unsuccessful payout attempts. It does not invent new current-month Shopify orders or Stripe captures; direct customer receipts are identified separately and backed by accounting records.

Inspect the returned observation windows. Current-month transaction activity is an interim source ledger; it is not added to a completed annual income statement or represented as a complete month. Bank cash, issuer-card purchases and merchant-processing settlements remain separate. A card purchase and its later bank repayment must not be counted twice as operating spending. Annual financial statements, collateral valuations and annual debt metrics retain their source closing date; the current loan schedule and latest bank balances can have a later observation date. A calculation requiring the same date, such as net debt, remains unavailable when those observations cannot be aligned.

The registry has **12,955 detailed definitions**: **12,805 bank/card definitions** and **150 cross-system definitions**. These combine contexts, categories, periods and statistics, rather than represent independent facts that must all be nonzero. The existing **184 scalar underwriting metrics across 24 dimensions** remain a separate catalog with their own [example coverage](/sandbox/sample-package#exercise-every-underwriting-metric).

Read each attribute's status and reason before asserting a value:

| Result | How to interpret it |
| --- | --- |
| Available, nonzero | The disclosed observations support this result in the stated context and period. |
| Available, zero | Complete, sufficiently classified observations support no matching activity, or the calculated balance or difference is zero. |
| Unavailable, sparse observations | Some statistics need more observations. For example, `p90` requires at least ten values and standard deviation requires at least three. |
| Unavailable, missing or restricted source | The required input population is not available or authorized. This is not zero activity. |
| Unavailable, insufficient classification detail | A broad expense label cannot establish whether a specific category, such as software, is absent. |
| Unavailable, empty period | The frozen source window contains no completed day for that period. |

Source examples follow each business's operations. A business need not have payroll deposits, consumer benefits, gambling activity or every possible issuer-card category. Do not treat a coverage percentage as credit quality, or fill unsupported combinations with invented transactions.

### Run and retrieve the detailed results

Get the current version from `GET /v1/sandbox/catalog`, then create the run with that exact `catalog_version`:

```bash
curl --fail-with-body -X POST "$AMLOK_API_BASE_URL/v1/sandbox/runs" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $AMLOK_API_KEY" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -H 'Idempotency-Key: detailed-attributes-maple-001' \
  -d '{"scenario_id":"detailed_attribute_coverage","fixture_id":"maple-retail","catalog_version":"2026-11-19.4"}'
```

Poll `GET /v1/sandbox/runs/{id}` until completion. Follow its linked risk package and retrieve `GET /v1/risk-packages/{id}?view=summary`. Read `financial_attribute_summary` for evaluated, available and unavailable counts; these counts refer to the particular assessment, not a promise that every definition has a usable value.

Page through `view=section&section=financial_attributes&limit=25`, following `page.next_cursor` until `page.has_more` is false. Filter by an exact `attribute_key` or a literal `attribute_prefix`; keep the same filter on every page. For each result, retrieve the matching `input_manifest_id` from `section=financial_attribute_inputs`, then follow its evidence references to the frozen source records. [Financial attributes](/data-model/financial-attributes) explains the fields and download catalogs.

In Console, use **Dev Tools > Data Lookup > Financial intelligence** for definitions, formulas and required inputs. Use **Sandbox scenarios > Run history** to inspect execution and linked records. Saved runs and packages retain their original catalog version; a new catalog does not rewrite them.
