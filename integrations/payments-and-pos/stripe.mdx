## Integration approach

Balance Transactions, Payments, Payouts, and dispute APIs; determine approved account-linking and read permissions.

## Access and approval

Confirm Connect/platform or Stripe Apps route; public app review ETA ND. Review source.

**Documentation availability:** Public API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
