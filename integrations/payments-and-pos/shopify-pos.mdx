## Integration approach

Shared Shopify Admin GraphQL authorization; distinguish POS orders by channel/location; assess payment payout scopes separately.

## Access and approval

Public distribution requires Shopify app review; ETA ND; custom distribution has narrower eligibility. Review source.

**Documentation availability:** Public API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
