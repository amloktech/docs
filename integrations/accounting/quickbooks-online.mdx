## Data refresh

**Provider update cadence:** On supported entity changes; CDC for reconciliation.

QuickBooks Online supports entity-change webhooks and Change Data Capture (CDC). Intuit recommends CDC reconciliation after missed events and suggests a daily CDC call for required entities.

The daily CDC check is an Intuit integration recommendation, not a statement that all data updates only daily. Notifications can be delayed or out of sequence; coverage depends on the entities and permissions enabled.

**AMLOK synchronization:** The enabled refresh mode and schedule are agreed for your connection. Provider timing describes upstream capability; allow for retrieval and processing before changes appear in AMLOK. See [data refresh and freshness](/integrations/data-refresh).

Provider references: [Intuit webhook best practices](https://static.developer.intuit.com/output_html/qbo/docs/develop/webhooks/best-practices.html).
