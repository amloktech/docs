## Integration approach

GraphQL/API assessment; verify financial-statement and ledger coverage; supplement with exports when needed.

## Access and approval

Wave approval and OAuth required for integrations beyond your own account; ETA ND. Minutes to create an app is not approval. Review source.

**Documentation availability:** Public GraphQL API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
