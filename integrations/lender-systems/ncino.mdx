## Integration approach

Integrate the lender's nCino product through its platform or consumer-banking APIs and supported connectors. Match the product, installed configuration and permitted objects; the U.S. Mortgage API is a separate product.

## Access and approval

Confirm lender authorization, product entitlement and vendor provisioning for the chosen API. No general production approval ETA found in reviewed sources.

**Documentation availability:** Public developer portal with product-specific API references; access and coverage vary by product.
