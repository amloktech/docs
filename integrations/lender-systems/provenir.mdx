## Integration approach

REST APIs, batch and webhooks connect AMLOK data to lender-defined decision workflows. Agree input/output schemas and ownership of decision rules with the institution.

## Access and approval

Arrange environment and technical access with Provenir and the lender. Approval ETA not published. Marketing examples of four-week go-live projects describe implementation, not guaranteed integration approval.
