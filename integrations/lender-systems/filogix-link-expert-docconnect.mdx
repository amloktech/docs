## Integration approach

Filogix Link carries broker submissions into lender workflows; DocConnect handles supporting documents. This is a mortgage intake/network connection, distinct from the lender's internal LOS.

## Access and approval

Arrange lender connectivity and document enablement with Filogix. DocConnect activation depends on the participating accounts/lenders. Approval ETA not published.
