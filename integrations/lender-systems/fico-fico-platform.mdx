## Integration approach

Candidate route: connect AMLOK data to the lender's licensed FICO decision services. Confirm the exact API, model/strategy version and integration contract; FICO scores and FICO Platform are different products.

## Access and approval

Confirm technical documentation, licence and environment access with FICO and the lender. Approval requirements and numeric ETA are not detailed in the reviewed public sources.
