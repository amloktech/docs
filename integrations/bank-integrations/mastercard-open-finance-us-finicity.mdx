## Integration approach

Data Connect plus Open Finance APIs for accounts, transactions, statements and reports. This documented offering is US-focused; Canadian institution support must be established separately before selecting it for a Canada-only rollout.

## Access and approval

Executed Mastercard contract plus Production access review required. Approval ETA not disclosed. The 30-day Test Drive Premium period is a live-data trial duration, not an approval estimate.

**Documentation availability:** Public REST API reference, quickstart and test profiles.

## Data refresh

**Provider update cadence:** Once daily for active accounts; restricted on-demand refresh.

Finicity automatically refreshes active accounts once per day. Its Refresh Customer Accounts endpoint allows a customer-specific refresh for a concrete business need, subject to product access.

The documented service prohibits automating calls to its Refresh endpoints. Additional refresh use must be agreed with the provider; repeated API reads do not force a new institution refresh. Availability also depends on the billing tier.

**AMLOK synchronization:** The enabled refresh mode and schedule are agreed for your connection. Provider timing describes upstream capability; allow for retrieval and processing before changes appear in AMLOK. See [data refresh and freshness](/integrations/data-refresh).

Provider references: [Mastercard official Refresh Customer Accounts reference](https://www.postman.com/mastercard/mastercard-open-finance/request/zy0586e/refresh-customer-accounts).
