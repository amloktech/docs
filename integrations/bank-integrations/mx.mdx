## Integration approach

MX Connect Widget plus platform APIs for account and transaction data. MX describes US and Canadian account connectivity; validate institutions, business-account support and enabled data products.

## Access and approval

Request API keys and sandbox access through the MX team; arrange production access and product entitlements. Numeric approval ETA not disclosed in reviewed sources.

**Documentation availability:** Public developer documentation for connectivity, aggregation and account data.
