## Integration approach

Register as a data recipient and use permissioned data APIs and provider subscriptions. Confirm target institution and country coverage; do not assume the network provides the Canadian bank accounts AMLOK needs.

## Access and approval

Production onboarding includes questionnaire, security/risk review and legal agreement; total ETA not disclosed. After production app registration, individual providers typically approve connections within 5–10 business days per Akoya's guide. That window excludes initial onboarding.

**Documentation availability:** Public data API documentation, sandbox and Data Recipient Hub guides.
