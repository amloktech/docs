## Integration approach

Flinks Connect plus API for permissioned bank accounts, balances and transaction history. Validate the particular Canadian institution, business account type, history and refresh access.

## Access and approval

Customer onboarding and production-instance credentials are required. Open-banking routes can also require data-provider activation. Production approval ETA not disclosed in reviewed sources.

**Documentation availability:** Public API documentation and Connect guides.
