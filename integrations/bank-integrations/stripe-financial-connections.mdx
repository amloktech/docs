## Integration approach

Financial Connections sessions plus APIs for balances, ownership and transactions. Published availability is for US bank accounts and eligible business countries; Canada is absent from the reviewed business-country list. Confirm eligibility before committing to this route.

## Access and approval

Requires an eligible Stripe business and enabled Financial Connections data permissions. Dedicated integration approval ETA not disclosed in reviewed docs; sandbox/testing does not establish Canadian eligibility or commercial access.

**Documentation availability:** Public API reference and Financial Connections guides.
