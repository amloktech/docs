## Integration approach

Plaid Link plus server APIs for Canadian institutions. Country matrix lists Transactions, Balance, Auth, Identity, Assets and Income; Liabilities has limited coverage. Plaid Check / Partner Insights is not listed for Canada. Validate institution, product and business-account coverage.

## Access and approval

Full Production: a few business days after company and use-case review, per Plaid; not a guaranteed SLA. Eligible US/CA Trial applications are usually auto-approved; manual-review follow-up is 2–3 business days. Trial is limited; paid products and institution permissions require the relevant access.

**Documentation availability:** Public API reference, SDKs, Link guides and Sandbox. Canadian coverage can be checked through the Institutions API.
