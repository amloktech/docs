## Integration approach

Trustly bank authorization SDK plus North America account-data APIs. Wait for DataReady before retrieving account data. Confirm Canadian availability of each data product and historical transaction access separately from payments.

## Access and approval

Trustly provisions sandbox/production credentials and merchant/product access. Numeric approval ETA not disclosed in reviewed sources; data-retrieval or customer-onboarding speed is not a partner-approval estimate.

**Documentation availability:** Public North America API reference, SDK and data-flow guides.
