## Integration approach

Zūm Connect plus Aggregation API returns linked bank accounts, balances and transaction history when aggregation is enabled. Public docs show Canadian and US response variants. Confirm underlying provider, account coverage and recurring refresh rights.

## Access and approval

Sandbox/production accounts are separate and aggregation must be enabled for the customer account. Coordinate production and data-product activation with Zūm Rails; numeric approval ETA not disclosed in reviewed sources.

**Documentation availability:** Public Aggregation API, supported-institution and authentication documentation.
