## Provider and privacy review

The integration catalog describes sources, availability, and candidate data coverage. It is not the same as the service-provider register. Review [hosting and subprocessors](/security/hosting-and-subprocessors) for Azure hosting, Stripe billing, Flinks/Plaid financial-data access, Baselayer verification, identity services, and website providers. A named source or active catalog entry does not authorize every data use or mean every customer uses that provider.

Review the [Trust Center](https://amlok.io/trust), [Privacy Policy](https://amlok.io/privacy), [connection terms](https://amlok.io/connection-terms), and [open banking and FDX guidance](/security/open-banking-and-fdx) before connecting live records.
