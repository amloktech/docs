## Integration approach

Service Layer/OData or approved SDK/reporting route; installed version and database matter.

## Access and approval

Customer licence, server and administrator access; partner certification separate; ETA ND.

**Documentation availability:** Public Service Layer API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
