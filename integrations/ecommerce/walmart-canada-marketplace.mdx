## Integration approach

Global Marketplace APIs for Canada/report exports; validate seller authorization, provider approval and migration state.

## Access and approval

Approved Solution Provider plus seller authorization; approval ETA ND. Review source.

**Documentation availability:** Public Global Marketplace API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
