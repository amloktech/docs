## Integration approach

Shared Square Orders API; identify online versus POS sales and reuse account authorization.

## Access and approval

Same Square authorization as PAY-03; self-serve API with seller consent; Marketplace ETA ND.

**Documentation availability:** Public API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
