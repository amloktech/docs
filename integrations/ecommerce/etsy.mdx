## Integration approach

Open API v3 + shop authorization; commercial app approval and permitted financial fields require review.

## Access and approval

AMLOK needs approved third-party/commercial access; ETA ND. Own-shop seller apps can be approved within minutes but do not authorize AMLOK's multi-merchant use. Review source.

**Documentation availability:** Public API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
