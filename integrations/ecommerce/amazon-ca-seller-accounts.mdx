## Integration approach

Selling Partner API, Orders/Reports/Finances as permitted; seller authorization and application approval required.

## Access and approval

Public developer registration, role/access and app distribution approval; end-to-end ETA ND.

**Documentation availability:** Public SP-API/docs.

*ND means the source catalog does not disclose an approval estimate.*
