Your AMLOK credit account is **read-only for customers**. Reporting shows credits received, product usage, remaining credits, and adjustments. It does not provide an interactive wallet or payment controls.

## Understand your balance

The credit balance identifies your billing account and its snapshot time, `as_of`. All amounts are exact CAD decimal strings.

- `committed_credits` records prepaid minimum-commitment credits.
- `additional_funds` records additional paid credits.
- `promotional_credits` records non-cash credits separately from paid principal.
- Each bucket shows `posted`, `held`, and `available` amounts.
- `pending_incoming` shows payments still awaiting settlement and is excluded from available credits.
- `available_for_usage` is the amount available for eligible AMLOK product usage.
- `remaining_paid_credits` is the remaining paid principal, including paid credits reserved for pending usage. It is not an amount you can withdraw on demand.

`available_for_usage = total_posted - total_held`

A hold reserves credits for pending activity without creating a second charge. AMLOK reconciles the hold when the activity is resolved. Paid and promotional credits remain separate in reporting.

## Review credit activity

Ledger entries are immutable, ordered by `sequence`, and show the signed amount and resulting `balance_after`. Use `sequence_after` and cursor pagination to read subsequent entries. Corrections appear as new entries linked to the original record.

Statements reconcile opening credits, settled additions, product usage, final-cancellation refunds, adjustments, and closing credits. Use the same billing account, date window, time basis, and snapshot when comparing reports. See [consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting).

## Access and exports

`billing:read` permits authorized credit, usage, and statement reads. `billing:export` permits report generation and authorized download links. Both are restricted to your institution's billing account.

Creating a report or download link only prepares reporting data. It does not change credits, charge a payment method, initiate a top-up, or move funds. There are no customer wallet endpoints or wallet-management scopes.

## Payment and agreement assistance

Contact your account manager for additional credits, payment arrangements, or changes to agreed top-ups. These are managed outside the customer credit view and API.

Your account manager also coordinates final cancellation and the full remaining paid-balance refund. AMLOK issues the refund **immediately after account closure**; bank receipt may take **7-10 business days**, depending on the bank. See [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing), [refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts), and [account management](/support/account-management).
