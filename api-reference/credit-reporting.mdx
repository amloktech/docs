Each workspace has one unified billing account and credit balance across eligible AMLOK products. The **public API is reporting-only**. The client portal separately lets customers manage card or bank-account funding, automatic top-up settings, and cancellation.

## One credit balance

Reports identify the authorized `workspace_id` and billing account. Paid funding and commercial promotional top-ups are combined; there are no separate source-based spending balances or consumption priorities. Product and tier identify the reason and rate for a charge, not a separate wallet.

The credit snapshot reports exact CAD decimal strings:

- `total_posted`: credits remaining after posted additions, deductions, refunds, and corrections.
- `total_held`: credits reserved for pending processing, not an additional posted debit.
- `available_for_usage`: posted credits less holds, before protecting the processing floor.
- `processing_floor`: CAD `200.00`, kept unused while the account is active.
- `spendable_for_processing`: available credits above that floor.
- `pending_incoming`: funding still awaiting settlement, excluded from usable credits.

`available_for_usage = total_posted - total_held`

`spendable_for_processing = max(0, available_for_usage - processing_floor)`

New billable processing or a new reservation must not take available credits below the floor. Capture of an existing hold replaces the reservation with the charge rather than deducting twice. Failed or pending funding can pause processing until sufficient usable credits arrive; reporting remains accessible on an active account.

For example, CAD 10,900 posted credits and CAD 500 held leave CAD 10,400 available, of which **CAD 10,200** can fund new processing while retaining the CAD 200 floor. The whole unused balance, including the floor and promotional credits, is refundable on final cancellation after pending usage is resolved.

## Ledger and product reporting

Ledger entries are immutable and ordered by `sequence`. Each entry shows a signed amount, resulting balance, movement reference, and any reversal link. There is no paid-versus-promotional debit allocation. Use `sequence_after` and cursor pagination for subsequent entries.

Statements reconcile opening credits, top-ups, product usage, cancellation refunds, and adjustments. Insights and Signal retain their own units, tiers, and effective rates within the same workspace account. See [consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting).

## API access and portal controls

`billing:read` authorizes credit, usage, and statement reads; `billing:export` authorizes report generation and download links. Reads and exports must be restricted to the authorized workspace within the institution. A response's `workspace_id` is context, not authorization, and callers cannot select another workspace by adding an undocumented parameter.

Report creation and download links do not change credits. There are no public wallet-management, payment-method, top-up, threshold-setting, cancellation, refund, or transfer endpoints.

In the portal, the minimum automatic top-up threshold is CAD 200 and the minimum customer-funded top-up is CAD 500. Customers can fund by card or bank account and must save a bank account for cancellation refunds. Enterprise clients can also request cancellation through their dedicated account manager.

Cancellation includes workspace deletion. AMLOK immediately refunds the entire unused credit balance to the saved bank account when the account closes; bank receipt can take **7-10 business days**. Used credits are non-refundable. See [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing) and [cancellation and refunds](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts).
