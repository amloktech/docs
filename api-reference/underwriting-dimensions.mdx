---
title: "Underwriting dimensions"
description: "Definitions, formulas, evidence requirements and applicability for 149 risk metrics."
---

Every risk package accounts for the following 24 dimension families and 149 metric definitions. A value is available only when its applicability, evidence, history and denominator requirements are met. The [machine-readable catalog](/downloads/underwriting-metric-catalog.json) is generated from the same definitions used by the API and console Data Lookup.

`GET /v1/configuration` returns the entitled scalar definitions in `dimensions`. These are the keys accepted by `PolicyRule.dimension_key`; a family such as `liquidity` is not itself a scalar policy field. An API key can have access to a subset of the public catalog.

Ratios are fractions; currency metrics use `scope.reporting_currency`. Counts and durations are nonnegative; the cash conversion cycle can be negative when suppliers are paid after cash is collected. Unsupported values are null with explicit reasons. [Calculation and interpretation rules](/api-reference/risk-package-methodology) apply to every entry.

## Identity and verification

Identity and account-ownership checks identify inconsistencies; they do not substitute for financial capacity.

Sources: identity, registry, banking, documents.

### business_age_months

**Business age** · `months` · business

Whole calendar months from verified formation date to assessment date.

Required inputs and conditions: Verified formation date; assessment date; business only.

Calculation version: `amlok-business_age_months-1`.

### verified_account_owner_ratio

**Verified account ownership share** · `ratio` · business, person

Accounts with positively matched ownership divided by eligible disclosed connected accounts.

Required inputs and conditions: Account ownership evidence and resolved borrower identity; nonzero eligible-account count.

Calculation version: `amlok-verified_account_owner_ratio-1`.

## Ownership and control

Use documented beneficial ownership, control, guarantees and related-party exposure. Do not infer family relationships or hidden ownership.

Sources: registry, identity, accounting, application, documents.

### ownership_disclosed_ratio

**Disclosed ownership share** · `ratio` · business

Sum of supported non-overlapping ownership fractions in the assessed entity.

Required inputs and conditions: Current ownership register; common share basis; do not double-count direct and indirect paths.

Calculation version: `amlok-ownership_disclosed_ratio-1`.

### related_party_balance_share

**Related-party receivables share** · `ratio` · business

Related-party receivables divided by total receivables.

Required inputs and conditions: Resolved related parties and same-date receivables; positive total receivables.

Calculation version: `amlok-related_party_balance_share-1`.

### supported_guarantee_coverage

**Supported guarantee coverage** · `ratio` · business

Documented collectible guarantee amount capped at exposure divided by exposure.

Required inputs and conditions: Guarantee terms, guarantor assessment, currency and exposure; positive exposure; no unsupported collectible value.

Calculation version: `amlok-supported_guarantee_coverage-1`.

## Cash flow

Use posted, deduplicated flows. Consolidation eliminates matched transfers; raw inflows remain distinct from operating receipts and revenue.

Sources: banking, payments, accounting.

### total_inflows

**Posted inflows** · `currency` · business, person

Sum posted inflow magnitudes before transfer or revenue exclusions.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete eligible transaction set for the window, scope and currency.

Calculation version: `amlok-total_inflows-1`.

### total_outflows

**Posted outflows** · `currency` · business, person

Sum posted outflow magnitudes before transfer or operating exclusions.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete eligible transaction set for the window, scope and currency.

Calculation version: `amlok-total_outflows-1`.

### net_cash_flow

**Net cash flow** · `currency` · business, person

Posted inflows minus posted outflows after consolidation transfer elimination.

Required inputs and conditions: Matched transfers, complete transactions and consistent scope/currency.

Calculation version: `amlok-net_cash_flow-1`.

### operating_cash_inflows

**Operating receipts** · `currency` · business, person

Sum classified operating receipts excluding financing, internal transfers and owner injections.

Required inputs and conditions: Supported transaction classifications and explicit exclusion bridge.

Calculation version: `amlok-operating_cash_inflows-1`.

### operating_cash_outflows

**Operating cash payments** · `currency` · business, person

Sum operating outflows excluding financing principal, capital expenditures and matched transfers.

Required inputs and conditions: Supported classifications and cash-flow basis; interest/tax treatment disclosed.

Calculation version: `amlok-operating_cash_outflows-1`.

### operating_net_cash_flow

**Net operating cash flow** · `currency` · business, person

Operating receipts minus operating cash payments.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-window receipts/outflows and documented classification basis.

Calculation version: `amlok-operating_net_cash_flow-1`.

### internal_transfer_inflows

**Internal transfer inflows** · `currency` · business, person

Sum inflows matched to outflows within the consolidation scope.

Required inputs and conditions: Supported transfer pairs with stable IDs and explicit FX treatment.

Calculation version: `amlok-internal_transfer_inflows-1`.

### financing_inflows

**Financing inflows** · `currency` · business, person

Sum identified debt and equity financing receipts.

Required inputs and conditions: Financing classification and evidence; uncertain receipts remain unclassified.

Calculation version: `amlok-financing_inflows-1`.

### positive_cashflow_month_share

**Positive cash-flow months** · `ratio` · business, person

Complete months with positive net cash flow divided by complete covered months.

Required inputs and conditions: At least three complete months and consistent account scope.

Calculation version: `amlok-positive_cashflow_month_share-1`.

### cashflow_volatility

**Operating cash-flow volatility** · `ratio` · business, person

Population standard deviation of complete monthly net operating cash flow divided by absolute mean.

Required inputs and conditions: At least six complete months; nonzero mean; disclose near-zero denominator sensitivity.

Calculation version: `amlok-cashflow_volatility-1`.

### cashflow_change_90d

**Operating cash-flow change** · `ratio` · business, person

Current 90-day operating net flow minus previous 90-day flow, divided by absolute previous flow.

Required inputs and conditions: Two complete comparable 90-day windows; nonzero previous flow.

Calculation version: `amlok-cashflow_change_90d-1`.

## Liquidity

Separate available, restricted and observed balances. Reconstructed daily balances require reconciled opening/closing balances and complete transaction coverage.

Sources: banking, accounting.

### unrestricted_cash

**Unrestricted cash** · `currency` · business, person

Sum verified cash balances available for use, excluding restricted balances.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-as-of reconciled balances and restriction status.

Calculation version: `amlok-unrestricted_cash-1`.

### average_daily_balance

**Average daily cash balance** · `currency` · business, person

Sum end-of-day balances divided by covered calendar days.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete reconciled daily balances for the selected account scope.

Calculation version: `amlok-average_daily_balance-1`.

### minimum_daily_balance

**Minimum daily cash balance** · `currency` · business, person

Minimum consolidated end-of-day balance.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete comparable daily balances; disclose observed versus reconstructed basis.

Calculation version: `amlok-minimum_daily_balance-1`.

### negative_balance_days

**Days below zero** · `days` · business, person

Count covered days with negative consolidated end-of-day balance.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete daily balance series; no missing day treated as zero.

Calculation version: `amlok-negative_balance_days-1`.

### cash_buffer_days

**Cash buffer** · `days` · business, person

Unrestricted cash divided by mean daily essential cash outflows.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive essential-outflow denominator; current cash; documented window and exclusions.

Calculation version: `amlok-cash_buffer_days-1`.

### current_ratio

**Current ratio** · `ratio` · business

Current assets divided by current liabilities.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-date classified balance sheet; positive current liabilities.

Calculation version: `amlok-current_ratio-1`.

### quick_ratio

**Quick ratio** · `ratio` · business

Cash plus short-term investments plus eligible receivables, divided by current liabilities.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-date asset/liability classifications; positive current liabilities.

Calculation version: `amlok-quick_ratio-1`.

### cash_ratio

**Cash ratio** · `ratio` · business

Unrestricted cash and cash equivalents divided by current liabilities.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-date balances; positive current liabilities.

Calculation version: `amlok-cash_ratio-1`.

## Revenue quality

Distinguish accrual revenue, gross sales, refunds, taxes, fees and cash settlement. Financing, transfers and owner injections are not sales.

Sources: banking, payments, commerce, accounting.

### verified_revenue

**Verified revenue** · `currency` · business

Sum supported revenue on the declared cash or accrual basis after exclusions.

Required inputs and conditions: Reconciled bank, accounting or commerce evidence; taxes, financing and transfers excluded.

Calculation version: `amlok-verified_revenue-1`.

### recurring_revenue_share

**Recurring revenue share** · `ratio` · business

Supported recurring revenue divided by total verified revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Defined recurrence rule and matched periods; positive total revenue.

Calculation version: `amlok-recurring_revenue_share-1`.

### revenue_growth_yoy

**Revenue growth year over year** · `ratio` · business

Current-period revenue minus prior-year comparable revenue, divided by prior-year revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Comparable periods separated by one year; positive prior revenue.

Calculation version: `amlok-revenue_growth_yoy-1`.

### revenue_growth_90d

**Revenue growth over 90 days** · `ratio` · business

Current 90-day revenue minus preceding 90-day revenue, divided by preceding revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Two complete 90-day periods; positive prior revenue.

Calculation version: `amlok-revenue_growth_90d-1`.

### revenue_volatility

**Monthly revenue volatility** · `ratio` · business

Population standard deviation of monthly revenue divided by mean monthly revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: At least six complete months; positive mean; consistent recognition basis.

Calculation version: `amlok-revenue_volatility-1`.

### refund_amount_share

**Refund amount share** · `ratio` · business

Refunded sales amount divided by eligible gross sales amount.

Required inputs and conditions: Matched sales/refund cohort and treatment of late refunds; positive gross sales.

Calculation version: `amlok-refund_amount_share-1`.

### chargeback_amount_share

**Chargeback amount share** · `ratio` · business

Disputed chargeback amount divided by eligible processed sales amount.

Required inputs and conditions: Matched processing cohort and consistent chargeback stage; positive denominator.

Calculation version: `amlok-chargeback_amount_share-1`.

### revenue_cash_realization

**Revenue cash realization** · `ratio` · business

Matched collections on recognized sales divided by recognized sales.

Required inputs and conditions: Defined collection horizon and sales cohort; positive recognized sales; not raw deposits/revenue.

Calculation version: `amlok-revenue_cash_realization-1`.

### unverified_receipts_share

**Unverified receipts share** · `ratio` · business

Unclassified or unsupported operating-like receipts divided by total candidate receipts.

Required inputs and conditions: Classification coverage and positive candidate receipts.

Calculation version: `amlok-unverified_receipts_share-1`.

### nonrecurring_revenue_share

**Nonrecurring revenue share** · `ratio` · business

Identified nonrecurring revenue divided by verified revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Versioned recurrence criteria and positive revenue.

Calculation version: `amlok-nonrecurring_revenue_share-1`.

## Profitability

Align accounting basis, reporting scope and periods. EBITDA addbacks require itemized justification; never infer profit from deposits alone.

Sources: accounting, tax, documents.

### gross_profit

**Gross profit** · `currency` · business

Recognized revenue minus cost of goods sold.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-period income statement with classified COGS.

Calculation version: `amlok-gross_profit-1`.

### gross_margin

**Gross margin** · `ratio` · business

Gross profit divided by recognized revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive revenue and consistent accounting basis.

Calculation version: `amlok-gross_margin-1`.

### ebitda

**EBITDA** · `currency` · business

Net income plus income tax expense, interest expense, depreciation and amortization.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete supported statement line items; no automatic discretionary addbacks.

Calculation version: `amlok-ebitda-1`.

### ebitda_margin

**EBITDA margin** · `ratio` · business

EBITDA divided by recognized revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive revenue and supported EBITDA.

Calculation version: `amlok-ebitda_margin-1`.

### operating_profit

**Operating profit** · `currency` · business

Revenue minus COGS and operating expenses.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete same-period operating statement; financing excluded.

Calculation version: `amlok-operating_profit-1`.

### operating_margin

**Operating margin** · `ratio` · business

Operating profit divided by revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive recognized revenue.

Calculation version: `amlok-operating_margin-1`.

### net_profit

**Net profit** · `currency` · business

Total recognized income minus all recognized expenses.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete statement on declared accounting basis.

Calculation version: `amlok-net_profit-1`.

### net_margin

**Net margin** · `ratio` · business

Net profit divided by recognized revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive recognized revenue.

Calculation version: `amlok-net_margin-1`.

### free_cash_flow

**Free cash flow** · `currency` · business

Operating cash flow minus cash capital expenditures.

Required inputs and conditions: Reconciled cash-flow statement or supported cash reconstruction; no debt proceeds.

Calculation version: `amlok-free_cash_flow-1`.

### return_on_assets

**Return on assets** · `ratio` · business

Period net profit divided by mean opening and closing total assets.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive average assets and complete period; disclose annualization, never apply silently.

Calculation version: `amlok-return_on_assets-1`.

## Working capital

Use dated invoice/bill aging, sales and cost denominators on the same basis. Inventory valuation and credit-sales coverage must be explicit.

Sources: accounting, commerce, inventory.

### net_working_capital

**Net working capital** · `currency` · business

Current assets minus current liabilities.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-date classified balance sheet.

Calculation version: `amlok-net_working_capital-1`.

### receivables_days

**Receivable days** · `days` · business

Average trade receivables divided by credit sales, multiplied by period days.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive credit sales; opening/closing receivables; same accounting period.

Calculation version: `amlok-receivables_days-1`.

### payables_days

**Payable days** · `days` · business

Average trade payables divided by credit purchases, multiplied by period days.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive credit purchases; opening/closing payables; do not substitute COGS silently.

Calculation version: `amlok-payables_days-1`.

### inventory_days

**Inventory days** · `days` · business

Average inventory divided by COGS, multiplied by period days.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive COGS; opening/closing comparable inventory valuation.

Calculation version: `amlok-inventory_days-1`.

### cash_conversion_cycle

**Cash conversion cycle** · `days` · business

Receivable days plus inventory days minus payable days.

Required inputs and conditions: All three components available on comparable periods and basis.

Calculation version: `amlok-cash_conversion_cycle-1`.

### ar_overdue_share

**Overdue receivables share** · `ratio` · business

Overdue collectible trade receivables divided by total collectible trade receivables.

Required inputs and conditions: Due dates and same-as-of open balances; positive receivables.

Calculation version: `amlok-ar_overdue_share-1`.

### ar_90_plus_share

**Receivables over 90 days past due** · `ratio` · business

Receivables more than 90 days past due divided by total trade receivables.

Required inputs and conditions: Aging based on due date, not invoice date; positive receivables.

Calculation version: `amlok-ar_90_plus_share-1`.

### ap_overdue_share

**Overdue payables share** · `ratio` · business

Overdue trade payables divided by total trade payables.

Required inputs and conditions: Due dates and open balances; positive payables.

Calculation version: `amlok-ap_overdue_share-1`.

### inventory_turnover

**Inventory turnover** · `ratio` · business

Period COGS divided by average inventory.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive comparable inventory and supported COGS.

Calculation version: `amlok-inventory_turnover-1`.

## Debt and leverage

Reconcile observed debt payments to disclosed and entitled bureau obligations. Unknown balances and undisclosed obligations are not zero.

Sources: accounting, banking, credit_bureau, lender, documents.

### total_debt

**Total interest-bearing debt** · `currency` · business, person

Sum outstanding principal of resolved interest-bearing obligations.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete disclosed and supported debt inventory; leases treatment explicit.

Calculation version: `amlok-total_debt-1`.

### net_debt

**Net debt** · `currency` · business, person

Total debt minus unrestricted cash eligible for offset.

Required inputs and conditions: Same-as-of currency and consolidated scope.

Calculation version: `amlok-net_debt-1`.

### debt_to_assets

**Debt to assets** · `ratio` · business

Total interest-bearing debt divided by total assets.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive total assets and matched scope; excludes non-debt liabilities by definition.

Calculation version: `amlok-debt_to_assets-1`.

### debt_to_equity

**Debt to equity** · `ratio` · business

Total debt divided by equity.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive equity; zero/negative equity produces unavailable ratio and a separate finding.

Calculation version: `amlok-debt_to_equity-1`.

### net_debt_to_ebitda

**Net debt to EBITDA** · `ratio` · business

Net debt divided by trailing-12-month EBITDA.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive EBITDA and complete 12-month period.

Calculation version: `amlok-net_debt_to_ebitda-1`.

### credit_utilization

**Revolving credit utilization** · `ratio` · business, person

Used revolving principal divided by committed revolving limits.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive known limits; distinguish drawn balances from pending authorizations.

Calculation version: `amlok-credit_utilization-1`.

### short_term_debt_share

**Debt due within one year** · `ratio` · business, person

Principal contractually due within 365 days divided by total debt.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete maturity schedule and positive total debt.

Calculation version: `amlok-short_term_debt_share-1`.

### debt_maturing_90d

**Debt maturing within 90 days** · `currency` · business, person

Sum principal contractually due within the next 90 days.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete maturity schedule including balloon payments.

Calculation version: `amlok-debt_maturing_90d-1`.

## Repayment capacity

Use complete existing and proposed debt schedules, eligible cash flow and consistent periods. Include balloon and variable-payment terms.

Sources: banking, accounting, lender, application, documents.

### dscr

**Cash-flow debt-service coverage** · `ratio` · business

Eligible cash flow available for debt service divided by existing plus proposed contractual debt service.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete eligible cash-flow bridge and existing/proposed principal, interest and mandatory debt fees for the same period; positive debt service.

Calculation version: `amlok-dscr-1`.

### fixed_charge_coverage

**Fixed-charge coverage** · `ratio` · business

Cash flow before the specified fixed charges divided by those fixed charges.

Required inputs and conditions: Explicit non-overlapping charge set, including leases where applicable; positive charges.

Calculation version: `amlok-fixed_charge_coverage-1`.

### interest_coverage

**Interest coverage** · `ratio` · business

EBIT divided by interest expense.

Required inputs and conditions: Supported same-period EBIT and positive interest expense.

Calculation version: `amlok-interest_coverage-1`.

### existing_debt_service

**Existing debt service** · `currency` · business

Sum existing contractual principal, interest and mandatory debt fees over the assessment window.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete schedule; variable and balloon assumptions explicit.

Calculation version: `amlok-existing_debt_service-1`.

### proposed_debt_service

**Proposed debt service** · `currency` · business

Sum proposed contractual principal, interest and mandatory debt fees over the assessment window.

Required inputs and conditions: Exact proposed product terms, amount, rate, amortization, fees and timing.

Calculation version: `amlok-proposed_debt_service-1`.

### total_debt_service

**Total debt service** · `currency` · business

Existing plus proposed debt service with duplicates removed.

Required inputs and conditions: Comparable complete schedules and shared currency.

Calculation version: `amlok-total_debt_service-1`.

### residual_cash_after_debt

**Cash after debt service** · `currency` · business

Eligible cash available for debt service minus total debt service.

Required inputs and conditions: Supported cash-flow bridge and complete obligations.

Calculation version: `amlok-residual_cash_after_debt-1`.

### debt_service_headroom

**Debt-service headroom** · `currency` · business

Eligible cash flow divided by institution DSCR requirement, minus total debt service.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive institution-approved DSCR threshold and complete components; analytical capacity, not an offer.

Calculation version: `amlok-debt_service_headroom-1`.

### debt_payment_to_receipts

**Debt payments to operating receipts** · `ratio` · business

Observed debt payments divided by operating cash receipts.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive receipts; payments classified with evidence; not a substitute for DSCR.

Calculation version: `amlok-debt_payment_to_receipts-1`.

Sources: accounting, payments, commerce, payroll, banking.

**Largest customer share** · `ratio` · business

Largest resolved customer revenue divided by covered customer revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive covered revenue and disclosed unknown-counterparty share.

Calculation version: `amlok-top_customer_share-1`.

### top_five_customer_share

**Top five customer share** · `ratio` · business

Revenue of five largest resolved customers divided by covered customer revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive covered revenue; fewer than five uses all resolved customers.

Calculation version: `amlok-top_five_customer_share-1`.

### top_supplier_share

**Largest supplier share** · `ratio` · business

Largest resolved supplier spend divided by covered supplier spend.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive supplier spend and explicit coverage.

Calculation version: `amlok-top_supplier_share-1`.

### top_channel_share

**Largest sales channel share** · `ratio` · business

Largest channel revenue divided by total covered channel revenue.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive channel revenue; no duplicated cross-platform sales.

Calculation version: `amlok-top_channel_share-1`.

### customer_hhi

**Customer concentration index** · `ratio` · business

Sum squared customer revenue fractions on a 0-to-1 scale.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete resolved customer allocation or explicit partial coverage; unknown is not one customer.

Calculation version: `amlok-customer_hhi-1`.

### income_source_hhi

**Income-source concentration index** · `ratio` · person

Sum squared verified income-source fractions on a 0-to-1 scale.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive verified income and resolved income streams.

Calculation version: `amlok-income_source_hhi-1`.

Sources: collateral, registry, lender, accounting, documents.

### collateral_ltv

**Loan to collateral value** · `ratio` · business, person

Required inputs and conditions: Positive eligible valuation with valuation date, currency and ownership.

Calculation version: `amlok-collateral_ltv-1`.

### combined_ltv

**Combined loan to value** · `ratio` · business, person

All known secured claims including proposed exposure divided by collateral value.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive valuation and complete supported lien/priority inventory.

Calculation version: `amlok-combined_ltv-1`.

### collateral_coverage

**Collateral coverage** · `ratio` · business, person

Eligible realizable collateral value after approved haircuts divided by secured exposure.

Calculation version: `amlok-collateral_coverage-1`.

### eligible_receivables

**Eligible borrowing-base receivables** · `currency` · business

Sum receivables meeting institution eligibility and concentration rules.

Calculation version: `amlok-eligible_receivables-1`.

### collateral_haircut_ratio

**Collateral haircut** · `ratio` · business, person

Gross eligible valuation minus supported realizable valuation, divided by gross valuation.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive valuation and disclosed approved valuation/haircut method.

Calculation version: `amlok-collateral_haircut_ratio-1`.

Sources: documents, banking, accounting, payments, identity.

### duplicate_transaction_share

**Duplicate transaction share** · `ratio` · business, person

Calculation version: `amlok-duplicate_transaction_share-1`.

### document_contradiction_count

**Document contradictions** · `count` · business, person

Count distinct supported inconsistencies between comparable document fields.

### identity_mismatch_count

### declared_income_gap_ratio

Calculation version: `amlok-declared_income_gap_ratio-1`.

## Income and affordability

Distinguish verified gross income, net deposits and modeled estimates. Include housing, essential spending, obligations and proposed payments without counting them twice.

Sources: payroll, banking, tax, credit_bureau, application, documents, lender.

### verified_gross_monthly_income

**Verified gross monthly income** · `currency` · person

Supported recurring pretax income normalized to one calendar month.

Required inputs and conditions: Payroll, tax or supported gross-income evidence; net deposits alone do not establish gross income.

Calculation version: `amlok-verified_gross_monthly_income-1`.

### verified_net_monthly_income

**Verified net monthly income** · `currency` · person

Supported recurring after-deduction income normalized to one calendar month.

Required inputs and conditions: Verified income streams, recurrence and calendar conversion; transfers excluded.

Calculation version: `amlok-verified_net_monthly_income-1`.

### debt_to_income

**Debt to income** · `ratio` · person

Existing plus proposed required monthly debt payments divided by verified gross monthly income.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive verified gross income and complete monthly obligations; housing treatment explicit.

Calculation version: `amlok-debt_to_income-1`.

### housing_to_income

**Housing cost to income** · `ratio` · person

Required monthly housing cost divided by verified gross monthly income.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive gross income; rent/mortgage/tax/insurance/components explicit.

Calculation version: `amlok-housing_to_income-1`.

### essential_monthly_spend

**Essential monthly expenditure** · `currency` · person

Sum evidenced essential non-debt spending normalized to one month.

Required inputs and conditions: Versioned institution expense taxonomy; exclude sensitive merchant categories and avoid housing/debt duplication.

Calculation version: `amlok-essential_monthly_spend-1`.

### residual_monthly_income

**Residual monthly income** · `currency` · person

Verified net income minus essential spending, housing and existing/proposed debt payments.

Required inputs and conditions: Complete non-overlapping monthly expense/obligation set.

Calculation version: `amlok-residual_monthly_income-1`.

### payment_to_income

**Proposed payment to income** · `ratio` · person

Proposed required monthly payment divided by verified gross monthly income.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive verified gross income and exact proposed terms.

Calculation version: `amlok-payment_to_income-1`.

### liquid_reserve_months

**Liquid reserve months** · `months` · person

Unrestricted liquid funds divided by required monthly outgoings.

Required inputs and conditions: Positive complete essential/housing/debt outgoings; verified liquid balances.

Calculation version: `amlok-liquid_reserve_months-1`.
