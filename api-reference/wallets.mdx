---
title: "Wallet lifecycle"
description: "Fund prepaid service credits, reconcile product usage, and refund the remaining balance on final agreement cancellation."
---

The wallet is a **prepaid AMLOK service-credit wallet**. Paid funds are consumed by AMLOK products or returned as a full remaining-balance refund after final agreement cancellation. There are no on-demand withdrawal or wallet-transfer operations.

The [billing account](/api-reference/endpoints/billing/retrieve-billing-account) exposes `outflow_policy: product_usage_or_final_cancellation_refund` and `refund_policy: full_remaining_paid_balance_on_final_cancellation`.

## Balances and funding

| Balance | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Committed credits | Unused credits funded by the minimum monthly commitment paid annually. |
| Additional funds | Other purchased credits and settled top-ups, with the same usage and cancellation-refund policy. |
| Promotional credits | Non-cash benefits for eligible products. They have no paid principal to refund. |
| Pending incoming | Payments that have not settled. They are excluded from posted and usable balances. |
| Held | Credits reserved for unresolved usage or an existing cancellation refund. A hold is not a second debit. |
| Available for usage | Posted credits less holds, usable only while services remain active. |
| Remaining paid credits | Paid principal still in the wallet, including unresolved paid-credit holds. This is not an amount available for withdrawal. |

Read the wallet's `agreement_id`, status, balance, credit lots, and ledger together. A cancelled agreement cannot create another wallet to resume service access.

1. Create a [hosted payment session](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/create-wallet-payment-session) to authorize a funding method. Never send raw bank credentials or card data to this API.
2. Retrieve the verified payment method and submit a [top-up](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/create-wallet-top-up) with a durable `client_reference` and idempotency key.
3. Treat `pending` and `requires_action` as unsettled. A redirect or successful HTTP response does not prove payment.
4. Use credits only after confirmed settlement. Funding lineage remains available for reconciliation and the eventual cancellation refund.

Cancelling an unsubmitted top-up stops the payment attempt. It does not allow a refund of credits that have already settled while the agreement is active.

## Automatic top-ups and payment methods

Read or update the [automatic top-up policy](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/update-automatic-top-up-policy) to change `funding_method_id`, `top_up_amount`, threshold, and monthly cap under the current authorization. Use `If-Match` to avoid overwriting a concurrent change.

Changes apply to future attempts. They do not rewrite submitted payments or remove funds from the wallet. Only one automatic attempt may be pending per wallet; failed payments do not trigger an unlimited retry loop. Automatic funding and new usage stop at the confirmed cancellation date.

## Final cancellation

1. [Quote cancellation](/api-reference/endpoints/billing/create-agreement-cancellation-quote) for the requested effective date. The estimate shows final usage, expected remaining paid credits, and separate agreement obligations.
2. [Request cancellation](/api-reference/endpoints/billing/create-agreement-cancellation) using the current quote. Notice requirements determine the confirmed effective date.
3. At that date, stop new usage and automatic funding. Resolve incurred usage, holds, disputes, and pending funding without treating a hold as a second charge.
4. The cancellation enters `refund_pending` after final reconciliation, with `reconciled_at`, `final_statement_id`, and `final_refund_amount` populated.
5. AMLOK returns the full remaining paid balance. The cancellation links its refund records and tracks `refunded_amount`. The obligation exists even if the customer does not submit a separate API refund request.
6. Mark cancellation `completed` only after the entire remaining paid balance has been returned, or reconciliation confirms that no paid balance remains. A later funding settlement or returned payout reopens refund reconciliation without restoring service access.

Pending usage in a cancellation estimate is counted once. Remaining agreement obligations are disclosed separately; they are not deducted from the unused paid-balance refund. Promotional benefits have no refundable cash principal.

## Quote and submit the cancellation refund

A [wallet refund quote](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/create-wallet-movement-quote) requires the same institution, legal owner, wallet, and cancelled agreement. The cancellation must be effective, finally reconciled, and in `refund_pending`. An active, merely requested, or scheduled cancellation returns `409 cancellation_not_final`.

```json
{
  "kind": "refund",
  "source_wallet_id": "wal_example",
  "cancellation_id": "cancellation_final_example",
  "currency": "CAD"
}
```

The server calculates the full remaining paid balance and allocations to original funding sources. The request does not accept a client-selected `amount`, partial refund, or transfer destination. A verified same-owner `payout_destination_id` may be supplied only when an original source cannot receive the refund.

The quote binds the cancellation, final statement revision, wallet version, amount, and destinations. It does not reserve funds. Submit its ID to [create the wallet refund](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/create-wallet-refund):

```json
{
  "quote_id": "wallet_quote_refund",
  "client_reference": "cancellation-refund-1001"
}
```

Submission rechecks eligibility and reserves the full unpaid amount atomically. There is one refund obligation per cancellation and wallet, including all original funding sources. Duplicate business requests return the existing refund or `409 refund_already_in_progress`; a new idempotency key cannot create a second entitlement.

The refund has no wallet fee deduction: `amount`, `total_debit`, and `recipient_amount` match. Allocations sum to that amount. `paid_amount` and `remaining_amount` track delivery across any multiple payouts.

## Delivery, failures, and retries

| Refund status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `requires_approval` | Payment instructions need the configured independent approval. The refund remains owed. |
| `pending` | Delivery is authorized and waiting for submission. |
| `submitted` | The payment route is processing the refund. Do not start a duplicate payment. |
| `partially_paid` | Some allocations have been delivered; the remainder is still owed. |
| `paid` | All allocations have been delivered. |
| `failed` / `returned` | Delivery failed definitively or funds were returned. Reconcile the unpaid amount before retrying. |
| `cancelled` | A delivery attempt was stopped before submission. This does not cancel the refund obligation. |

Read [wallet payouts](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/list-wallet-payouts) for individual payment references. These are cancellation-refund payments; `/v1/payouts` separately describes payment-provider data.

To [retry a refund](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/retry-wallet-refund), first confirm every previous payout outcome. Request a fresh quote with the same cancellation and `refund_id`; its amount is the reconciled unpaid balance. Submit the quote with `If-Match`, a new business reference for the retry, and an idempotency key that stays stable across retries of that command. Never resend principal already paid.

A timeout or unknown bank outcome is not a definitive failure and returns `409 refund_not_retryable` if a retry is attempted. A changed destination requires verification and any configured approval. Returned funds are credited once to the closing wallet and remain owed under the same cancellation; they are not usable for new products.

## Authorization and reconciliation

- Institution and legal-owner checks apply to every linked resource, including cancellation, statement, funding origin, refund, and destination.
- Keep billing read, export, wallet read, funding, refund delivery, and approval scopes separate.
- Independent delivery approval is tied to the exact refund version. It cannot authorize an active-agreement cash-out or reject the obligation to refund the remaining balance.
- Use a durable `client_reference` beyond request-idempotency retention. Look up the existing resource after a timeout.
- Use `If-Match` for policy changes, approvals, cancellations, and refund retries. Retrieve the current version after a `412`.
- Ledger corrections and payment reversals are system reconciliation entries, not customer-controlled cash-out operations.

Consume [wallet and billing events](/api-reference/event-catalog) at least once and potentially out of order. Deduplicate by event ID, compare versions, and retrieve the current resource. Reconcile usage and statements through [consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting).

## Close the wallet

[Wallet closure](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/create-wallet-closure) requires the final cancellation ID, completed refunds, and resolved funding and holds. Closure cannot trigger a refund for an active agreement. Retained billing and audit records remain available under the applicable retention policy.
