The AMLOK wallet holds prepaid service credits used by AMLOK products. The customer API supports **balance and ledger reads, funding-method setup, top-ups, and automatic top-up settings**. [Reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting) explains usage, rates, statements, and exports.

Agreement changes, cancellation, account closure, and the resulting refund are handled by your account manager. There are no customer API operations for transfers, withdrawals, refund requests, payouts, wallet approvals, or account closure.

## Read balances and activity

Wallets are provisioned for your institution through the platform. [List wallets](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/list-wallets) or retrieve a known wallet, then read its balance and ledger.

| Balance | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Committed credits | Unused paid credits funded by your monthly minimum commitment, paid annually. |
| Additional funds | Settled top-ups, available for eligible product usage. |
| Promotional credits | Non-cash benefits, including applicable SLA service credits. No paid principal is refundable from this bucket. |
| Pending incoming | Funding that has not settled and is not yet available for usage. |
| Held | Credits reserved for unresolved activity. A hold is not a second debit. |
| Available for usage | Posted credits less holds, usable only while services remain active. |
| Remaining paid credits | Paid principal remaining in the wallet, including unresolved paid-credit holds. This is not a withdrawable balance. |

Credit lots link balances to their funding origin. Ledger entries record usage, funding, system corrections, and any final-cancellation refund administered by AMLOK. Customer integrations cannot post arbitrary usage charges or ledger adjustments.

## Set up a funding method

1. Create a [hosted funding session](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/create-wallet-payment-session) with `purpose: funding_method` and your allowlisted return URL.
2. Complete authorization in the hosted flow. Never send raw bank credentials or card data to the AMLOK API.
3. Retrieve the funding method and verify that it is authorized for the same legal entity, has `funding` capability, and is in `verified` status. A browser return alone does not prove verification.

These methods fund wallet top-ups. They are not customer-managed payout destinations. A method cannot be disabled while a pending payment or active automatic top-up policy still requires it; update the policy and resolve pending payments first.

## Purchase credits

Send a [top-up](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/create-wallet-top-up) using the verified funding method, exact CAD amount, a durable `client_reference`, and a stable `Idempotency-Key`.

```json
{
  "wallet_id": "wal_example",
  "funding_method_id": "wallet_payment_method_example",
  "amount": "1000.00",
  "currency": "CAD",
  "client_reference": "top-up-1001"
}
```

A `202` response accepts the payment instruction; it does not confirm settlement. Retrieve the existing top-up or consume `wallet.top_up.updated`. Only settled funding becomes usable credit. A failed or reversed payment is reconciled against its original funding reference.

After a timeout, look up the original payment by `client_reference` before trying again. Do not start a new payment with a different reference while its outcome is unknown. Funding is rejected after account closure.

## Automatic top-ups

Read or update the [automatic top-up policy](/api-reference/endpoints/wallets/update-automatic-top-up-policy) to change the payment method, top-up amount, trigger balance, and monthly cap under the current funding authorization. Include `If-Match` and an idempotency key when updating the policy.

Changes apply to future attempts. They do not modify already submitted payments. Only one automatic attempt can be pending per wallet, and failures do not trigger an unlimited retry loop. The top-up amount cannot exceed the monthly cap. Automatic funding stops when the agreement ends.

Automatic top-ups purchase credits. They do not enable automatic withdrawals, payouts, or transfers.

## Permissions and recovery

`wallets:read` permits authorized reads. `wallets:funding` permits funding-method and top-up actions. Tenant, legal-owner, funding authorization, and capability checks still apply. Reporting access does not authorize funding.

Use stable business references beyond request-idempotency retention. Read the current record after `412` before retrying a policy update. Deduplicate events and retrieve the current resource because notifications may arrive more than once or out of order.

## Cancellation and remaining credits

Contact your account manager for final cancellation. AMLOK reconciles final usage and pending funding, verifies refund instructions, closes the account, and **immediately issues the full remaining paid-balance refund**. Funds may take **7-10 business days** to reach the bank account, depending on the bank.

This is an account-management process; no customer API refund or payout request is needed or supported. Your account manager provides the refund reference and delivery updates. The wallet cannot be withdrawn while the agreement is active. See [refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts) and [account management](/support/account-management).
