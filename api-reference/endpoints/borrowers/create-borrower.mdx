---
title: "Create borrower"
description: "Register a business or consumer borrower. Registration records institution-supplied identity; it does not assert successful KYB or KYC."
openapi: "/openapi/amlok.json POST /v1/borrowers"
---

Register a reusable business or person identity before collection, underwriting or monitoring. Registration records institution-supplied identity and creates no product assessment charge; it does not assert KYB/KYC or product enrollment. Reuse stable client_reference within borrower type/workspace/environment. Required scope: borrowers:write.

Examples use synthetic IDs. Replace them with values returned by your environment.
