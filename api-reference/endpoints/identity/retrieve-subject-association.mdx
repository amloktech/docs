title: "Retrieve subject association"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/subject-associations/{id}"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `identity:read`

Associate an existing person with a business in this workspace using permitted evidence and recorded authorization. This is a client-declared association, not a verified legal ownership determination. AMLOK validates subject ownership, the authorization and supporting evidence; status becomes active automatically when checks pass, or remains pending for platform review. Rejected records expose reason_code. An active association authorizes only related-person verification within an agreed parent product workflow. It does not grant broader data access. Canonical verified relationships remain read-only evidence. Review, corrections and revocation occur in the portal; create a new association for corrected evidence. Completed assessments retain their historical association version.
