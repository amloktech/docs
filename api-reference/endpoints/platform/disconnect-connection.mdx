---
title: "Disconnect connection"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/connections/{id}/disconnect"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `connections:write` · **Execution:** synchronous.

Stops future collection; not a full evidence deletion command. Once account cancellation is submitted, processing and usage deductions stop immediately. Reject new operational commands with 409 invalid_state; pending work is cancelled and unused holds are released without another debit. Read-only reporting remains available until workspace deletion.
