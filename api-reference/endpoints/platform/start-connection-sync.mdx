**Required scope:** `connections:write` · **Execution:** asynchronous.

Poll GET /v1/sync-jobs/{id}; may complete partially. Once account cancellation is submitted, processing and usage deductions stop immediately. Reject new operational commands with 409 invalid_state; pending work is cancelled and unused holds are released without another debit. Read-only reporting remains available until workspace deletion.

[Data dictionary](/data-model/field-dictionary#connection) · [Response conventions](/api-reference/response-format) · [Errors](/api-reference/errors)

Poll `GET /v1/sync-jobs/{id}` with the returned resource ID, or consume the relevant webhook. A successful submission does not mean the work completed.

**Lifecycle event:** `sync.completed`. See [webhook delivery and verification](/api-reference/webhooks).
