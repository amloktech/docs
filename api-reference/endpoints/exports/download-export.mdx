title: "Download export"
description: "Download export."
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v2.json POST /v2/exports/{id}/download-links"
---

Download export. All IDs and cursors must belong to the authorized workspace and environment. Required scope: exports:read. Risk-package exports require results:read; billing exports require billing:read and Owner authority; evidence exports require evidence:read and source entitlements. Permissions are checked at creation, retrieval and download.

Examples use synthetic IDs. Replace them with values returned by your environment.
