---
title: "Get export"
description: "Get export."
openapi: "/openapi/amlok.json GET /v1/exports/{id}"
---

Get export. All IDs and cursors must belong to the authorized workspace and environment. Required scope: exports:read. Risk-package exports require results:read; billing exports require billing:read and Owner authority; evidence exports require evidence:read and source entitlements. Permissions are checked at creation, retrieval and download.

Examples use synthetic IDs. Replace them with values returned by your environment.
