title: "Calculate attributes"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/attribute-calculations"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `risk:calculate` · **Execution:** asynchronous.

GET /v1/attribute-calculations/{id} reports job state; values in /v1/attributes. Discover input_manifest_id through GET /v1/input-manifests. It must be ready and belong to the same subject and workspace. The calculation window must be contained within its frozen coverage.

Poll `GET /v1/attribute-calculations/{id}` with the returned resource ID, or consume the relevant webhook. A successful submission does not mean the work completed.

**Lifecycle event:** `attributes.calculated`. See [webhook delivery and verification](/api-reference/webhooks).
