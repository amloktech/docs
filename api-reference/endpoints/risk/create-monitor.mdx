**Required scope:** `monitoring:write`

Configure ongoing monitoring against a baseline.

Required scope: `monitoring:write`. Refresh frequency remains subject to provider and consent limits. Once account cancellation is submitted, processing and usage deductions stop immediately. Reject new operational commands with 409 invalid_state; pending work is cancelled without another debit. Read-only reporting remains available until workspace deletion. Discover approved baselines through GET /v1/monitoring-baselines. Supply its snapshot_id as baseline_snapshot_id; it must be ready and belong to this subject and workspace. Multiple monitors do not create multiple Signal account-month charges. Signal charges the full agreed monthly rate once per borrower/business/workspace at 00:00 UTC on the first day of the month, before monitoring proceeds. First activation during a month charges the full monthly rate immediately, without proration; the next charge is at the next month boundary. Repeated monitor creation, pauses, resumes, checks and reassessments do not add charges in an already paid period. Insufficient credits block monitoring without a debit or debt; funding resumes only the current period after its single successful charge, without charging missed inactive months. User-paused monitoring remains paused until the user resumes it. Account cancellation stops processing and all further deductions immediately.
