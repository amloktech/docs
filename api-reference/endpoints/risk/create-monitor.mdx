**Required scope:** `monitoring:write` · **Execution:** synchronous.

Refresh frequency remains subject to provider and consent limits. Discover approved baselines through GET /v1/monitoring-baselines. Supply its snapshot_id as baseline_snapshot_id; it must be ready and belong to this subject and workspace. Multiple monitors do not create multiple Signal account-month charges.
