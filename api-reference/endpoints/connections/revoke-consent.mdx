openapi: "/openapi/amlok.json POST /v1/consents/{id}/revoke"
---

Record withdrawal of authorization and stop future collection covered by that consent. Revocation does not edit historical assessments or serve as a data-deletion command. All IDs and cursors must belong to the authorized workspace and environment. Required scope: connections:write.

Examples use synthetic IDs. Replace them with values returned by your environment.
