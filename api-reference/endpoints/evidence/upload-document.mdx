Upload borrower evidence or documents supporting a lending product or risk policy. Upload creates a new record; it cannot overwrite an existing document. All IDs and cursors must belong to the authorized workspace and environment. Required scope: evidence:write.

Examples use synthetic IDs. Replace them with values returned by your environment.
