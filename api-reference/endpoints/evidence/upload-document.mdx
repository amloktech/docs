Upload borrower evidence or documents supporting a lending product or risk policy. Returns 202 with status processing; inspect GET /v1/documents/{id} until available or rejected. Wait for available before referencing initial document IDs in an origination. A borrower-only upload does not attach to an existing assessment. Each upload creates a new immutable file; it cannot overwrite a document or revise a completed result. All IDs and cursors must belong to the authorized workspace and environment. Required scope: evidence:write.

Examples use synthetic IDs. Replace them with values returned by your environment.
