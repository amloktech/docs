Retrieve an underwriting request, its immutable submitted application, product and policy versions, document associations, requirements, status and risk-package reference, including before processing completes. All IDs and cursors must belong to the authorized workspace and environment. Required scope: insights:read.

Examples use synthetic IDs. Replace them with values returned by your environment.
