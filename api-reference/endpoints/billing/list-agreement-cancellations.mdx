---
title: "List agreement cancellations"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/agreement-cancellations"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Track cancellation through requested, scheduled, effective, reconciling, refund_pending and completed. refund_pending requires a final statement and reconciled_at; completed requires every remaining paid balance returned, including late settled funding and returned payouts, or a zero final balance. Remaining term obligations are separately disclosed and are not deducted from the wallet refund.
