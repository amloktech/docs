---
title: "Retrieve billing account"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/billing/account"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Read the minimum monthly commitment, prepaid-service-credit policy, enabled funding capabilities, and cancellation-refund delivery approvals. Funds are consumed by AMLOK products or returned in full after final agreement cancellation; on-demand withdrawals and transfers are not supported.
