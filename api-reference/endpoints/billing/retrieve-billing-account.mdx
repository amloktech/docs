---
title: "Retrieve billing account"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/billing/account"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Read agreed pricing, the prepaid-service-credit policy and enabled funding capabilities. Agreement changes and final cancellation are handled by your account manager. The API cannot request refunds, payouts, transfers, approvals or account closure.
