openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/billing/account"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Read the unified workspace billing policy, CAD 200 hard processing floor, and CAD 500 minimum customer-funded top-up. The client portal manages card or bank-account payment methods, automatic top-ups and cancellation; enterprise clients can also use their dedicated account manager. The public API provides reporting only. Final cancellation deletes workspace data and immediately refunds the full unused balance, including promotional credits, to the saved portal bank account when the account closes. Used credits are non-refundable.
