openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/billing/account"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Read the unified workspace billing policy, CAD 200 default automatic top-up trigger, and CAD 500 minimum customer-funded top-up. The client portal manages card or bank-account payment methods, automatic top-ups and cancellation; enterprise clients can also use their dedicated account manager. The public API provides reporting only. Cancellation is available at any time with no notice period. Full closure is at the end of the request month, with an immediate refund of all unused credits, including promotional credits, to the saved portal bank account. Data is deleted after the month closes and necessary final information is generated. Used credits are non-refundable.
