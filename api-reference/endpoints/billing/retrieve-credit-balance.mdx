---
title: "Retrieve credit balance"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/billing/credits"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Read the authenticated institution billing account credit snapshot. Paid and promotional credits are separate. Pending incoming payments are excluded from usable credits. Holds reserve usage without a second debit. remaining_paid_credits is paid principal, not an on-demand withdrawal balance. This endpoint cannot change credits or payment arrangements.
