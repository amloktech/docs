---
title: "Create agreement cancellation quote"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/agreement-cancellation-quotes"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:manage`

Estimate unused paid credits, final usage and the full remaining-balance refund at the requested cancellation date. Pending usage holds are included in estimated final usage once, not deducted twice. Remaining agreement obligations are disclosed separately and do not become wallet deductions. A quote neither cancels services nor pays funds; reconciliation establishes the final amount.

[Wallet lifecycle](/api-reference/wallets) · [Consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting) · [Refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts)

Use an idempotency key for every mutation. Money commands also require a durable, institution-scoped business reference. A successful submission is not confirmation of settlement.
