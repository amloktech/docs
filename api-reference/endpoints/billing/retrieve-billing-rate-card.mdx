---
title: "Retrieve billing rate card"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/billing/rate-card"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Read published or negotiated rates and agreed workload limits for the workspace. Custom quotes retain standard, advanced or complex as the tier and use pricing_basis negotiated with the agreed effective_rate and workload_limits. Insights record_limit covers the complete underwriting snapshot, including at least 12 months of banking history. Signal record_limit covers only newly processed records during the month; each updated record version counts as new, while retries and unchanged baseline records are not counted again. The tier must cover every workload dimension; custom allowances require an agreed quote. Historical usage retains its own rate-card identifier and effective rate.

Required scope: billing:read. The API key is bound to exactly one workspace, institution and environment. There is no workspace override; this binding applies to every read and export.
