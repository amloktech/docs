title: "Retrieve agreement cancellation"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/agreement-cancellations/{id}"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Track cancellation through requested, scheduled, effective, reconciling, refund_pending and completed. refund_pending requires a final statement, reconciled_at and account_closed_at. The account is already closed and the refund is issued immediately after closure, while bank receipt may take 7-10 business days depending on the bank. completed describes financial reconciliation, not the account-closure date, and requires every remaining paid balance returned, including late settled funding and returned payouts, or a zero final balance. Remaining term obligations are separately disclosed and are not deducted from the wallet refund.
