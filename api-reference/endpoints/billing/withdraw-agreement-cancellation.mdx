---
title: "Withdraw agreement cancellation"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/agreement-cancellations/{id}/cancel"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:manage`

Withdraw a cancellation request before its effective boundary, subject to agreement terms. This does not reverse already stopped services or completed payments.
