---
title: "List credit ledger entries"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/billing/credit-ledger"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `billing:read`

Read posted credit additions, product consumption, reconciliation adjustments and final-cancellation refunds. Entries are immutable and returned in ascending sequence order at a stable snapshot. The customer cannot create or change ledger entries. Read a statement or export for period reconciliation.

Required scope: billing:read. The API key is bound to exactly one workspace, institution and environment. There is no workspace override; this binding applies to every read and export.
