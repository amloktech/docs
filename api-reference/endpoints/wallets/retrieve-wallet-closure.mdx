---
title: "Retrieve wallet closure"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/wallet-closures/{id}"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `wallets:read`

Read closure blockers, final statement, closed_at and linked refund_ids. A completed closure can have an issued refund still in bank processing; closure does not imply funds have arrived. A blocked closure does not forfeit funds.
