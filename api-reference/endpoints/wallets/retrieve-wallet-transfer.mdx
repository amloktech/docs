---
title: "Retrieve wallet transfer"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/wallet-transfers/{id}"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `wallets:read`

Move available funds between distinct wallets of the same institution AND legal owner, in CAD. Debit and credit are atomic and preserve origin, commitment classification and refund restrictions. No transfer to another customer.
