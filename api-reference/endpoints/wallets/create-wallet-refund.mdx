**Required scope:** `wallets:refund`

Prepare the full reconciled remaining paid-balance refund only after final agreement cancellation. Complete destination verification and delivery approval before account closure. AMLOK issues the refund automatically and immediately after account closure, without requiring a separate customer API call. Funds may take 7-10 business days to reach the bank account, depending on the bank. account_closed_at records closure; issued_at records issuance, not bank receipt. Preparation can return requires_approval or pending with null timestamps; issuance requires account_closed_at and sets issued_at. Require a valid cancellation-refund quote; recheck its cancellation state, effective date, statement revision, wallet version, verified same-owner destinations and settled funding. Reserve the complete unpaid balance atomically, with no wallet deduction for refund fees. One refund obligation exists per cancellation and wallet, across all funding sources; a duplicate request returns the existing refund or 409 refund_already_in_progress. No caller-selected partial amount, discretionary eligibility rejection, active-agreement refund, withdrawal or transfer is supported. Delivery approval validates instructions and cannot waive the obligation to refund. AMLOK owes the remaining balance on final cancellation even if the client does not call this endpoint. A retry preserves the original issued_at, records each new payout submission separately, and cannot reopen the account.
