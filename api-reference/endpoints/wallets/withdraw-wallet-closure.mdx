---
title: "Withdraw wallet closure"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/wallet-closures/{id}/cancel"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `wallets:write`

Withdraw a closure before completion. Final agreement cancellation and the remaining-balance refund obligation still apply; AMLOK completes the account-closure and refund process. This does not re-enable service access or an automatic funding mandate.

[Wallet lifecycle](/api-reference/wallets) · [Consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting) · [Refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts)

Use an idempotency key for every mutation. Money commands also require a durable, institution-scoped business reference. A successful submission is not confirmation of settlement.
