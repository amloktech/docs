---
title: "Cancel wallet top-up"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/wallet-top-ups/{id}/cancel"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `wallets:funding`

Cancel an unsettled funding attempt only before irreversible processor submission. Once settled, the credits remain for product usage and can only be refunded through final agreement cancellation. A failed or reversed funding payment is reconciled without treating it as a customer withdrawal.

[Wallet lifecycle](/api-reference/wallets) · [Consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting) · [Refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts)

Use an idempotency key for every mutation. Money commands also require a durable, institution-scoped business reference. A successful submission is not confirmation of settlement.
