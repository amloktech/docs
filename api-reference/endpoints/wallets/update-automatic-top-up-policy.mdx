---
title: "Update automatic top-up policy"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json PATCH /v1/wallets/{id}/auto-top-up"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `wallets:funding`

Replace the policy with a current ETag and an institution-approved authorization. Amount cannot exceed cap. One pending automatic attempt per wallet; failed attempts do not loop indefinitely. The confirmed agreement end date disables automatic funding and new usage. Changing funding_method_id and top_up_amount updates future automatic attempts, within the cap and current authorization; it does not modify already submitted payments.

Use an idempotency key for every mutation. Money commands also require a durable, institution-scoped business reference. A successful submission is not confirmation of settlement.
