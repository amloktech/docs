openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/wallet-closures"
playground: simple

**Required scope:** `wallets:write`

Close only after final agreement cancellation, full remaining paid-balance refund and resolution of holds and in-flight funding. Require the same cancellation_id and legal owner. Zero-cost promotional credits are retired without cash payment. Closure cannot be used as an alternative cash-out path, does not delete audit records, and never re-enables funding or usage. A later payout return reopens refund reconciliation, not service access.

[Wallet lifecycle](/api-reference/wallets) · [Consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting) · [Refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts)

Use an idempotency key for every mutation. Money commands also require a durable, institution-scoped business reference. A successful submission is not confirmation of settlement.
