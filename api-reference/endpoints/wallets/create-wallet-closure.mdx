**Required scope:** `wallets:write`

Close only after final agreement cancellation, final usage reconciliation, resolved funding and usage holds, and verified and approved refund instructions. Require the same cancellation_id and legal owner. Reserve the full reconciled paid balance for its cancellation refund; that reserved balance and subsequent bank processing do not block account closure. Complete account closure and immediately issue the refund automatically. Record closed_at and link refund_ids. Funds may take 7-10 business days to reach the bank account, depending on the bank. Closure and refund issuance must be durably linked so a retry cannot lose the refund obligation or issue a duplicate. A closed wallet permits refund delivery and reconciliation only, never new funding or usage. Zero-cost promotional credits are retired without cash payment. Closure cannot be used as an alternative cash-out path, does not delete audit records, and never re-enables funding or usage. A later payout return reopens refund reconciliation, not service access.
