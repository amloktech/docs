---
title: "Retrieve wallet refund"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json GET /v1/wallet-refunds/{id}"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `wallets:read`

Request an agreement-based refund to the original funding method. Requested amounts cannot exceed remaining funding principal after usage, withdrawals, prior refunds, transfers and holds. Refund approval is distinct from the independent customer approval workflow.
