title: "Create wallet"
openapi: "/openapi/amlok-v1.json POST /v1/wallets"
playground: simple
---

**Required scope:** `wallets:write`

Create a prepaid CAD service-credit wallet under the authenticated institution, onboarded legal owner and active billing agreement. The client cannot supply another tenant, owner or arbitrary agreement. A cancelled agreement cannot create a wallet to resume usage.

[Wallet lifecycle](/api-reference/wallets) · [Consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting) · [Refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts)

Use an idempotency key for every mutation. Money commands also require a durable, institution-scoped business reference. A successful submission is not confirmation of settlement.
