These definitions describe `risk_package.verification` and the same assessment at `normalized.assessment` when `normalized.object` is `verification_assessment`. The [KYC and KYB guide](/api-reference/verification) explains retrieval, status, evidence and authorization. Every section is present even when no result is available.

All values in the [business example](/downloads/verification-business-example.json) and [consumer example](/downloads/verification-consumer-example.json) are synthetic. The [machine-readable dictionary](/downloads/verification-field-catalog.json) uses the exact field IDs imported by console Data Lookup.

## VerificationCheck

Provider and observation context remain visible. Inconclusive, not checked and no matching record must never become a verified pass.

### VerificationCheck-id

`id` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

Id. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

### VerificationCheck-check

`check` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

Check. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

Allowed values: `identity`, `account_ownership`, `business_registration`, `beneficial_ownership`, `document_authenticity`, `income_verification`, `credit_report_match`, `lien`, `tax_filing`.

### VerificationCheck-subject_id

`subject_id` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

Subject id. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

### VerificationCheck-result

`result` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

Result. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

Allowed values: `matched`, `not_matched`, `inconclusive`, `not_checked`.

### VerificationCheck-provider

`provider` · `string | null` · Required · Nullable: Yes

Provider. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

### VerificationCheck-observed_at

`observed_at` · `string | null` · Required · Nullable: Yes

Observed at. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

### VerificationCheck-explanation

`explanation` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

Explanation. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

### VerificationCheck-evidence

`evidence` · `EvidenceLocation[]` · Required · Nullable: No

Exact evidence records and source-document locations.

## VerificationCheckSection

Coverage and results for verifications. Empty unavailable/not_applicable sections carry an explicit reason. Partial results must identify their limitation; an empty list alone never means a clean assessment.

### VerificationCheckSection-status

`status` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

State of this specific result; separate from entity legal status and lending decisions.

Allowed values: `available`, `partial`, `unavailable`, `not_applicable`.

### VerificationCheckSection-reason

`reason` · `string | null` · Required · Nullable: Yes

Explicit explanation code when no complete usable value is available.

### VerificationCheckSection-evidence_ids

`evidence_ids` · `string[]` · Required · Nullable: No

Evidence ids. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

### VerificationCheckSection-items

`items` · `VerificationCheck[]` · Required · Nullable: No

All authorized observations in this section, including distinct source observations.

## VerificationProvenance

Preserve conflicting source observations as separate items. Field evidence overrides item evidence only for the named path.

### VerificationProvenance-basis

`basis` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

How this observation was established. A declaration or extracted text is not verification.

Allowed values: `registry`, `provider_verified`, `document_extracted`, `declared`, `derived`.

### VerificationProvenance-source_id

`source_id` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

Opaque source identifier from assessment.sources, authorized for this workspace.

### VerificationProvenance-observed_at

`observed_at` · `string | null` · Required · Nullable: Yes

Time the source observed the fact, null when unknown.

### VerificationProvenance-retrieved_at

`retrieved_at` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

UTC time AMLOK retrieved this source version.

Format: `date-time`.

### VerificationProvenance-evidence

`evidence` · `VerificationEvidence[]` · Required · Nullable: No

Exact records or document pages supporting the observation; at least one.

### VerificationProvenance-field_evidence

`field_evidence` · `object[]` · Required · Nullable: No

Field-specific provenance for values that differ from the item-level source.

### VerificationProvenance-field_evidence-path

`field_evidence[].path` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

RFC 6901 pointer relative to this item for a field with different provenance.

### VerificationProvenance-field_evidence-basis

`field_evidence[].basis` · `string` · Required · Nullable: No

Basis. Preserve the source meaning; null or unknown does not establish a negative or a verified result.

Allowed values: `registry`, `provider_verified`, `document_extracted`, `declared`, `derived`.

### VerificationProvenance-field_evidence-evidence

`field_evidence[].evidence` · `VerificationEvidence[]` · Required · Nullable: No

Exact evidence records and source-document locations.
