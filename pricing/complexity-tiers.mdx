Complexity tiers reflect the work required to process your data. They apply separately to Insights and Signal, so an account can have one underwriting tier and a different monitoring tier.

## The three tiers

| Dimension | Standard | Advanced | Complex |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | Routine, consistent evidence | Larger or less consistent evidence sets | Extensive evidence with substantial investigation |
| People and entities | A limited borrower and ownership structure | Multiple income sources or related entities | Extensive ownership and related-party relationships |
| Reconciliation | Routine matching across the agreed sources | More discrepancies and cross-system analysis | High-volume reconciliation and extensive exception analysis |
| Analysis | Standard financial and risk assessment | Expanded financial, tax, or source-of-funds analysis | Investigation-heavy analysis and detailed reporting |
| Monitoring | Routine incremental activity | Higher volumes or deeper reassessment | Large ongoing workloads and complex relationships |

The number of documents is one input. History length, record volume, evidence quality, relationships, and the requested output also matter. A small file can require substantial analysis, while a large structured dataset can be straightforward to process.

## Illustrative monitoring profiles

The following business-monitoring examples show how the recurring workload can grow. Counts refer to source bank accounts and new bank transactions feeding **one monitored portfolio account**.

| Workload input | Standard example | Advanced example | Complex example |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Connected bank accounts | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| New bank transactions / month | 1,500 | 4,000 | 12,000 |
| Analysis scope | Routine synthesis | Deeper reconciliation and reassessment | Extensive ongoing analysis |

Consumer portfolios and other source types use their own agreed workload profiles. These examples do not guarantee a monitoring frequency, data refresh interval, or universal included volume.

## Set the limits in your order form

Specify the limits that apply to each contracted workflow:

| Input | What to agree |
| --- | --- |
| Application scope | Borrowers, businesses, guarantors, related entities, and facilities. |
| Evidence volume | Documents, pages, source accounts, records, and historical lookback. |
| Analysis depth | Reconciliation, investigations, scenarios, and required outputs. |
| Monitoring scope | Active account definition, recurring volumes, cadence, and reassessment coverage. |
| Out-of-scope work | Approval process, applicable rate, and when a new underwriting is required. |

Agree the tier before billable work begins. If several dimensions exceed the scope, assess the workflow as a whole and agree the appropriate tier or custom price. There is no automatic surcharge based on internal model calls or retries.

## Included processing and separate provider fees

The AMLOK rate covers the contracted AMLOK analysis and processing. It does not bundle third-party bank connectivity, bureau reports, identity or business verification, document-provider services, or specialized property and vehicle data.

Any provider charges, payment arrangements, or pass-through treatment must be identified separately in your order form. Excluding these fees from AMLOK's tier rates does not make those services free.
