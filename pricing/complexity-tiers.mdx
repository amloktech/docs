Complexity tiers reflect the full borrower-permissioned workload: connected bank accounts, connected business systems, total records analyzed, and analysis scope. They apply separately to Insights and Signal, so an account can have one underwriting tier and a different monitoring tier.

## Compare the tiers

Each tier covers a combined workload across banking and the business systems your borrower authorizes you to access.

| Workload dimension | Standard | Advanced | Complex |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Connected bank accounts | 2 | 4 | 8 |
| Business integrations, in addition to banking | 5 | 10 | 20 |
| Total new records analyzed / month | 75,000 | 250,000 | 1,000,000 |
| Analysis scope | Routine reconciliation and standard analysis | Multiple entities or channels and deeper reconciliation | Extensive relationships, exceptions, and investigation |

**Record volumes include all connected sources**, such as bank transactions, payment records, commerce orders, accounting records, and separately analyzed line items. The integration counts exclude banking connections.

These are tier workload profiles. Your order form confirms the applicable source connections, record allowances, historical lookback, and analysis scope. Evaluate the full workload: evidence quality, related entities, document volume, and reconciliation depth also matter. A high-volume dataset with consistent evidence can require less analysis than a smaller dataset with substantial exceptions.

The monthly volumes describe new activity for monitoring. Underwriting covers the historical snapshot agreed in your order form.
