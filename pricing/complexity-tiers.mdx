Amlok assesses the complexity of the borrower and the analysis required. **Standard, Advanced and Complex are analysis-complexity tiers. They are not volume bands.** More API calls, accounts or records do not automatically select a more expensive tier. Approval, decline, credit score and requested loan amount do not determine the price by themselves.

## Published rates

| Complexity | Insights / completed underwriting | Signal / active borrower-month |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Standard | CAD 8.00 | CAD 5.00 |
| Advanced | CAD 25.00 | CAD 8.00 |
| Complex | CAD 45.00 | CAD 12.00 |

Each workspace has six agreed rates. Your negotiated rates replace these defaults independently for each product and tier. Your monthly minimum and quarterly or annual funding schedule are separate agreement terms; neither selects the analysis tier.

## What the assessment considers

| Tier | Illustrative analysis |
| --- | --- |
| Standard | Straightforward operating finances and routine reconciliation for a domestic entity. |
| Advanced | Specialized revenue recognition, asset finance, or operating structures that require additional reconciliation. |
| Complex | Interdependent entities, cross-border obligations, inventory and work-in-progress, or substantial exceptions requiring deeper analysis. |

These examples explain the distinction, not a customer-side classification formula. Amlok evaluates the required analysis and returns the assessed tier and rationale. Insights and Signal may differ when their analysis differs. Clients do not submit a price or tier in an underwriting or monitoring request.

## How the assessment becomes a charge

1. Amlok assesses complexity and records its rationale and assessment-policy version.
2. Insights uses the workspace rate in effect when the successful output and charge are published together.
3. Signal uses one assessed tier and agreed rate for each borrower/workspace/UTC calendar month. The first successful evaluation in that month publishes the monthly charge with its output. Further checks, monitors and reassessments reuse that charge.
4. Each charge retains its historical tier, rate-card ID and agreement version. Later rate changes do not rewrite it. Reassessed Signal complexity or changed rates apply to the next new monthly charge.

Read `complexity_assessment` and `tier` on a [billing usage record](/api-reference/endpoints/billing/get-billing-usage). `unit_rate × quantity = amount` is the positive cost; its linked wallet transaction carries the negative debit. Older records may not include the newer assessment metadata. Missing metadata does not mean Standard.

## Operational capacity is separate

[Workload reports](/api-reference/workload-reporting) show connected accounts, systems and processed records. Those counts explain capacity, not price. The current default capacity is 8 bank accounts, 20 business integrations and 1,000,000 records, independently of complexity. Read the effective rate card's `workload_limits` for the applicable capacity.

Insights counts records in the complete underwriting evidence set. Signal counts new or updated versions processed during the UTC month across the borrower's monitors; unchanged baseline records and retries of the same version are excluded. If an analysis exceeds capacity, processing fails without publishing a new result or usage debit; contact support to arrange capacity. It does not automatically move to another complexity price.
