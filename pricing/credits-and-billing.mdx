## Fund one annual wallet

Your minimum monthly commitment is agreed during contracting. You prepay the full 12-month amount at signing.

| Item | Minimum commitment |
| --- | ---: |
| Monthly commitment used to calculate prepayment | CAD 1,000 |
| Contract term | 12 months |
| Annual prepayment | CAD 12,000 |
| Credit balance funded by that payment | CAD 12,000 |

The prepayment is usable credit for eligible products and workflows. It is not an additional platform charge on top of those usage deductions.

## How deductions work

The examples express credits as a **CAD monetary balance**. Deduct the contracted product rate for each billable unit:

- **Insights:** completed underwritings multiplied by the applicable tier rate.
- **Signal:** billable active account-months multiplied by the applicable tier rate, using the account-counting and proration rules in your order form.

`Closing balance = opening balance + purchased credits - billable usage + agreed adjustments`

For a 10% contracted unit-rate discount, a Standard Insights underwriting would use **CAD 4.50** instead of CAD 5. This is an illustration, not a published discount offer.

Your order form should identify the usage records used to reconcile each debit: product, application or account identifier, billing period or event date, tier, quantity, contracted rate, and amount. The billing mechanism and reporting format are agreed during onboarding.

## Flexible allocation

Use the wallet across eligible Insights and Signal workflows, regardless of the product mix assumed during contracting. Credits are not divided into twelve monthly balances or reserved for a particular product unless your order form explicitly defines an eligibility restriction.

Spending more than the monthly commitment in one month does not itself trigger an overage. Additional funding is needed when the **shared wallet balance** is insufficient for the requested usage.

## No monthly expiration and active-account rollover

Unused credits remain available while you maintain an active account. They carry forward across months and renewals.

Rollover preserves the unused balance. Your next annual commitment is still reviewed and agreed separately. Your order form must state how carried credits interact with the next annual prepayment and define the conditions for an active account, including any suspension or lapse.

Cancellation ends this active-account condition. Remaining-credit treatment is addressed in [contract terms](/pricing/contract-terms).

## Additional credits and automatic top-ups

You can purchase additional credits when the prepaid balance runs out. Automatic top-ups require an agreement in advance.

For automatic top-ups, agree the trigger balance, purchase amount, funding method, spending limits, and treatment of a failed payment. Your order form should also define what happens to billable processing when there are insufficient credits and no authorized top-up.

A top-up increases the available wallet balance. It does not by itself change the agreed product rates or create a new 12-month term; any change to those terms belongs in a separate agreement.

## Currency and separate charges

Prices and wallet examples are in **Canadian dollars (CAD)**. If you pay in another currency, agree the conversion rate source, conversion date, fees, and credited CAD amount before payment.

Third-party provider fees are outside the AMLOK processing rates. Your order form separately specifies those charges and the treatment of applicable taxes. Do not deduct an unagreed provider charge from the wallet.
