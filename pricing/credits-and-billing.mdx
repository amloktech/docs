## Monthly minimum, paid annually

Your quote sets a minimum monthly commitment, starting at **CAD 1,000**. Pay 12 times that amount at agreement signing and each renewal: **CAD 12,000 at the minimum** for a 12-month agreement. The payment becomes unused credits in your workspace balance; it is not an additional fee on top of product usage.

## One balance for your workspace

Your workspace has one shared credit balance for all eligible AMLOK products. The annual prepayment, additional paid top-ups, and promotional top-ups from the AMLOK commercial team all increase the same balance. There are no separate spending buckets or consumption priorities based on how credits were added.

As billable processing occurs, AMLOK deducts the agreed cost from that balance:

- **Insights:** the agreed rate for each completed underwriting.
- **Signal:** the agreed rate once per active borrower/business per month in the workspace, regardless of the number of monitoring checks. Partial-month proration follows your agreement.

Reporting identifies the product, tier, billable unit, quantity, effective rate, and charge so you can understand each deduction. Products share funding; their different billing units remain distinct. See [consumption reporting](/api-reference/consumption-reporting).

`Closing credits = opening credits + posted top-ups - product usage - cancellation refunds + reconciliation adjustments`

Credits already consumed by a product are non-refundable. Corrections to an erroneous or reversed charge are recorded separately and are not a refund of correctly billed usage.

## Payment methods and automatic top-ups

Manage payment methods and top-up settings in the **client portal**. Accepted funding methods are **card or bank account**. You can change the payment method, the balance threshold that triggers a top-up, and the amount added each time.

| Setting | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic top-up threshold | CAD 200 or higher. Choose a higher threshold to allow time for funding and anticipated usage. |
| Customer-funded top-up amount | At least CAD 500 per top-up. |
| Processing balance floor | CAD 200. Billable processing cannot reduce available credits below this amount. |
| Refund destination | At least one bank account must be saved in the portal for cancellation refunds, including when you fund by card. |

An authorized automatic top-up is triggered when available credits reach or fall below your configured threshold. A paid top-up adds usable credits when the payment settles; pending payments do not fund processing. Commercial promotional top-ups add credits when posted by AMLOK.

AMLOK checks the projected available balance before accepting a billable deduction or reserving credits for pending work. If that activity would reduce available credits below **CAD 200**, processing pauses until sufficient credits are available. Existing holds count toward this check, so concurrent activity cannot spend the same credits twice. The CAD 200 floor remains unused credit and is included in the cancellation refund.

Automatic top-ups help maintain continuity, but a pending or failed payment can still cause a processing pause. Set a threshold and top-up amount appropriate to your usage and payment settlement time. Credit reporting remains available while processing is paused on an active account.

Portal payment and cancellation controls are separate from the public API. The public API provides reporting and exports only; it cannot change payment methods, initiate top-ups, configure thresholds, request cancellation, or move funds.

## Rollover and renewal

Credits do not expire monthly. Unused credits remain in the shared balance while your account is active, including across renewal. At the annual review, AMLOK reassesses your monthly minimum, workload limits, product rates, and applicable discounts. Payment of the next 12 months' minimum is due at renewal; any agreed treatment of carried credits is recorded in your renewal agreement.

## Currency and separate charges

Prices and credits are in **Canadian dollars (CAD)**. If you pay in another currency, agree the conversion methodology and credited CAD amount before payment. Third-party provider charges are separate from AMLOK processing rates and the service-credit balance. Your agreement identifies those charges and applicable taxes.

## Cancellation and unused credits

Request account cancellation in the client portal. Enterprise clients can also use their dedicated account manager. Cancellation initiates workspace deletion and reconciliation of final usage. All workspace data is deleted as part of completed cancellation.

AMLOK issues an **immediate refund of the entire unused credit balance** when the account closes after final reconciliation, including promotional credits and the unused CAD 200 floor. The refund goes to the bank account saved in the portal, not automatically to the card used for funding. Funds may take **7-10 business days**, depending on your bank. Used credits are non-refundable.
