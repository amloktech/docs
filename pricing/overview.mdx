## Commit annually. Use credits as you need them.

Start with a **CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment**, prepaid as **CAD 12,000 for a 12-month term** at signing. The full payment funds one shared wallet. Eligible usage deducts credits from that balance throughout the year.

Your monthly commitment determines the annual prepayment. It does not limit how much you can use in a particular month.

## Product rates

Choose a complexity tier that matches the data and analysis your workflow requires.

| Complexity tier | Insights / underwriting | Signal / active account / month |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Standard | CAD 5 | CAD 3 |
| Advanced | CAD 15 | CAD 5 |
| Complex | CAD 35 | CAD 8 |
| Custom | Agreed in your order form | Agreed in your order form |

<Note>
**All rates are in CAD, before negotiated discounts.** Your signed order form records the final rates, workload limits, eligible products, and any volume discounts. Third-party provider fees are separate.
</Note>

Published rates are the base rates for each tier before negotiated discounts. A larger commitment may qualify for lower unit rates. See [complexity tiers](/pricing/complexity-tiers) for the workload differences.

## One wallet across both products

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agree your commitment">
    Set the monthly commitment, eligible products, workload tiers, and unit rates during contracting. The minimum annual prepayment is CAD 12,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund the annual wallet">
    Prepay the full 12-month commitment at signing. Credits are available as one annual balance, with no monthly expiration or product-specific allocation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use credits across your workflows">
    Deduct the agreed rate when an Insights underwriting is billable or a Signal monitoring period is billed. Your mix of products can change from the original volume estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up and review annually">
    Purchase additional credits when needed, or arrange automatic top-ups. Review the next commitment and applicable discounts every 12 months.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example, **100 Standard underwritings** and **200 Standard monitored accounts for one month** use **CAD 1,100** of credits. The wallet covers that usage even though it exceeds the CAD 1,000 monthly commitment used to calculate the prepayment.

Unused credits remain available while you maintain an active account, including across renewal. See [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing) and [contract terms](/pricing/contract-terms) for rollover and cancellation treatment.
