## Monthly minimum, paid annually

Start with a **CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment, paid annually at contract signing and each contract renewal**. At this minimum, you prepay **CAD 12,000 for the next 12 months**. The full payment funds one shared wallet, and eligible usage deducts credits from that balance.

Your minimum monthly commitment determines the amount paid annually. It does not limit how much you can use in a particular month.

## Product rates

AMLOK assigns the lowest tier that covers all workload dimensions and the required analysis scope.

| Complexity tier | Insights / underwriting | Signal / active account / month |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Standard | CAD 5 | CAD 3 |
| Advanced | CAD 15 | CAD 5 |
| Complex | CAD 35 | CAD 8 |

<Note>
**All rates are in CAD, before negotiated discounts.** Your signed order form records the final rates, workload limits, eligible products, and any volume discounts. Third-party provider fees are separate.
</Note>

Custom quotes retain Standard, Advanced, or Complex with a negotiated rate and explicit workload limits. There is no fourth reporting tier. Published rates are the base rates for each tier before negotiated discounts. A higher minimum monthly commitment may qualify for lower unit rates. See [complexity tiers](/pricing/complexity-tiers) for the workload differences.

## One workspace balance across both products

<Steps>
  <Step title="Agree your minimum monthly commitment">
    Set the minimum monthly commitment, eligible products, workload tiers, and unit rates during contracting. The minimum monthly commitment starts at CAD 1,000.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay annually at signing and renewal">
    At contract signing and each renewal, prepay 12 times your agreed minimum monthly commitment. At the CAD 1,000 monthly minimum, the payment is CAD 12,000. Credits are available in one shared balance, with no monthly expiration or product-specific allocation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use credits across your workflows">
    Deduct the agreed rate when an Insights underwriting is billable or a Signal monitoring period is billed. Your mix of products can change from the original volume estimate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up and review annually">
    Manage card or bank-account payment methods and automatic top-ups in the client portal. The trigger is at least CAD 200 and each customer-funded top-up is at least CAD 500. Processing pauses before crossing the CAD 200 balance floor. Review your monthly minimum, rates, and workload limits each year.
  </Step>
</Steps>

For example, **100 Standard underwritings** and **200 Standard monitored accounts for one month** use **CAD 1,100** of credits. The wallet covers that usage even though it exceeds the CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment used to calculate the prepayment.

Paid and promotional top-ups join the same workspace balance with no spending priority. Unused credits remain available while the account is active, including across renewal. On final cancellation, workspace data is deleted and the entire unused balance is refunded to the saved portal bank account; used credits are non-refundable. See [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing) and [agreement terms](/pricing/contract-terms) for rollover and cancellation treatment.
