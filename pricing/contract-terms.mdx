Your signed order form records the commercial terms that apply to your account. Use this page to understand the minimum monthly commitment, annual payment timing, and the items that must be defined before signing.

## Monthly minimum and payment timing

The minimum monthly commitment starts at **CAD 1,000**, paid annually at **contract signing and each contract renewal**. Each contract term is **12 months**. At signing or renewal, prepay 12 times the agreed minimum monthly commitment: **CAD 12,000 at the minimum**. A higher monthly minimum results in a larger payment.

The prepaid amount funds your credit wallet for eligible usage. Usage is charged at your contracted rates as the relevant billing events occur.

## Annual review and volume discounts

Review the minimum monthly commitment every 12 months using actual product usage, expected growth, projected origination volume, the size of the monitored portfolio, and the expected complexity mix.

At renewal, agree the minimum monthly commitment and applicable volume discounts for the next term. The payment for the next 12 months is due at that renewal. Base unit rates can be reduced by negotiated discounts. Discount percentages and eligibility thresholds are agreed commercially rather than promised by a public automatic schedule.

Existing credits remain available while the account stays active. Document whether carried credits offset any part of the next annual payment. Rollover alone does not specify that treatment.

## Cancellation and remaining credits

<Note>
**Cancellation terms are contract-specific.** Your signed agreement defines the treatment of remaining prepaid credits, any refund provisions, exchange-rate methodology, and obligations under the 12-month term.
</Note>

Before signing, your order form must define:

| Term | Required treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Cancellation date | Required notice, when access and monitoring end, and how final usage is reconciled. |
| Remaining prepaid credits | Whether credits can be used during a notice period, retained, refunded, or forfeited after closure. |
| Refund eligibility | Which circumstances qualify, any deductions, the calculation, and the payment deadline. |
| Minimum-term obligations | How cancellation affects obligations under the 12-month term and any unpaid contractual amounts. |
| Currency conversion | The currency of any refund, exchange-rate source and date, conversion fees, and treatment of FX gains or losses. |
| Active-account requirement | Whether a pause, suspension, non-renewal, or account closure ends rollover eligibility. |

Any refund, retention, or forfeiture of unused credits after cancellation follows the signed agreement.

## Order-form checklist

- Contracting parties, effective date, 12-month term, and renewal procedure.
- Minimum monthly commitment, payment for 12 months at signing and each renewal, billing currency, and tax treatment.
- Eligible products, complexity tiers, workload limits, base rates, and discounts.
- Insights completion criteria and Signal account-counting, billing-date, and proration rules.
- Third-party provider charges and payment responsibility.
- Wallet rollover, active-account conditions, and renewal treatment of carried credits.
- Top-up authorization, limits, and insufficient-balance handling.
- Cancellation, remaining credits, refund provisions, FX methodology, and minimum-term obligations.
