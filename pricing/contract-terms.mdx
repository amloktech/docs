## Your agreement

Your agreement defines the monthly minimum based on your estimated volume, assigned complexity tiers, and agreed unit rates. The published default is **CAD 1,000.00 per month**, funded annually as **CAD 12,000.00**, or quarterly as **CAD 3,000.00** when that schedule is agreed. Your workspace can have negotiated terms.

There is no scheduled monthly payment. Product usage draws from your prepaid workspace balance. Additional top-ups are separate funding payments when needed.

| Agreement item | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly minimum | The quoted minimum based on expected monthly volume |
| Payment timing | Quarterly prepayment (3 months) or annual prepayment (12 months), according to the agreement |
| Prepayment discount | A separately agreed discount on scheduled prepayment; the nominal monthly minimum and product rates remain unchanged |
| Workload and tiers | Amlok-assessed Standard, Advanced or Complex analysis; capacity limits are separate |
| Rates | Published or negotiated CAD rates, shown to two decimals |
| Insights usage | One charge for a completed full underwriting |
| Signal usage | One charge per active borrower/business per workspace per month, including change-driven reassessment |
| Cancellation cutoff | Immediate processing cutoff under the cancellation terms, confirmed through support; previously consumed credits are non-refundable |
| Additional charges | Any separately agreed services are identified in your agreement |

Custom pricing retains one of the three tiers and records the negotiated rate and workload limits.

## Payment methods and top-ups

Console **Billing** exposes the payment methods and automatic-payment settings available to your environment. Sandbox uses simulated card or ACH methods and never transfers real funds. Live funding requires activated payment facilities; support confirms the available funding process. Automatic top-ups trigger below **CAD 200.00** by default, or your selected higher threshold. The amount you select must be at least **CAD 500.00**.

With auto-pay off, requests continue below CAD 200.00 while available credits cover them. Insufficient credits cause the billable operation to be rejected without charging for unprocessed work. Pending payments do not count as available credits.

## Annual review and renewal

Every 12 months, AMLOK reviews usage, expected growth, processing needs, tier assignments, and applicable volume discounts with you. Your renewed agreement sets the next monthly minimum and rates. Payments follow the agreed quarterly or annual schedule.

Unused credits do not expire monthly. They remain in the shared balance while your account is active; your agreement specifies their treatment in the renewal payment calculation.

## Cancellation and refund

The commercial terms allow cancellation at any time, including before renewal, with **no notice period**. Submit your request through [support](/support/create-ticket) or your dedicated account manager. There is no self-service account-cancellation or refund action in Console or the public API.

The agreed terms provide for immediate cessation of processing, usage deductions and new funding attempts, then full closure at the **end of the request month**. Support confirms the cutoff and closure schedule in writing and coordinates any in-flight work and funding. A support request acknowledgment, a paused monitor or a revoked API key is not evidence that every closure step has completed.

At closure, the terms provide for immediate issuance of the **full unused-credit refund**, including promotional credits, with no refund fee or remaining-term deduction. Used credits are non-refundable. Support confirms the reconciled amount and refund destination through the approved payment process. Never send bank credentials or full payment details by email. Bank receipt can take **7-10 business days after issuance**.

Export needed records before closure. Data deletion is a separate, confirmed process governed by the agreement, applicable retention obligations and any legal hold; it is not triggered automatically by a calendar date or by sending a support message. See [cancellation and refunds](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts) and [privacy and data handling](/security/privacy).

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I make a monthly payment?">
    No. Your quoted monthly minimum determines gross quarterly prepayment (× 3) or annual prepayment (× 12), then any agreed prepayment-only discount reduces that payment. Additional top-ups fund usage when needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does Signal have a monthly rate?">
    The account-month measures usage deducted from your prepaid credits. It is not a separate monthly funding payment. Each active borrower/business is counted once per workspace per month.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a Signal alert trigger another Insights charge?">
    No. Signal includes reassessment using existing data and new changes. A separately requested full Insights underwriting is a distinct product operation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can my balance fall below CAD 200.00?">
    Yes. CAD 200.00 is the default automatic-payment trigger. Requests continue as long as available credits cover their cost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are promotional credits separate?">
    No. Paid and promotional top-ups share one balance with no consumption priority. The full unused balance is refundable at final cancellation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel before the annual renewal?">
    Yes, at any time without notice. Request cancellation through support. The agreed terms provide for month-end closure and refund issuance at closure; support confirms the cutoff, reconciliation and payment reference.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do custom rates create another complexity tier?">
    No. Your quote uses Standard, Advanced, or Complex with negotiated rates and explicit workload limits.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do unused credits expire each month?">
    No. They remain available while your account is active.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Product charge timing

Insights consumes credits once the full agreed underwriting output is completed and published. Signal publishes a full monthly charge with the first successful evaluation output in each UTC calendar month, including a first evaluation published mid-month, without proration. An already paid borrower/business is not charged again for another monitor, pause/resume, check or reassessment in that month. Insufficient credits block the unfunded operation without a debt or negative balance. See [Signal pricing](/pricing/signal) and [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing).
