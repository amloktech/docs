Your AMLOK agreement and signed order form set out the commercial terms that apply to your account. This page explains the pricing and billing model, including the terms that can vary based on your business volume, product mix, and processing needs. Your signed documents contain the specific terms agreed for your business.

## Monthly minimum and payment timing

The minimum monthly commitment starts at **CAD 1,000**, paid annually at **agreement signing and each renewal**. Each agreement term is **12 months**. You pay 12 times your agreed minimum monthly commitment upfront: **CAD 12,000 at the minimum**. The amount is higher if your agreement includes a higher monthly minimum.

This payment funds your credit wallet for eligible usage. Credits are deducted at your agreed rates when billable events occur.

## Terms tailored to your business

Your order form reflects the services and billing arrangements agreed for your account. These details can vary between clients:

| Area | What applies to your account |
| --- | --- |
| Products and processing | Your eligible products, assigned complexity tiers, connected sources, and workload allowances. |
| Pricing | Your minimum monthly commitment, unit rates, and any negotiated volume discounts. |
| Billing events | The completion criteria for Insights, and the account-counting, billing-date, and proration rules for Signal. |
| Additional charges | Any applicable third-party provider charges, payment responsibility, and tax treatment. |
| Wallet funding | Your authorized payment method, automatic top-up amount and trigger, spending limits, and insufficient-balance handling. |
| Withdrawals and transfers | The applicable verification requirements, limits, fees, and payout arrangements. Transfers remain limited to wallets owned by the same customer institution and legal entity. |

Your agreement also identifies the parties, effective date, and renewal procedure.

## Annual review and volume discounts

The AMLOK team reviews your account with you every 12 months. The review considers actual product usage, expected growth, projected origination volume, the size of your monitored portfolio, and your processing and analysis requirements.

Your renewed agreement reflects the minimum monthly commitment, complexity tiers, and applicable volume discounts agreed for the next term. Payment for the next 12 months is due at renewal. Discounts can reduce the base unit rates; their availability and amount depend on your agreement and are not applied automatically through a public volume schedule.

## Credits at renewal

Unused credits remain available while your account stays active and can carry forward across months and renewals. Whether carried credits offset any part of your payment at renewal depends on your agreement. Rollover does not automatically reduce that payment.

Your agreement also explains how a pause, suspension, non-renewal, or account closure affects active-account status and rollover eligibility.

## Cancellation and remaining credits

<Note>
**Cancellation terms are agreement-specific.** Your signed agreement defines the treatment of remaining prepaid credits, any refund provisions, exchange-rate methodology, and obligations under the 12-month term.
</Note>

Cancellation arrangements vary by client. Your agreement explains how each of the following applies to your account:

| Term | How it applies |
| --- | --- |
| Notice and end date | Your notice period determines when cancellation takes effect and when access and monitoring end. Final usage is reconciled under the agreed billing rules. |
| Remaining credits | Unused settled committed credits remain eligible for withdrawal, subject to holds and disclosed restrictions. Usage during the notice period and final reconciliation affect the available balance. |
| Refund eligibility | The circumstances that qualify, any authorized deductions, the refund calculation, and the payment deadline depend on your signed terms. |
| Minimum-term obligations | Cancellation does not automatically waive obligations under the 12-month term or unpaid amounts. Your agreement sets out the obligations that remain. |
| Currency conversion | If a refund involves currency conversion, your agreed terms determine the refund currency, exchange-rate source and date, fees, and treatment of exchange-rate gains or losses. |

Unused settled committed credits can also be withdrawn without requesting an agreement-based refund. A withdrawal does not cancel your services, reduce your minimum monthly commitment, or waive remaining obligations. Any set-off or deduction is itemized and subject to your signed agreement and applicable law. A delay in closing your account does not automatically forfeit cash-eligible funds.
