Your agreement records the monthly minimum, product rates, workload limits, and processing scope quoted for your business. These terms can vary by client; the shared-credit and cancellation policies below explain how the account operates.

## Monthly minimum, paid annually

The minimum monthly commitment starts at **CAD 1,000**. You pay 12 times the agreed monthly minimum at signing and each annual renewal: **CAD 12,000 at the minimum**. Each agreement covers a 12-month term. The payment becomes unused credits in your workspace's shared balance, available across eligible products.

Product usage is deducted at the agreed rate. The monthly minimum is not a separate subscription fee on top of those deductions and does not cap activity in any particular month. Credits do not expire monthly.

## Terms tailored to your business

| Area | What your agreement records |
| --- | --- |
| Processing | Eligible products, assigned Standard, Advanced, or Complex tier, workload limits, banking-history coverage, and analysis scope. |
| Rates | Published or negotiated unit rates, applicable discounts, and the monthly minimum. Custom quotes retain one of the three tier labels. |
| Billable activity | Insights completion criteria and Signal billing-period and proration rules. Signal counts each active borrower/business once per workspace per month, regardless of the number of checks. |
| Separate charges | Applicable taxes and third-party provider charges outside the service-credit balance. |
| Renewal | The annual review, next monthly minimum, revised rates and limits, and treatment of carried credits. |
| Cancellation | The effective service end date and the process for closing the account, deleting workspace data, and returning unused credits. |

## Manage payments in the portal

The client portal lets you manage **card or bank-account payment methods**, the automatic top-up threshold, and the top-up amount. Customer-funded top-ups start at **CAD 500**. The threshold cannot be below **CAD 200**, and billable processing pauses before it would reduce available credits below the **CAD 200 hard floor**.

Choose a higher trigger and sufficient top-up amount to allow for payment settlement and your expected activity. Pending or failed funding does not provide usable credits. The unused floor remains part of your balance and is refundable on cancellation.

Save at least one bank account in the portal for refunds, even if your funding method is a card. These portal controls are not public API operations. See [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing).

## One balance and annual review

Paid and promotional top-ups join the same workspace balance. There is no source-based spending priority or product-specific funding allocation. Reports identify which product and tier consumed credits without creating separate product balances.

AMLOK reviews the agreement every 12 months using actual usage, expected growth, processing requirements, and applicable volume discounts. Unused credits carry forward while the account is active. The next 12 months' minimum is paid at renewal; any agreed offset using carried credits is recorded in the renewed agreement.

## Cancellation and refund

Request cancellation in the client portal. Enterprise clients may also use their dedicated account manager. Cancellation schedules the workspace for deletion, stops new billable activity and automatic top-ups at the effective service end date, and reconciles final usage and pending funding.

**Completed cancellation deletes all workspace data. AMLOK issues an immediate refund of the entire unused credit balance when the account closes.** This includes unused paid and promotional credits and the CAD 200 floor. Credits already used on products are non-refundable. The refund is not reduced by withdrawal fees, refund fees, or remaining minimum-term obligations.

The refund goes to the bank account saved in the portal. Funds may take **7-10 business days** to arrive, depending on your bank. Export needed records before requesting cancellation. See [cancellation and refunds](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts) for reconciliation, delivery, and returned-payment handling.

There are no public API commands for cancellation, refunds, transfers, or payment settings. Reporting and export operations do not move money.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the monthly minimum an additional subscription fee?">
    No. The monthly minimum determines the amount paid annually. That payment becomes credits used to pay for eligible product activity.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do credits expire each month?">
    No. Unused credits stay in the shared balance while the account remains active, including across renewals.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I manage payment methods and automatic top-ups myself?">
    Yes, in the client portal. Use a card or bank account, choose a threshold of at least CAD 200, and a customer-funded top-up amount of at least CAD 500. Processing cannot take available credits below CAD 200.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are promotional credits spent first?">
    No. Paid and promotional top-ups become one balance with no consumption priority. The full unused balance is refundable on cancellation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are Insights and Signal billed separately?">
    Both draw from one workspace balance. Reports separate their activity and rates so each deduction is clear; connected bank accounts are inputs, not additional Signal billing accounts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a custom quote create a fourth tier?">
    No. It specifies a negotiated rate and agreed workload limits under Standard, Advanced, or Complex. Reporting uses that tier and the actual effective rate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are provider fees included?">
    Third-party data, verification, connectivity, and document-provider charges are separate from AMLOK product rates and are specified in your agreement.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What is refunded when I cancel?">
    The entire unused balance, including promotional credits and the unused CAD 200 floor. Used credits are non-refundable. AMLOK issues the refund immediately when the account closes; bank receipt may take 7-10 business days. A saved bank account is required, even if you funded by card.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
