Your AMLOK agreement and signed order form set out the commercial terms that apply to your account. This page explains the pricing and billing model, including the terms that can vary based on your business volume, product mix, and processing needs. Your signed documents contain the specific terms agreed for your business.

## Monthly minimum and payment timing

The minimum monthly commitment starts at **CAD 1,000**, paid annually at **agreement signing and each renewal**. Each agreement term is **12 months**. You pay 12 times your agreed minimum monthly commitment upfront: **CAD 12,000 at the minimum**. The amount is higher if your agreement includes a higher monthly minimum.

This payment funds your credit wallet for eligible usage. Credits are deducted at your agreed rates when billable events occur.

## Terms tailored to your business

Your order form reflects the services and billing arrangements agreed for your account. These details can vary between clients:

| Area | What applies to your account |
| --- | --- |
| Products and processing | Your eligible products, assigned complexity tiers, connected sources, and workload allowances. |
| Pricing | Your minimum monthly commitment, unit rates, and any negotiated volume discounts. |
| Billing events | The completion criteria for Insights, and the account-counting, billing-date, and proration rules for Signal. |
| Additional charges | Any applicable third-party provider charges, payment responsibility, and tax treatment. |
| Wallet funding | Your authorized payment method, automatic top-up amount and trigger, spending limits, and insufficient-balance handling. |
| Cancellation arrangements | The notice period, effective cancellation date, and verified return destination. AMLOK issues the full remaining paid-balance refund immediately after account closure; funds may take 7-10 business days to reach your bank account, depending on your bank. |

Your agreement also identifies the parties, effective date, and renewal procedure.

## Annual review and volume discounts

The AMLOK team reviews your account with you every 12 months. The review considers actual product usage, expected growth, projected origination volume, the size of your monitored portfolio, and your processing and analysis requirements.

Your renewed agreement reflects the minimum monthly commitment, complexity tiers, and applicable volume discounts agreed for the next term. Payment for the next 12 months is due at renewal. Discounts can reduce the base unit rates; their availability and amount depend on your agreement and are not applied automatically through a public volume schedule.

## Credits at renewal

Unused credits remain available while your account stays active and can carry forward across months and renewals. Whether carried credits offset any part of your payment at renewal depends on your agreement. Rollover does not automatically reduce that payment.

Your agreement also explains how a pause, suspension, non-renewal, or account closure affects active-account status and rollover eligibility. Account cancellation is distinct from workspace deletion; completed workspace deletion removes all associated workspace data.

## Cancellation and remaining credits

Your account manager handles agreement changes, renewal, and cancellation. Contact them to confirm the effective date; there are no customer API endpoints for agreement cancellation, refund requests, payouts, approvals, or account closure.

On final cancellation of your agreement, AMLOK reconciles product usage and settled funding before closing your account. AMLOK issues a refund of your **full remaining paid wallet balance immediately after account closure**. Funds may take **7-10 business days** to reach your bank account, depending on your bank. This applies to both prepaid minimum-commitment credits and additional top-ups. Promotional credits have no cash refund value.

Cancellation arrangements such as notice, the effective date, and any currency conversion can vary by client. Your signed agreement explains how these apply:

| Term | How it applies |
| --- | --- |
| Notice and end date | Your notice period determines when cancellation takes effect and access and monitoring end. A cancellation request alone does not release wallet funds. |
| Final usage | Product usage incurred before services end is reconciled once. Pending payments and holds are resolved before the final balance is confirmed. |
| Remaining paid credits | The entire unused paid balance is returned on final cancellation. AMLOK does not deduct a withdrawal fee, refund fee, or remaining minimum-term obligation from that balance. |
| Minimum-term obligations | Any separate obligations under the 12-month agreement remain separately identified. They do not become wallet usage or reduce the remaining-balance refund. |
| Refund delivery | AMLOK issues the refund immediately after account closure. Funds may take 7-10 business days to reach your bank account, depending on your bank. The refund normally returns to the original payment source; if that source cannot receive it, a verified alternative owned by the same legal entity is used. Any currency-conversion arrangements follow your signed terms. |

The wallet does not support on-demand withdrawals or transfers. Disabling top-ups, changing payment methods, or pausing usage does not cancel your agreement or trigger a refund.

See [refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts) for the final cancellation process and [credits and billing](/pricing/credits-and-billing) for the wallet model.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment an additional subscription fee?">
    No. You pay the minimum monthly commitment annually: CAD 1,000 multiplied by 12, or CAD 12,000, at agreement signing and each renewal. The payment becomes usable credits for eligible usage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I lose credits in a quiet month?">
    No. Credits stay in one shared wallet, with no monthly expiration. Unused credits remain available while you maintain an active account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move usage between Insights and Signal?">
    Yes. Use the shared wallet across eligible products and workflows, even when your usage mix differs from the original volume estimate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every connected bank account create a Signal charge?">
    No. Signal bills the enrolled portfolio accounts defined in your order form. Their connected bank accounts are inputs to the monitoring workload.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are provider fees included?">
    No. Third-party data, verification, connectivity, and document-provider charges are separate. The applicable charges are specified in your order form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are unused credits refunded if I cancel?">
    Yes. AMLOK issues the full remaining paid-balance refund immediately after your account closes following final cancellation and reconciliation. Funds may take 7-10 business days to reach your bank account, depending on your bank. Funds cannot be withdrawn while the agreement is active. See [refunds and payouts](/pricing/refunds-and-payouts) for the process.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
