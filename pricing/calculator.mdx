Choose your products and enter monthly volumes for each complexity tier. The estimate separates **usage deductions** from the **payment due at contract signing or renewal**.

## How to use the estimate

- **Insights:** enter completed underwritings per month. Split your expected volume across Standard, Advanced, and Complex.
- **Signal:** enter the average number of active monitored portfolio accounts in each full month. Connected bank accounts are data inputs, not separate billable portfolio accounts.
- **Minimum monthly commitment:** starts at CAD 1,000, paid annually as 12 times the agreed amount at signing and each renewal.
- **Agreed discount:** enter a discount only if it applies to your contract. The calculator applies the same percentage to all product rates, without reducing the monthly minimum.

## Calculation assumptions

The estimate repeats the entered monthly volumes for 12 months, uses full monitoring months, and starts with no carried credit balance. It rounds each monthly product-and-tier subtotal to the nearest cent before projecting 12 months.

The payment at signing or renewal funds the credit wallet. **Total funding** is that payment plus any additional credits needed to cover estimated usage. It does not add the same usage charges a second time.

Unused credits remain available while your account stays active. The timing of actual top-ups depends on when usage occurs. Renewal treatment of carried credits follows your signed agreement.

This is a planning estimate, not a quote. It excludes taxes, third-party provider fees, custom-priced workflows, and partial-month adjustments. The figures describe usage, not a monthly invoice. Final rates and workload limits are agreed in your order form.
