AMLOK Signal monitors your existing portfolio after origination. It processes new evidence and account activity to identify changes that may require attention.

## Monthly rates

| Tier | Rate per active account / month | Typical workload |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Standard | CAD 3 | Routine account activity, incremental analysis, and periodic risk summaries. |
| Advanced | CAD 5 | More connected sources, higher activity, and deeper reconciliation or reassessment. |
| Complex | CAD 8 | High-volume activity, related entities, and substantial ongoing analysis. |
| Custom | Agreed rate | Specialized cadence, unusually large volumes, or workflows outside the tier limits. |

<Note>
**Rates are in CAD, before negotiated discounts.** Your order form confirms the rates, monitoring scope, and workload limits.
</Note>

## What counts as an active account

A monitored account is the portfolio account you enroll in Signal under the account identifier agreed in your order form. It is active for billing when monitoring is enabled for the applicable billing period.

Connected bank accounts and other data sources feed that monitored account. They are workload inputs, not additional monitored portfolio accounts. Your order form specifies whether multiple lending facilities for the same borrower are billed separately or grouped.

## What the monthly rate covers

Within your agreed scope, the rate covers processing incremental evidence, routine risk analysis, and the agreed monitoring summaries and alerts. It includes the cadence and material-change analysis defined for that tier.

The account-month is the billing unit. Individual source transactions, routine alerts, or internal processing steps do not each create an additional Signal charge within the agreed scope.

A separately requested full re-underwriting can be billed under [Insights](/pricing/insights). Agree that work before it runs so a routine monitoring update is not also charged as a full underwriting.

## Billing period and account changes

Your order form defines:

- The monthly billing date and time zone.
- How activation and deactivation dates determine billable usage.
- Whether partial months are prorated, and the calculation used.
- How suspended monitoring and interrupted data access affect billing.
- The effective date of a change in monitoring tier.

The CAD 3 Standard rate is per active monitored account **per month**. Your minimum monthly commitment is paid annually at contract signing and each renewal to fund the shared credit wallet.

## Example

Monitoring **200 Standard accounts for 12 full months** uses:

`200 × CAD 3 × 12 = CAD 7,200`

With a CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment paid annually, you add CAD 12,000 to the wallet at signing or renewal. From that payment, this monitoring usage leaves **CAD 4,800** available for other eligible usage, including Insights. This example assumes the same 200 accounts stay active for every full month.
