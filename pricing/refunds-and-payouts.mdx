Your AMLOK wallet holds prepaid credits for AMLOK products. Funds leave the wallet when they are used for a product or when **the remaining paid balance is refunded after final cancellation of your agreement**. The wallet does not support on-demand withdrawals or transfers.

Both your prepaid minimum monthly commitment and additional top-ups follow this rule. Your minimum monthly commitment is paid annually at agreement signing and each renewal.

## When a refund applies

AMLOK issues a refund of your **full remaining paid balance immediately after your account closes** following final cancellation. Funds may take **7-10 business days** to reach your bank account, depending on your bank.

Final usage, pending payments, and refund instructions are reconciled before account closure. Requesting cancellation, disabling automatic top-ups, changing a payment method, or pausing product usage does not make the wallet balance available for withdrawal.

The notice period and effective cancellation date follow your signed agreement and can vary by client. The full remaining paid-balance refund is issued immediately after account closure.

## How the remaining balance is calculated

Your final statement reconciles settled payments, product usage, and any corrections:

`Remaining paid balance = settled paid credits + payment and usage corrections - consumed paid credits - amounts already refunded for this cancellation`

For example, if you have paid **CAD 12,000** and used **CAD 1,100** on AMLOK products, the remaining paid balance to refund at final cancellation is **CAD 10,900**.

- Final usage includes billable activity incurred before services end, even if its charge posts later.
- Pending funding is reconciled before the refund amount is finalized. A payment that settles after cancellation is included in the final reconciliation and returned if unused.
- A hold is resolved before the final calculation. It is not a second charge or a forfeiture of funds.
- Promotional credits are not money you paid and have no cash refund value.
- AMLOK does not deduct a wallet withdrawal fee, refund fee, or remaining minimum-term obligation from the remaining paid-balance refund. Any separate agreement obligations remain separately identified.

## Cancellation and refund process

1. Contact your account manager to request final cancellation and receive confirmation of the effective date under your agreement.
2. At that date, new product usage and automatic top-ups stop. Usage already incurred and payments already in flight are reconciled.
3. AMLOK confirms the remaining paid balance in your final statement and verifies the refund payment instructions.
4. Your account closes, and AMLOK immediately issues the full remaining-balance refund. You do not need to submit a separate withdrawal request.
5. Funds may take **7-10 business days** to reach your bank account, depending on your bank. Your account manager provides the refund reference and delivery updates. Coordinate any final statements before completing workspace deletion.

Your account manager coordinates payment-instruction verification and refund delivery. Customers do not submit refund requests, payouts, approvals, transfers, or account-closure commands through the API. Correcting a payment instruction does not cancel the obligation to return the remaining funds.

## Where the refund is sent

Refunds normally return to the original payment method. When several payments funded your wallet, the refund may be delivered through several payments linked to their original sources. Together, those payments return the full remaining paid balance.

If an original payment method cannot receive the refund, AMLOK arranges a verified alternative destination owned by the same customer legal entity. This remains a cancellation refund, not a withdrawal facility or a payment to a third party. Updating the payment method for future top-ups does not change an already submitted refund.

Wallet balances and refund amounts are recorded in **CAD**. If currency conversion is needed, your agreement and payment instructions explain the settlement currency and conversion method before payment.

## Payment status, failures, and returns

Refund issuance and bank receipt are separate events. AMLOK issues the refund **immediately after account closure**; funds may take **7-10 business days** to reach your bank account, depending on your bank. Your account remains closed while bank processing completes.

If a payment fails or is returned, the unpaid amount remains owed under the same cancellation. AMLOK resolves the delivery issue and retries the remaining amount without paying the same funds twice. Returned funds do not reactivate the wallet for product usage or new top-ups.

For an unresolved payment, contact your account manager or [support@amlok.tech](mailto:support@amlok.tech) with the refund reference. AMLOK confirms the amount due, the amount paid, and any amount still awaiting delivery.
