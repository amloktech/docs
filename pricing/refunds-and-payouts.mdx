description: "How withdrawals, refunds, internal transfers, cancellation, and remaining balances work."

**Unused settled committed credits can be withdrawn**, along with eligible additional funds. Transfers can move an eligible balance between wallets belonging to the **same customer institution and legal owner**.

Your minimum monthly commitment remains **CAD 1,000 or the higher amount agreed**, paid annually at agreement signing and each renewal. Moving money out of the wallet does not cancel this commitment, waive a remaining obligation, or create new usable credits.

## Choose the right action

| Action | What it does | What remains separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Withdrawal | Sends eligible unused funds, including committed credits, to your verified same-owner bank destination. | Agreement cancellation and remaining term obligations. |
| Internal transfer | Moves funds between your institution's wallets while preserving origin and restrictions. | Transfers to other customers, third-party recipients, and borrower disbursement. |
| Refund | Returns an eligible amount to its original funding source under the signed refund terms. | Eligibility review; a request does not automatically cancel the agreement. |
| Agreement cancellation | Ends services on the confirmed effective date under the notice and term provisions. | A separate withdrawal or refund instruction to return funds. |
| Wallet closure | Closes a reconciled wallet after funds, holds, and pending movements are resolved. | The commercial agreement and retained audit records. |

## Amount available to withdraw

Start with unused settled committed credits and additional funds. Subtract amounts already consumed, reserved for an existing movement, disputed, legally restricted, or otherwise unavailable under a disclosed restriction. Promotional credits have no cash withdrawal or refund value.

The withdrawal quote states the principal, fees, total wallet debit and recipient amount before submission. Only disclosed, agreed fees may be charged. A quote does not reserve funds; the submitted instruction must still pass balance and authorization checks.

For example, a wallet with CAD 9,900 in settled unused committed credits and a CAD 500 hold has **CAD 9,400 available**, before any new quoted fee. A withdrawal reduces future purchasing capacity. Processing follows the agreed insufficient-balance policy until you fund the wallet again.

## Refunds to the original source

Refunds reference the original funding payment. The amount cannot exceed the eligible unconsumed principal remaining after prior refunds, withdrawals, usage, transfers and holds. Funding lineage remains traceable through internal transfers so the same principal cannot be refunded twice.

The signed agreement defines refund eligibility, permitted deductions, processing expectations and treatment of outstanding obligations. Refund approval does not prove that the payment has settled.

Use the original funding method where the route supports a refund. If that method cannot receive funds, obtain documented approval for a separately verified same-owner withdrawal route. Do not edit an already submitted refund or automatically redirect it to a third party.

## Payout timing, failures and returns

A successful request means the instruction was received. It does not mean the recipient bank has made funds available.

- Track review, required approval, submission, payment confirmation, and any return.
- Timing depends on the configured rail, bank cutoffs, verification, and review. The agreement or onboarding disclosures provide the applicable expectations.
- Cancel only while the instruction is cancellable. After submission, a recall is not guaranteed.
- If a response times out, retrieve the existing movement. Do not initiate another payment with a new business reference.
- A failed payment releases a reservation only after confirming funds did not leave.
- A returned payment creates a linked credit for the amount actually returned. Any agreed nonrefundable fee remains separately identifiable.

For an unresolved payment, contact your agreed support channel with the movement ID and request ID. Do not treat silence as failure or make the amount available for a duplicate withdrawal.

## Cancellation and final reconciliation

1. Request an estimate of final usage, remaining obligations, holds and distributable funds.
2. Give the required notice and receive confirmation of the effective date.
3. Stop new services and monitoring at that boundary according to the agreement. Reconcile usage already incurred and payments already submitted.
4. Disable future automatic top-ups and resolve any in-flight funding attempt.
5. Request a withdrawal or an eligible refund, then track settlement or a documented return.
6. Close the wallet after balances, pending movements, disputes and required records are resolved.

Unused committed credits retain their withdrawal eligibility; cancellation does not silently convert a cash-eligible balance into forfeited credits. Remaining minimum-term obligations, permitted set-off, refunds and cancellation charges follow the signed agreement. Any deduction must be itemized and authorized by those terms and applicable law. A remaining obligation is not automatically a wallet debit.

A blocked closure does not erase or forfeit funds. Restricted, disputed or unclaimed balances require a documented resolution under the agreement and applicable law. Retention of billing and audit records follows the data-retention policy after money has been paid out.

## Currency and agreement disclosures

Wallet amounts and the current movement API are in **CAD**. Cross-currency transfers are not part of these routes. If a funding payment or approved return requires conversion outside this API, agree the source and settlement currencies, exchange-rate source and date, fees, recipient amount, and treatment of FX gains or losses before execution.

Your agreement and onboarding disclosures must identify:

- Notice period, confirmed cancellation date, and treatment of the 12-month obligation.
- Eligible withdrawal and refund amounts, authorized deductions, limits and required approvals.
- Payment routes, verification, fees, expected processing times, returns and escalation contacts.
- Custody and payment-provider roles, applicable safeguarding disclosures, and any applicable deposit protection.
- Remaining balances, disputed or unclaimed funds, retention, and final statements.
