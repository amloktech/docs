---
title: "Cancellation and refunds"
description: "Request cancellation through support and track cutoff, reconciliation, refund issuance and data deletion."
---

You can request cancellation **at any time, without a notice period**, including during the 12-month agreement. The commercial terms provide for refund of the **entire unused balance**, including paid and promotional credits, at final closure. Used credits are non-refundable.

## Request cancellation

An authorized Owner should [contact support](/support/create-ticket) or their dedicated account manager with the organization, workspace and requested cancellation date. Do not send bank credentials, payment-card details, API keys or borrower documents in the request.

Console and the public API do not provide self-service cancellation, refund, payout or transfer commands. Pausing or stopping a monitor, disconnecting an integration, disabling autopay or revoking an API key does not cancel the account or complete a refund.

## Track each step

1. **Confirm scope and cutoff.** Support verifies the requester and affected workspaces. The cancellation terms provide for immediate cessation of processing, usage deductions and new or scheduled top-ups. Obtain written confirmation of the effective cutoff; a ticket acknowledgment alone does not prove that every operation has stopped.
2. **Reconcile activity.** Support reviews posted charges, unfinished work and funding already submitted. A submitted payment can still settle or be returned. Posted funding and the payment attempt must not be counted as two deposits. Erroneous charges are corrected through linked adjustments rather than changing historical entries.
3. **Confirm closure.** The agreed closure timing is the end of the calendar month in which cancellation is requested. Support supplies the reconciled unused balance and closure information. A statement marked `provisional` is a snapshot of posted entries, not the final closure statement.
4. **Confirm refund issuance.** The terms provide for immediate issuance of the full unused-credit refund at closure, without refund fees or deductions for remaining-term obligations. Support verifies the authorized bank destination through the approved payment process and provides a payment reference. Do not send payment credentials by email.
5. **Confirm data handling.** Export required records before closure. Support confirms the deletion scope, completion and any separately retained records under the agreement and applicable obligations. Closure, deletion and bank receipt are distinct outcomes.

For example, a request on October 10 has an immediate-cutoff requirement under the terms and October month-end as its closure timing. Track the actual cutoff confirmation, final reconciliation, refund reference and deletion confirmation individually. Sending the request does not automatically run those steps in Console.

## Refund calculation

**Unused credits = posted funding and promotional credits + correcting adjustments - product usage - any prior returns or refunds.**

Paid and promotional credits share one balance with no consumption priority. For example, CAD 12,000.00 of settled funding plus CAD 500.00 of promotional credits, less CAD 1,800.00 of usage, leaves **CAD 10,700.00**, assuming no other movements.

A funding attempt that is pending or failed is not part of available credits. A payment that settles or is returned after reconciliation requires a linked correction and an updated confirmation. A payment return after credits have been consumed requires account review; it must not be treated as new spendable credit or hidden by rewriting earlier charges. Contact support to dispute a charge or reconcile a return.

## Delivery and records

Bank receipt can take **7-10 business days after issuance** under the stated terms. Issuance is the confirmed payment instruction, not the date of the cancellation request or a simulated Sandbox refund. A rejected or returned refund needs support follow-up and a new confirmed destination or retry reference; do not assume a second attempt succeeded.

Sandbox payment, return and refund scenarios use fictional funds and cannot refund a real payment. Live payment handling depends on the activated funding facility and verified settlement records.

While workspace access remains available, use billing reports and exports to preserve your reconciliation records. Financial records required for final reconciliation or retention may be retained separately from borrower data. Support confirms the scope and retention schedule; there is no customer retention-setting or deletion-request API. See [privacy and data handling](/security/privacy).
