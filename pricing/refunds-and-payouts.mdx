title: "Cancellation and refunds"
description: "Request cancellation, delete your workspace, and receive the unused credit balance in your saved bank account."
---

Your workspace credits fund AMLOK product usage. **Used credits are non-refundable. On final account cancellation, AMLOK refunds the entire unused balance**, including paid and promotional top-ups and the unused CAD 200 processing floor. There are no on-demand withdrawals or transfers while the account is active.

## Request cancellation

Request account cancellation in the **client portal**. Enterprise clients can also request it through their dedicated account manager. Your agreement and cancellation confirmation identify the effective service end date.

You must have **at least one bank account saved in the portal** to receive the refund, even if you paid for credits by card. Confirm that the bank details are current before completing cancellation. Do not send bank credentials or full payment details by support email.

## What happens during cancellation

1. The cancellation process schedules your workspace for deletion. Export any records you need before submitting the request.
2. New billable processing and automatic top-ups stop at the effective service end date. AMLOK reconciles incurred usage, pending funding, and reservations without charging the same activity twice.
3. All data associated with the workspace is deleted as part of completed cancellation. AMLOK confirms the final unused credit balance and refund bank account before account closure.
4. **AMLOK issues the refund immediately when the account closes.** No separate refund request or customer-selected payout amount is needed.
5. Funds may take **7-10 business days** to reach the saved bank account, depending on the bank. Account closure does not wait for bank receipt.

The bank-delivery estimate starts when the refund is issued. It is not a stated deadline for completing cancellation, reconciliation, or workspace deletion.

## Refund calculation

`Unused credits = posted credit additions + credit corrections - consumed credits - refunds already issued for this cancellation`

Paid and promotional credits share one balance with no consumption priority. For example, a CAD 12,000 prepayment plus a CAD 500 promotional top-up, less CAD 1,100 of usage, leaves **CAD 11,400** to refund on final cancellation.

Pending reservations are resolved before the final calculation: completed billable usage is deducted once, and unused reservations are released. A pending payment that subsequently settles is reconciled and any unused amount returned. Consumed credits are non-refundable; a correction to an erroneous charge remains a separate ledger adjustment.

The unused balance is refunded in full. AMLOK does not deduct a withdrawal fee, refund fee, or remaining minimum-term obligation from that refund.

## Refund destination and delivery

Refunds are sent to the bank account saved for refunds in the client portal, including refunds of card-funded credits. Keep the account details current and use an account belonging to the customer named in the agreement. AMLOK verifies the destination as part of cancellation.

If a refund fails or is returned, the unpaid amount remains owed. AMLOK coordinates corrected bank instructions and reissues the unpaid amount without duplicating the refund. Returned funds do not reactivate product usage or automatic top-ups.

For delivery assistance, contact [support@amlok.tech](mailto:support@amlok.tech) or your enterprise account manager with your cancellation or refund reference. Any currency conversion follows the agreed methodology.

The public API reports credit activity and consumption. It does not expose cancellation, refund requests, payout instructions, approvals, or transfer endpoints. See [credit reporting](/api-reference/credit-reporting) and [account management](/support/account-management).
