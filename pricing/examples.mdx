These examples start with a **CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment, paid annually as CAD 12,000 at contract signing and each renewal**. They use the base rates, assume full monitoring months, and exclude discounts, taxes, and third-party fees. They illustrate wallet usage, not a quote or a guaranteed workload allowance.

Try your own volumes in the [pricing calculator](/pricing/calculator).

## Underwriting only

With Standard Insights at CAD 5 per completed underwriting:

| Calculation | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Wallet funded at signing or renewal | CAD 12,000 |
| 2,400 Standard underwritings × CAD 5 | CAD 12,000 |
| Remaining balance | CAD 0 |

The 2,400 underwritings can occur throughout the year according to your needs. There is no monthly allocation of 200 underwritings.

## Monitoring only

With Standard Signal at CAD 3 per active account per month:

| Calculation | Amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Wallet funded at signing or renewal | CAD 12,000 |
| 300 accounts × 12 months × CAD 3 | CAD 10,800 |
| Remaining balance | CAD 1,200 |

The CAD 1,200 remains available for eligible usage while the account stays active.

## A mixed portfolio

| Usage over 12 months | Calculation | Wallet deduction |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Standard Insights | 600 underwritings × CAD 5 | CAD 3,000 |
| Advanced Insights | 120 underwritings × CAD 15 | CAD 1,800 |
| Standard Signal | 200 accounts × 12 months × CAD 3 | CAD 7,200 |
| **Total** | | **CAD 12,000** |

This mix uses the CAD 12,000 prepaid from a CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment. It is an example allocation, not a bundle that limits how you can spend credits.

## A busier month

You start the month with CAD 4,000 remaining. You complete 100 Standard underwritings and monitor 200 Standard accounts for the full month.

`CAD 4,000 - (100 × CAD 5) - (200 × CAD 3) = CAD 2,900`

The CAD 1,100 deduction exceeds the CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment used to calculate the payment due at signing or renewal. It is still covered by your wallet, so no additional payment is needed for this usage.

## Buying additional credits

You have CAD 200 remaining and need 100 Standard underwritings, which will use CAD 500. You purchase an illustrative CAD 1,000 top-up before proceeding.

`CAD 200 + CAD 1,000 - (100 × CAD 5) = CAD 700`

The resulting balance is CAD 700. The CAD 1,000 top-up is an example, not a minimum top-up requirement. If you authorize automatic top-ups, the amount and trigger follow your agreement.

## Common questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is the CAD 1,000 minimum monthly commitment an additional subscription fee?">
    No. You pay the minimum monthly commitment annually: CAD 1,000 multiplied by 12, or CAD 12,000, at contract signing and each renewal. The payment becomes usable credits for eligible usage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I lose credits in a quiet month?">
    No. Credits stay in one shared wallet, with no monthly expiration. Unused credits remain available while you maintain an active account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I move usage between Insights and Signal?">
    Yes. Use the shared wallet across eligible products and workflows, even when your usage mix differs from the original contracting estimate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does every connected bank account create a Signal charge?">
    No. Signal bills the enrolled portfolio accounts defined in your order form. Their connected bank accounts are inputs to the monitoring workload.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are provider fees included?">
    No. Third-party data, verification, connectivity, and document-provider charges are separate and must be addressed in your order form.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are unused credits refunded if I cancel?">
    Refund and remaining-credit treatment must be defined in your signed agreement. See [contract terms](/pricing/contract-terms).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
