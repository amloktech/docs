Account security protects your own sign-in. Organization security sets requirements for people accessing an organization. These controls serve different scopes, so check which settings page you are using.

## Set up an authenticator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open account security">
    Open your account menu and choose **Sign-in and security**. Select **Set up MFA**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your identity">
    Enter your current password when requested. If your account uses a sign-in provider, complete a recent provider sign-in when prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add AMLOK to your authenticator">
    Scan the displayed QR code in your authenticator app, or use the manual setup key. Enter the current six-digit code and select **Verify and enable MFA**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save recovery codes">
    Download or securely store the recovery codes and confirm that you saved them. Each code can be used once if you lose access to the authenticator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

MFA setup is complete only after verification. Keep QR codes, setup keys and recovery codes private; do not include them in screenshots or support messages.

## Review sessions and password security

In account security, inspect the session list and identify your current session. Revoke an unfamiliar session or use **Sign out other sessions** when appropriate. Review the available security activity to understand recent changes.

Accounts with a password can use **Change password** after identity confirmation. Changing the password signs out other sessions. If you cannot sign in, use **Forgot your password?** from the sign-in page. Provider-based accounts should also protect access to their Microsoft, Google or GitHub account.

## Require MFA for an organization

An Owner can open **Organization security** and change the organization's MFA requirement. Members must enroll an authenticator before accessing organization data when the policy requires it; account security remains accessible for enrollment.

Owner management actions can also require MFA or a fresh identity check. If you encounter such a prompt, complete the requested setup or sign in again, then return to the action. Do not ask a colleague to share an authenticator code.

Disabling MFA is blocked when an organization you belong to requires it. Review your [team membership](/console/team) and organization requirements before changing authentication settings.

## If access is lost

Use an unused recovery code if you cannot access your authenticator. If you cannot recover access, contact [support@amlok.io](mailto:support@amlok.io) from your registered address and describe the affected organization and sign-in method. Do not send passwords, provider tokens or recovery codes.

See [account access](/console/account-access) for expired invitations and [troubleshooting](/console/troubleshooting) for workspace access issues.
