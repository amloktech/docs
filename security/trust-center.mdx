## Privacy and security contact

Contact **[privacy@amlok.io](mailto:privacy@amlok.io)** for privacy requests, security questions, vulnerability reports, procurement materials, or accessibility assistance. Describe the issue and affected service; arrange an appropriate secure channel before sending sensitive evidence.

Vulnerability-reporting details are published in the [security policy](https://app.amlok.io/security) and [security.txt](https://app.amlok.io/.well-known/security.txt).

## Accessibility approach

AMLOK's web-application accessibility policy follows an AODA-informed approach and WCAG 2.1 AA objectives. It describes semantic navigation, keyboard access, visible focus, contrast, accessible labels, and feedback channels. Review the published policy for its current scope and third-party experiences.

Our [brand guidelines and design system](/resources/brand) set WCAG 2.2 AA as the design target for new components, including reflow, meaningful labels, reduced motion, and accessible interaction states.

## Agreements and product pricing

Application policies and enterprise product agreements serve different scopes. Insights and Signal pricing follows the agreed product schedule and [agreement terms](/pricing/contract-terms), including the minimum monthly commitment paid annually at signing or renewal. Review application-specific billing terms separately when using the funding portal.
