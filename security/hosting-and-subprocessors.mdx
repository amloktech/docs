Processing locations, approved providers, and data-use restrictions are agreed for your deployment. Review the full data flow before connecting live information.

## Agree the processing boundary

Specify where information is stored, processed, backed up, and accessed. Database location alone does not describe the full flow. Include inference, telemetry, search indexes, support access, exports, and disaster recovery.

| Component | Information to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Primary hosting | Provider, region, tenant boundary, and permitted data categories. |
| Backup and recovery | Regions, replication, access restrictions, retention, and restoration testing. |
| Model processing | Provider and service, inference location, logging, retention, and training terms. |
| Operational access | Countries from which personnel and suppliers may access information. |
| Integrations | Data returned or sent, permissions, provider role, and applicable agreement. |

Record Canada-only requirements explicitly and check the whole boundary. Do not infer exclusive Canadian processing from a Canadian primary hosting region.

## Suppliers and subprocessors

The deployment's register should identify each legal entity, function, data received, locations, onward providers, retention, and safeguards. It should explain how material changes are communicated and reviewed.

Providers may process information on behalf of an institution, act under their own obligations, or supply data under separate terms. An [integration catalog](/integrations) entry is not a declaration that the provider receives data in every deployment.

For the application register, material additions are announced at least 30 days in advance through the service or account-holder email. Contact [privacy@amlok.io](mailto:privacy@amlok.io) about provider changes or processing details.

## Cross-border processing

PIPEDA does not generally prohibit outsourcing processing outside Canada. The responsible organization remains accountable and must use contractual or other means to provide a comparable level of protection. Transparency and the transfer's circumstances remain important. See the [OPC guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/airports-and-borders/gl_dab_090127/).

Quebec adds assessment and agreement requirements for processing outside Quebec, including elsewhere in Canada. Review [Quebec privacy](/security/quebec).

## Exit and portability

Agree export format, transition period, access removal, return or deletion, backup treatment, provider coordination, and completion evidence before deployment. Identify records subject to a legal hold or retention obligation.
