## Purpose and processing scope

AMLOK uses purpose-limited processing: the institution defines its underwriting or portfolio-monitoring purpose, and AMLOK processes agreed information under documented instructions. Actual activities and applicable law determine the legal allocation of responsibilities.

The processing schedule should identify people and entities involved, data categories, permitted workflows, duration, customer instructions, approved providers, and secondary-use restrictions.

## Source permissions and notices

Before collection, identify the authority to obtain information, relevant notices and consent where required, and permissions granted by each source. Access to a connected account does not automatically authorize every downstream use.

For monitoring, distinguish permission for new collections from obligations to retain earlier records. Revocation should trigger a review of future collection, processing, and retention, with legal requirements considered separately.

Authorization records include the source, permitted scope, purpose, subject or account reference, and grant, expiry, and revocation details.

## Data minimization

Limit collection to the agreed workflow. Business records may contain personal information about owners, guarantors, employees, or customers. Treat sensitive identifiers and financial details accordingly.

Avoid full borrower records, credentials, identification documents, or sensitive account information in general support tickets, logs, or demonstrations. Define an approved channel for necessary diagnostic material.

## Model use and secondary purposes

AMLOK's data-handling policy separates customer-requested inference from model training, service improvement, and other secondary purposes. Review AMLOK's use and each model provider's retention and training terms independently.

The agreement must state permitted uses, any opt-in mechanism, and restrictions on customer data and derived information. An integration's ability to transmit data does not establish permission to use it for training.

## Retention, return, and deletion

Agree a schedule by category: source data, normalized records, derived attributes, outputs, logs, and backups. Account for purpose, legal obligations, customer instructions, and documented holds.

Distinguish deletion from active systems, expiry from backups, downstream handling, and lawful exceptions. Confirm completion only after the agreed scope is addressed. Deleting a connection and deleting previously collected data are separate actions.

## Access and correction requests

Determine who receives and answers requests, how identity and scope are verified, and how AMLOK assists the responsible organization. Search supplied information and relevant derived records. Correcting a source record may require reviewing affected analysis.

Apply the legal response deadline for the jurisdiction and request. Service response targets are separate from legal deadlines.
