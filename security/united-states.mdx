For a United States Amlok deployment, determine requirements by legal entity, regulator, state, activity, and information. Identify whether each party supplies software, makes credit decisions, furnishes or uses consumer reports, or performs another regulated function. Your institution owns lending decisions and interventions; Amlok provides Insights underwriting and Signal monitoring.

## Financial information safeguards

GLBA and the applicable regulator's rules govern covered financial information. The [FTC Safeguards Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftc-safeguards-rule-what-your-business-needs-know) applies to financial institutions within the FTC's jurisdiction; other regulated institutions can have different implementing rules. Activities determine coverage, and covered institutions must also oversee service-provider safeguards.

Review the security program, risk assessment, access control, encryption, authentication, supplier oversight, testing, incident response, and applicable reporting duties. Agree the evidence and allocation of responsibilities relevant to Amlok's service. Azure hosting supports the platform's infrastructure boundary; it does not remove application, configuration, data-handling, or institutional responsibilities.

### Financial privacy and permitted disclosure

Review GLBA financial privacy requirements separately from safeguards. The Privacy Rule and applicable Regulation P obligations concern covered consumer financial information, with consumer relationships generally involving financial products or services used primarily for personal, family, or household purposes. A business loan does not automatically make every business record covered consumer information; other privacy and source-use obligations can still apply.

Identify required privacy notices, any sharing-related opt-out, applicable exceptions, and contractual restrictions before disclosing nonpublic personal information. A recipient can face limits on reuse and redisclosure even when it is not itself the institution providing the consumer's financial service. Record why Amlok receives the information and which uses and recipients are permitted. See the [FTC's financial privacy guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/how-comply-privacy-consumer-financial-information-rule-gramm-leach-bliley-act) and your institution's applicable regulator rules.

## Lending decisions and explanations

ECOA and Regulation B address relevant consumer and business credit decisions. When a statement of adverse-action reasons is required, it must identify the specific principal reasons actually used. A score or generic reference to an algorithm does not replace the required explanation. Review [Regulation B, section 1002.9](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1002/2026-07-21/9/).

Establish approved criteria, discrimination-risk review, explanations tied to the decision, and required records. Amlok evidence must be used within that agreed scope. Your institution determines the credit outcome and handles notices and any subsequent outreach in its own systems. See [AI and model governance](/security/ai-governance).

## Consumer reporting

If a workflow obtains, supplies, or uses consumer reports, assess the FCRA and each party's role as agency, furnisher, or user. Permissible-purpose controls, accuracy, disputes, and adverse-action or risk-based-pricing notices depend on the activity. See the [FTC FCRA overview](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/statutes/fair-credit-reporting-act) and [credit-decision notice guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/using-consumer-reports-credit-decisions-what-know-about-adverse-action-risk-based-pricing-notices).

Alternative data or a business-lending label does not resolve applicability. Review information about individuals, including guarantors, and the intended eligibility use. Document who supplies the report, who decides, and who manages requests or disputes.

Provider restrictions apply in addition to the FCRA analysis. Baselayer supplies business-verification information under source-specific terms that prohibit covered consumer-eligibility uses. Assessing FCRA applicability does not authorize a use prohibited by those terms. Review the [provider register](/security/hosting-and-subprocessors), [Amlok's provider-data notice](https://amlok.io/privacy#financial-and-verification-providers), and [Baselayer's permitted-use terms](https://baselayer.com/terms-of-service/) before selecting data for a decision.

## Bank model and third-party governance

The current [Federal Reserve SR 26-2 guidance](https://www.federalreserve.gov/frrs/guidance/supervisory-guidance-on-model-risk-management.htm), issued April 17, 2026, replaced SR 11-7. Its scope includes relevant statistical, quantitative, and non-generative/non-agentic AI models; it expressly excludes generative and agentic AI. Use it for applicable predictive-model review rather than as blanket assurance for every AI component.

Agree documentation, conceptual soundness, validation support, performance/limitations, monitoring, and changes for models used by the bank. Supervisory guidance is distinct from a certification or enforceable standard.

The [interagency third-party risk guidance, SR 23-4](https://www.federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/sr2304.htm), addresses banks' planning, diligence, contracts, monitoring, and termination. The [NYDFS guidance on third-party risks](https://www.dfs.ny.gov/industry-guidance/industry-letters/il20251021-guidance-managing-risks-third-party) addresses Part 500 responsibilities of covered entities. Amlok's relevant evidence can support customer reviews of service scope, access, encryption, providers, incidents, continuity, and exit arrangements. A bank's regulated status does not automatically make Amlok a DFS-covered entity.

## Sanctions and activity boundaries

Assess [OFAC requirements](https://ofac.treasury.gov/faqs/11) for relevant U.S. persons, activities, transaction nexus, and program-specific foreign-entity restrictions. Determine the appropriate screening, ownership/control review, escalation, blocking and reporting responsibilities. A screening signal is evidence for review, not a substitute for the legal determination.

Amlok's Stripe integration handles billing for Amlok services. It does not turn the product API into a lending-payment, borrower-repayment, payout, or transfer interface. If your institution undertakes money transmission, collections, or borrower communications elsewhere, evaluate its separate BSA/MSB, licensing, FDCPA/Regulation F, and communications duties for those activities. See [FinCEN's MSB scope](https://www.fincen.gov/resources/money-services-business-msb-registration) and [CFPB debt-collection definitions](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1006/2/).

## Open banking

The [CFPB personal financial data rights page](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/other-applicable-requirements/personal-financial-data-rights/) reports that the original section 1033 compliance dates were stayed by a court on October 29, 2025. Confirm current requirements and source permissions for your connection rather than relying on the original implementation dates.

Technical standards, legal access authority, provider participation, and downstream use remain separate. See [Open banking and FDX](/security/open-banking-and-fdx).

## Accessibility, communications, and sensitive data

Assess [DOJ website accessibility guidance](https://www.ada.gov/resources/web-guidance/) for the customer-facing service and relevant legal context. Review [CAN-SPAM](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business) for commercial email. These are operational obligations, not certification badges.

HIPAA applies to covered entities and business associates within its scope. Do not send protected health information, biometric templates, or verification media through an ordinary financial-data workflow without first agreeing the purpose, supported scope, legal requirements, safeguards, and retention. See [HHS business-associate guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/business-associates/index.html).

Use [standards and control alignment](/security/standards), [privacy and data handling](/security/privacy), and [due diligence](/security/due-diligence) to define the service boundary and request relevant evidence.
