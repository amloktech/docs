Use this guide to map requirements for your Canadian Amlok deployment. Identify the legal entities, regulator, lending product, information, processing locations, and activities involved. Amlok supplies Insights underwriting and Signal monitoring; your institution owns its lending decisions and performs outreach and interventions in its own systems.

## Privacy across Canada

Personal information can appear in business records, including information about owners, guarantors, employees, and customers. Determine the applicable law for the organization and activity rather than treating a business borrower as outside privacy law. PIPEDA can apply to interprovincial and international commercial flows, while substantially similar provincial laws can govern activity within a province. See the [OPC's jurisdiction guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/r_o_p/02_05_d_26/).

| Framework | Matters to address |
| --- | --- |
| [PIPEDA](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/) | Accountability, appropriate purposes, required consent, collection limits, safeguards, retention, access/correction, and breach handling. |
| [Alberta PIPA](https://www.alberta.ca/organization-responsibilities-for-protecting-personal-information) | Provincial scope, reasonable purposes, rights, protection, breach assessment, and transparency about service providers outside Canada. |
| [British Columbia PIPA](https://www.oipc.bc.ca/for-private-organizations/) | Private-organization processing, consent and exceptions, protection, access, correction, and retention. |
| [Québec private-sector privacy law, amended by Law 25](https://www.legisquebec.gouv.qc.ca/en/document/cs/P-39.1) | Governance, impact assessments, sensitive information, exclusively automated decisions, transfers, and individual rights. |

For Québec, section 12.1 addresses decisions based exclusively on automated processing. Assess notice, requested information about the data and principal factors, correction, and an opportunity to submit observations to a person able to review the decision. Also address applicable system and transfer impact assessments. See [Québec privacy](/security/quebec).

Under [PIPEDA breach guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/privacy-breaches-at-your-business/gd_pb_201810/), assess reporting and notification requirements and record breaches. The [OPC's record guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/privacy-breaches-at-your-business/bir_201920_001/) specifies a minimum 24-month record period after determining that a breach occurred. Agree escalation, assessment, reporting, and individual-notification responsibilities between the institution and Amlok.

PIPEDA does not create a universal Canada-only hosting requirement. Amlok nevertheless contractually restricts its service-provider processing of Amlok service data to Canada, including storage, backups, and support access. Review accountability and comparable protection for any independent or institution-controlled activity, together with provincial, public-sector, contractual, and customer-specific restrictions. See [OPC cross-border guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/airports-and-borders/gl_dab_090127/) and [hosting and subprocessors](/security/hosting-and-subprocessors).

## Financial-institution oversight

OSFI guidance concerns federally regulated financial institutions. Provincial lenders and credit unions can have different regulators. Agree the evidence and assistance Amlok provides for the customer's applicable obligations; outsourcing does not transfer the institution's accountability.

| Guidance | Review focus |
| --- | --- |
| [B-10: Third-party risk management](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/third-party-risk-management-guideline) | Service materiality, diligence, contracts, subcontractors, concentration, monitoring, continuity, and exit. |
| [B-13: Technology and cyber risk management](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/technology-cyber-risk-management) | Governance, information protection, technology operations, incidents, resilience, and control evidence. |
| [E-23: Model risk management](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/guideline-e-23-model-risk-management-2027) | Model inventory, intended use, validation, oversight, monitoring, change, and accountability. The revised guideline takes effect May 1, 2027. |
| [E-21: Operational risk management and resilience](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/operational-risk-management-resilience-guideline) | Critical operations, dependency mapping, disruption tolerances, recovery and scenario testing. OSFI's [transition schedule](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/news/backgrounder-guideline-e-21-operational-risk-resilience) expected full adherence by September 1, 2026. |
| [B-20: Residential mortgage underwriting](https://www.osfi-bsif.gc.ca/en/guidance/guidance-library/residential-mortgage-underwriting-practices-procedures-guideline-2017) | Relevant federally regulated institutions' mortgage underwriting/acquisition, borrower capacity and collateral assessment. It is not general business-lending guidance. |

Review the applicable version and institution scope. OSFI guidance is not a certification of Amlok. See [AI and model governance](/security/ai-governance) for validation responsibilities and the distinction between model assurance and individual lending decisions.

## Sanctions and AML/ATF

Canadian sanctions requirements depend on the applicable regulations, parties, ownership/control, activities, and prohibitions. Establish screening scope, escalation, review, and any reporting duties using [Global Affairs Canada's compliance guidance](https://www.international.gc.ca/world-monde/international_relations-relations_internationales/sanctions/compliance-program-conformite.aspx?lang=eng). A screening result or unresolved match does not itself establish that a transaction is permitted or prohibited.

The Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act and its regulations apply to covered reporting entities. Determine the sector and activity using [FINTRAC's reporting-entity guidance](https://fintrac-canafe.canada.ca/reporting-declaration/Info/re-ed-eng). Relevant duties can include identity, beneficial ownership, monitoring, records, and reporting. Amlok analysis supports the agreed evidence workflow; it is not an institution's entire AML program or a regulatory filing.

## Consumer-driven banking and FDX

FDX, acting through its Canada Council, is the designated technical standards body for Canada's consumer-driven banking framework. The Canadian profile is still being finalized, and framework participation remains distinct from use of a commercial connector. Review [Open banking and FDX](/security/open-banking-and-fdx) for the standard, current framework status, and connection responsibilities.

Permissioned access does not authorize every downstream use. Specify account/data scope, purpose, ongoing collection, expiry, revocation, and retained records. Confirm provider participation and connection coverage rather than inferring them from an integration listing.

## Activity-based requirements

| Activity or characteristic | Requirement to assess |
| --- | --- |
| Furnishing or using consumer credit reports | Provincial consumer-reporting laws can impose registration, permitted-purpose, accuracy, access/correction and related duties. Determine each party's actual role; see the [Ontario Consumer Reporting Act](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90c33). |
| Commercial email or SMS | [CASL](https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm) requires an applicable consent basis, identification, and unsubscribe handling, subject to exceptions. B2B messages are not universally exempt. |
| Ontario operations and public websites | [AODA web requirements](https://www.ontario.ca/page/how-make-websites-accessible) generally require WCAG 2.0 AA for covered public-sector organizations and businesses/nonprofits with 50+ employees. Other accessibility duties can apply below that threshold. |
| Federally regulated operations | Assess the [Accessible Canada Act](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/programs/accessible-canada/act-summary.html); bank-vendor status alone does not determine direct applicability. |
| Performing payment functions | Assess the [Retail Payment Activities Act registration criteria](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2026/06/criteria-for-registering-payment-service-providers/) where a covered function is performed as a non-incidental service. Reading financial data is distinct from initiating or transferring funds. |
| Lending products and personal information | Assess provincial lending, mortgage, human-rights, consumer-protection, language, and any specialized health/public-sector requirements. |

Amlok uses Stripe for its own billing payments. The product API does not expose payment initiation, payouts, transfers, or borrower-servicing commands. Institutions conduct their own lending, repayment, collections, and communications workflows.

## AI, fairness, and evolving requirements

Review protected-group harms, proxies, evidence quality, oversight, and correction/review pathways. The [Canadian privacy regulators' AI principles](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/technology/artificial-intelligence/gd_principles_ai/) provide guidance grounded in existing privacy duties. Relevant [human-rights protections](https://www.ohrc.on.ca/en/policy-discrimination-and-language/relevant-code-provisions) must also be considered.

[Bill C-36](https://www.parl.ca/LegisInfo/en/bill/45-1/c-36) remains proposed legislation rather than an enacted replacement for PIPEDA. The [federal automated-decision directive](https://www.canada.ca/en/government/system/digital-government/digital-government-innovations/responsible-use-ai/automated-decision-making/guide-scope-directive-automated-decision-making.html) does not automatically govern private fintech vendors. Use enacted requirements and your institution's applicable obligations when defining the deployment.
