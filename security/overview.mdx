AMLOK maintains security controls, privacy and data-handling policies, and incident-response procedures to protect borrower information and support accountable processing. The program covers Insights, Signal, their integrations, and the infrastructure and providers involved in processing data.

AMLOK is built with controls aligned to the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 security-management principles. Read our [standards and control alignment](/security/standards) for our security frameworks and financial-sector control focus.

Agree the processing scope, security requirements, permitted locations, retention schedule, and responsibilities before connecting live borrower data.

## Shared responsibilities

| Area | AMLOK responsibilities | Customer responsibilities |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data processing | Process agreed information for authorized workflows. | Establish collection authority, notices, source permissions, and permitted purposes. |
| Access | Protect service and tenant boundaries. | Approve users, protect credentials, and remove unnecessary access. |
| Underwriting and monitoring | Process information within the agreed scope and protect workflow records. | Set lending policy, approve model use, review exceptions, and own decisions and notices. |
| Privacy and incidents | Maintain data-handling, investigation, and escalation procedures. | Identify applicable obligations and coordinate requests, notifications, and retention. |

The signed agreement and applicable law determine each party's obligations. A software integration does not transfer a lender's regulatory accountability to its provider.
