Amlok's institutional platform runs on **Microsoft Azure**, with **Stripe** as the payment provider for platform billing. These guides bring together infrastructure, data handling, assurance frameworks, and the responsibilities relevant to Insights and Signal.

Agree the processing purpose, permitted sources and locations, access, retention, model use, and institutional responsibilities before connecting live borrower information. Amlok service data is contractually restricted to Canadian processing across its listed providers, with core hosting in Canada Central. The [provider register](/security/hosting-and-subprocessors) distinguishes hosting, billing, financial-data and verification services, sign-in, development, and public website services.

## Shared responsibilities

| Area | Amlok and its service providers | Your institution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infrastructure | Operate the agreed Azure services and application safeguards; review provider dependencies. | Define your required processing locations, service boundary, and assurance evidence. |
| Payments | Use Stripe for platform billing and maintain relevant Amlok billing records. | Authorize billing administrators and payment arrangements. |
| Data | Process agreed information for authorized workflows. | Establish collection authority, source permissions, notices, and permitted purposes. |
| Access | Enforce application and environment boundaries. | Approve users and identity policies, protect credentials, and remove unnecessary access. |
| Underwriting and monitoring | Produce evidence and risk signals within the agreed scope. | Validate use, set policy, own decisions and notices, and perform interventions in your systems. |
| Privacy and incidents | Support the agreed data handling, investigation, and escalation process. | Identify applicable duties and coordinate individual requests, notifications, and retention. |

Provider assurance covers the provider services named in its reports. Amlok's control mapping, certifications or reports, and your institution's obligations require their own scoped evidence. See [standards](/security/standards).
